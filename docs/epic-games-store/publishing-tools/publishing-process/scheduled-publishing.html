Scheduled Publishing

Publish changes to your product at a specific date and time.

5 mins to read

You can schedule a publish of your Epic Games Store product. When you do this, you can select a set of entities (artifacts and binaries, settings, offers, and pages), and specify a date and time. The entities you select automatically go live at the specified date and time.

For general information on publishing, see Release Management. For a complete end-to-end walkthrough of publishing, see the Epic Games Store Get Started.

Note:

  • You can only have one scheduled publish at a time for a product.
  • When you do a manual or scheduled publish, the Developer Portal always publishes the selected entities that are in your Stage sandbox at the time of the publish. To prevent unexpected changes being published in a scheduled publish, if you push any changes to your Stage sandbox when you have a scheduled publish, you can only proceed if you cancel the scheduled publish. If you still want to schedule a publish, you will need to schedule it again, and re-select any entities you want to include in the publish.
  • You can view details of which assets and other entities are included in a scheduled publish. For more information, see View Scheduled Publish Details on this page.
  • You can cancel a scheduled publish or change the scheduled date and time. For more information, see Cancel a Scheduled Publish and Change the Date of a Scheduled Publish on this page.
  • If you want to change which artifacts and binaries, settings, offers, and pages are included in the scheduled publish, you must cancel the scheduled publish and then schedule a new publish. For more information, see Cancel a Scheduled Publish and Schedule a Publish on this page.
  • When you have a scheduled publish waiting to go live, you cannot do a manual publish. However, you can cancel the scheduled publish and then do a manual publish. For more information, see Cancel a Scheduled Publish on this page, and Get Started > Launch Your Product > Process.

Schedule a Publish

To schedule a publish:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Release Management page. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal), and make sure you are in the correct organization for your product.
    • b. In the left navigation, under Your products, select the name of your product.
    • c. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store and then Release Management.
  2. Make sure that your product's artifacts and binaries, settings, offers, and pages in the Stage sandbox are in the Ready for Live status.
    • For more information on how to get your artifacts, and binaries, settings, offers, and pages ready for live, see Epic Games Store Get Started > Submit for Final Review.
    • For information on the Ready for Live status, see Release Management > Status Stage Sandbox Statuses.
  3. In the Stage sandbox, click Push to Live.
  4. Turn on the Schedule publish toggle and select the date and time you want to publish.
  5. At the bottom of the page, click Schedule publish.

View Scheduled Publish Details

On the Release Management page of the Developer Portal, you can view the details of any scheduled publish that is currently set up. This includes the date of the scheduled publish, and a list of the artifacts and binaries, settings, offers, and pages that are included in the publish.

To view scheduled publish details:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Release Management page. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal), and make sure you are in the correct organization for your product.
    • b. In the left navigation, under Your products, select the name of your product.
    • c. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store and then Release Management.
  2. If a publish is currently scheduled, a banner above the sandboxes shows the details. To view the details of this publish, click View scheduled publish.

You can cancel a scheduled publish, or change the scheduled date or time. For more information, see the following sections of this page:

  • Cancel a Scheduled Publish
  • Change the Date or Time of a Scheduled Publish

Change the Date or Time of a Scheduled Publish

You can change the date or time of a scheduled publish and leave the scheduled publish active.

Note: If you want to change which artifacts and binaries, settings, offers, and pages are included in the scheduled publish, you must cancel the scheduled publish and schedule a new publish. For more information, see Cancel a Scheduled Publish and Schedule a Publish on this page.

To change the date or time of a scheduled publish:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Release Management page. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal), and make sure you are in the correct organization for your product.
    • b. In the left navigation, under Your products, select the name of your product.
    • c. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store and then Release Management.
  2. If a publish is currently scheduled, a banner above the sandboxes shows the details. To open the scheduled publish, click View scheduled publish.
  3. At the bottom of the page, click Reschedule.
  4. In Scheduled date and Scheduled time, select the date and time you want to publish.
  5. At the bottom of the page, select Save changes.

Cancel a Scheduled Publish

You can cancel a scheduled push on the Release Management page before its specified and time.

To cancel a scheduled publish:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Release Management page. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal), and make sure you are in the correct organization for your product.
    • b. In the left navigation, under Your products, select the name of your product.
    • c. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store and then Release Management.
  2. If a publish is currently scheduled, a banner above the sandboxes shows the details. To open the scheduled publish, click View scheduled publish.
  3. At the bottom of the page, select Cancel publish.
