Achievements Setup Guide

Implement Epic Games Store achievements for your product.

25 mins to read

For products published to the Epic Games Store, you can enhance your product’s user experience with Epic Games Store achievements.

These achievements extend the functionality of Epic Online Services (EOS) achievements and you can use them to reward users with experience points (XP) for interacting with your product. As part of the Epic Games Store achievement setup, you link every Epic Games Store achievement to an Epic Online Services (EOS) achievement.

This page describes the process to implement Epic Games Store achievements for your product, including linking Epic Games Store achievements with EOS achievements.

Related documentation:

  • Background information on Epic Games Store achievements: Achievements Overview.

Epic Games Store Requirements

Products that support achievements through other PC storefronts that sell third-party products must also support Epic Games Store achievements. The Epic Games Store achievements available to users on the Epic Games Store must be substantially similar to those offered through other storefronts.

Exceptions:

  • Epic Games Store achievements are not mandatory for mobile (iOS, Android) products.

  • Web-based and HTML products are exempt from this requirement because EOS does not support them.
  • If a product in Early Access does not yet have a finalized set of base game achievements defined, you only need to implement EOS achievements at the time of early release (documentation: EOS > Achievements Interface). When your product is ready for full release, you must enable Epic Games Store achievements.
  • This requirement does not retroactively apply to any existing products available on the Epic Games Store or any products that onboarded in the Developer Portal prior to March 9, 2023. However, we recommend that you add Epic Games Store achievements to your product for an optimal user experience.

For information on the distribution requirements, see the documentation: Store Requirements Overview.

Before You Start

You can set up Epic Games Store achievements before you have set up EOS achievements in your product's code. However, you cannot publish an Epic Games Store achievement to make it live to users if it is not linked to a corresponding EOS achievement. You set up EOS achievements by integrating EOS with your product code. See the documentation listed below for guidance on integrating EOS and EOS achievements with your product.

EOS documentation:

  • Get started: Guide: Get Started with Epic Online Services.
  • Add EOS achievements to your product: see EOS > Guide: How to Add Achievements to Your Game.
  • Add EOS stats to your product: EOS > Guide: How to Add Stats to Your Game.
    (You might choose to base each EOS achievement on a stat. For example, to create an Epic Games Store achievement that rewards users for traveling a certain distance in a game, first create a stat using the EOS Stats Interface to track user travel distance.)

A: Set up Epic Games Store Achievements in the Developer Portal

Follow the steps below to prepare your product for Epic Games Store achievements.

Step 1: Accept the Epic Online Services Developer Agreement

The owner of your organization must read and sign the updated Developer Agreement. Only the organization owner can complete this step. You cannot use Epic Games Store achievements until the owner has accepted this agreement. (Note that you only do this step once for your organization.)

Accept the agreement on the Achievements page in the Developer Portal. To do this, follow the steps below:

  1. Go to your product's Developer Portal page:
    • a. Sign into the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product. (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)
  2. In the left navigation, click Epic Online Services.
  3. In the left navigation, in the Player & Game Data section, click Achievements.
  4. Select the Epic Games Store tab.
  5. In the Developer Agreement section, click See Agreement.
  6. Review the Epic Online Services Developer Agreement and click Accept.

After the owner has signed the agreement, continue to step 2. below. You do not have to be the organization owner to complete step 2.

Step 2: Generate Sandboxes and Deployments, Select Deployments.

In this step, you generate a Dev sandbox and a Stage sandbox and generate a deployment in each sandbox and in the Live sandbox. These are the deployments you will use for your Epic Games Store Achievements.

After you have generated these, you select a deployment in each sandbox as the primary deployment for use with Epic Games Store achievements. Epic Online Services (EOS) associates all user data for a product, including Epic Games Store achievement data and EOS achievement data, with a specific deployment.

You generate and select your sandboxes and deployments in the Developer Portal, on the Epic Games Store tab of the Game Services' Achievements page. To do this, follow the steps below:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Epic Games Store tab of the Game Services' Achievements page:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product. (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)
    • c. In the Developer Portal's left navigation, click Game Services.
    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Online Services.
    • e. In the left navigation, in the Player & Game Data section, click Achievements.
    • f. Select the Epic Games Store tab.
  2. In the Generate Sandboxes and Deployments section, click Generate to create new Dev and Stage sandboxes and associated deployments.
    • Note: If Dev and Stage sandboxes containing one or more deployments already exist for your product, skip this step and go to the next one.
  3. In the Please Select a Primary Deployment per Sandbox section, choose a primary deployment for each sandbox to contain your Epic Games Store achievements.
    • Tip: For the Dev sandbox and Stage sandbox, select the deployments you are using for pre-production QA. For the Live sandbox, select the deployment associated with your live product.
  4. Click Submit.

You are now ready to create your Epic Games Store achievements.

B. Create Epic Games Store Achievements for Your Product

From the Developer Portal, you must retrieve a starting file to define your Epic Games Store achievements in, define them in it, and then upload it to the Developer Portal.

There are two methods to create this. These are as follows:

  • Single definition:
    Define Epic Games Store achievements one at a time in the Developer Portal.
  • Bulk import:
    Define multiple Epic Games Store achievements in one action using bulk import to the Developer Portal./ We recommend this method if you have already added Epic Online Services (EOS) achievements to your product.

Single Definition

When you create an Epic Games Store achievement, you link it to an EOS achievement and assign it an XP value. If you do not have a corresponding EOS achievement available, you can create one in the Developer Portal.

Note: This section describes how to create a single Epic Games Store achievement and link it to a single EOS achievement. If you already have EOS achievements in your product or wish to create multiple achievements, we recommend you follow the bulk import process (see section of this page Bulk Import).

Step 1: Add a Corresponding EOS Achievement

Before you can create an Epic Games Store achievement, you must have a corresponding EOS achievement to link it with.
There are two ways to define EOS achievements:

  • EOS Achievements Interface
    In your product's code, add an EOS Game Services' Achievements Interface integration.
    For guidance, see documentation: EOS > Achievements Interface.
  • EOS Stats Interface
    You might choose to base each EOS achievement on a stat. For example, to create an Epic Games Store achievement that rewards users for traveling a certain distance in a game, first create a stat using the EOS Stats Interface to track user travel distance. Then, link the Epic Games Store achievement with that stat.
    For guidance, see documentation: EOS > Stats Interface

To prepare an EOS achievement for linking to an Epic Games Store achievement, follow the steps below:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Achievements page. To do this:
    • a. Sign into the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product. (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)
    • c. In the left navigation, click Epic Online Services.
    • d. In the left navigation, in the Player & Game Data section, click Achievements.
  2. Select the Epic Online Services tab.
  3. In the Deployment drop-down, select the deployment associated with your Dev sandbox.
  4. From the options in the top right, click Create New.
  5. To base this achievement on a stat, select the desired option from the drop-down. Then, enter the stat value that unlocks the achievement.
  6. Click Next.
  7. Complete the Create New Achievement form as appropriate:
    • Achievement ID: A unique value that identifies the achievement.
    • Select Locale: The locale for the provided achievement name and description. Unique sets of Epic Games Store achievements can be created for each language the Epic Games Store supports, as needed.
    • Visibility: Determines whether the achievement is displayed to product users before they unlock it.
    • Unlocked Icon: The image the Social Overlay or your product displays to users after they unlock the achievement.
    • Locked Icon: The image displayed to users before they unlock the achievement. Note that this icon is not used by Epic Games Store achievements; instead, Epic Games Store users see a semi-transparent version of the unlocked icon with a superimposed lock.
    • Locked Display Name: The name displayed to users before they unlock the achievement.
    • Locked Description: The description displayed to users before they unlock the achievement.
    • Unlocked Display Name: The name displayed to users after they unlock the achievement.
    • Unlocked Description: The description displayed to users after they unlock the achievement.
    • Flavor Text Optional: Optional text that you can freely use in your product.
  8. After completing the form, click Create to save your new achievement.

Step 2: Create and Link an Epic Games Store Achievement

Once you have an EOS achievement prepared in the Developer Portal, you can create your Epic Games Store achievement and link the two achievements together.

To link the achievements in the Developer Portal, follow the steps below:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Achievements page. To do this:
    • a. Sign into the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product. (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)
    • c. In the left navigation, click Epic Online Services.
    • d. In the left navigation, in the Player & Game Data section, click Achievements.
  2. Select the Epic Online Services tab.
  3. In the Deployment drop-down, select the deployment associated with your Dev sandbox.
  4. In the list of achievements, locate the EOS achievement you wish to link to your new Epic Games Store achievement.
  5. Click the three dots ( … ) to the right of the achievement’s row, then select Link Achievements.
  6. Enter an XP value for the achievement.
    • Tip: For guidelines, review XP (Experience Points) requirements for achievements.
  7. Select Create Link to finalize and save your new Epic Games Store achievement.

Bulk Import

To add multiple Epic Games Store achievements to your product in one action, use the bulk import process.

Notes:

  • You must complete the bulk import process in the deployment linked to Epic Games Store achievements in your Dev sandbox.
  • The bulk import process only allows you to upload either EOS achievements or Epic Games Store achievements in one upload. You cannot upload both EOS achievements and Epic Games Store achievements together in one import.

Step 1: Retrieve a Starting File

To begin this process, you must retrieve a starting file to define your Epic Games Store achievements in. Select one of the following options to retrieve a starting file:

  • Option 1: Export your current EOS achievements as a zip file.
    You do this from your Live deployment in the Developer Portal. We recommend this option if you already have EOS achievements defined for your product.
  • Option 2: Download the Epic Games Store achievements template.
    You add to this template file to create new achievements.

After you have retrieved a starting file, you define and upload your achievements.

Option 1: Export Your Current EOS Achievements as a Zip File
  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Achievements page. To do this:
    • a. Sign into the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product. (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)
    • c. In the left navigation, click Epic Online Services.
    • d. In the left navigation, in the Player & Game Data section, click Achievements.
  2. Select the Epic Online Services tab.
  3. In the Deployment drop-down, select your Live sandbox.
  4. To the top right, click Bulk Import / Export.
  5. On the Bulk Export form, select Export Current EOS Achievements to download your file. Then, close the form.

When you have your zip file, go to the next step.

Option 2: Download the Epic Games Store Achievements Template in a Zip File
  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Achievements page. To do this:
    • a. Sign into the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product. (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)
    • c. In the left navigation, click Epic Online Services.
    • d. In the left navigation, in the Player & Game Data section, click Achievements.
  2. Select the Epic Online Services tab.
  3. In the Deployment drop-down, select your Dev sandbox.
  4. To the top right, click Bulk Import / Export to open the form. Then, click Next.
  5. Select Download Epic Games Store Achievements Zip Template to download your file. Then, close the form.
    • Note: If the Epic Games Store Achievements option is not available, ensure you have fully completed the setup process.

When you have your zip file, go to the next step.

Step 2: Define and Upload Your Achievements

Define your Epic Games Store achievements in the downloaded file and upload it to the Developer Portal to create your Epic Games Store achievements.

To define your Epic Games Store achievements, follow the process below:

  1. On your computer, extract the zip file you downloaded.
  2. Open the file labeled achievementDefinitions.csv.
  3. In the CSV file, ensure the last column is labeled user_epic_achievements_xp. If this column is not available, add it to the file.
  4. Populate the CSV file with the appropriate values for your achievements.
    • Ensure that you include any live EOS achievements in the file. If you used Option 1: Export Your Current EOS Achievements as a Zip File these EOS achievements are already included.
    • Learn more about usage limitations for achievements.
  5. In the user_epic_achievements_xp column, add the desired experience point (XP) value for each achievement.
    • Note: When defining XP values, ensure you follow the appropriate XP requirements. See the section of this page: XP Requirements for Epic Games Store Achievements.

An example achievement definition in the CSV file.

  1. (Optional) Add localized text and associate images with your achievements by editing the achievementLocalizations.csv file.
    • For guidance on achievementLocalizations.csv, see documentation: EOS > Tool: Bulk Import / Export.
  2. Compile your files, including your modified achievementDefinitions.csv file, into a new zip archive. Place all the files at the root level of the zip archive.

To upload your Epic Games Store achievements, follow the process below:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Achievements page. To do this:
    • a. Sign into the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product. (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)
    • c. In the left navigation, click Epic Online Services.
    • d. In the left navigation, in the Player & Game Data section, click Achievements.
  2. Select the Epic Games Store tab.
  3. In the Deployment drop-down, select your Dev sandbox.
  4. To the top right, click Bulk Import / Export to open the form. Then, click Next to advance to the Bulk Import page.
  5. Locate the Upload Achievement section at the bottom of the page. Then, review and accept the warning message.
  6. Click Choose File to Upload and select your zip archive. Then, click Next to upload the file.

Step 3: Review Your Achievements Upload

After you import your achievements, Epic Games checks whether they satisfy the criteria specified in the XP requirements for achievements. See the section on this page: XP Requirements for Epic Games Store Achievements.

Review your achievements upload in the Developer Portal. To do this follow the steps below:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Achievements page. To do this:
    • a. Sign into the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product. (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)
    • c. In the left navigation, click Epic Online Services.
    • d. In the left navigation, in the Player & Game Data section, click Achievements.
  2. Select the Epic Games Store tab.
  3. Locate Push to Stage at the top of the page.
    • If Push to Stage is enabled, your achievements passed validation and satisfy the XP requirements.
    • If Push to Stage is disabled, your achievements do not meet the XP requirements.
      Expand the message to review the details of the failed validation.
      Tip: Common errors include missing or incorrect XP values, an XP total that does not equal 1000, incorrect icon file sizes, or exceeded character limits.
  4. If one or more errors are present in your achievements, resolve those issues in your CSV file. Then, update your zip archive and reupload the archive following the bulk import process.

Tips:

  • You can validate your Epic Games Store achievements in your product before you push them to Stage. To do this, create a build of your product that, during EOS SDK initialization, uses the Dev sandbox and deployment associated with your achievements. (For guidance on how to get started with the EOS SDK and SDK initialization, see documentation: EOS > Get Started Guide.)
  • If you push your achievements to Live, you can preview them on the Product Detail Page and Achievement Details Page. To do so, first push your achievements to Stage for review and approval. (See the section of this page: E:Epic Games Review, Approval, and Publish.)

C: Additional Setup Options

You have the option to edit an Epic Games Store achievement’s XP value before you push it to Live. You can also delete an Epic Games Store achievement before you push it to Live.

Edit an Epic Games Store Achievement’s XP Value

Before an Epic Games Store achievement is pushed to Live, you can edit its XP value. Once the achievement is live, this value cannot be modified.

To edit the XP awarded by an Epic Games Store achievement, follow the steps below:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Achievements page. To do this:
    • a. Sign into the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product. (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)
    • c. In the left navigation, click Epic Online Services.
    • d. In the left navigation, in the Player & Game Data section, click Achievements.
  2. Select the Epic Online Services tab.
  3. In the drop-down above your Epic Online Services achievements, select the deployment associated with your Dev sandbox.
  4. Click Epic Games Store achievements. Then, locate the achievement you wish to update.
  5. To the right of the achievement’s row, click the three dots ( … ) and choose Edit XP.
  6. Enter the desired XP value for the achievement.
  7. Click Save to finalize your changes.

Delete an Epic Games Store Achievement

To delete an Epic Games Store achievement, you unlink the achievement from the associated EOS achievement. This action removes the Epic Games Store achievement from your product. It does not delete the EOS achievement.

After an Epic Games Store achievement has been pushed to Live, you cannot unlink the achievement in the Dev Portal. To delete an Epic Games Store achievement in this situation, contact Support. To do this, create a private discussion using this support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com.

To unlink and delete an Epic Games Store achievement in the Developer Portal, follow the steps below:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Achievements page. To do this:
    • a. Sign into the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product. (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)
    • c. In the left navigation, click Epic Online Services.
    • d. In the left navigation, in the Player & Game Data section, click Achievements.
  2. Select the Epic Online Services tab.
  3. In the drop-down above your Epic Online Services achievements, select the deployment associated with your Dev sandbox.
  4. Click Epic Games Store achievements. Then, locate the achievement you wish to remove.
  5. To the right of the achievement’s row, click the three dots ( … ) and choose Unlink.
  6. Select Remove Link to unlink and delete your Epic Games Store achievement.

D: Preview Your Live Epic Games Store Achievements

In the Epic Games Store, information about your achievements displays on both your Product Details Page and on the dedicated Achievements page available for your product. You can preview this information before you push your Epic Games Store achievements to Live.

To preview this information, first ensure the following:

  • You have pushed your product to the Live sandbox.
  • Your product has an audience item (also known as a "catalog item").
  • Your product has an offer. (Documentation: Manage Offers.)

Then, use the following URL to preview your product’s Epic Games Store achievements before pushing them live. Replace <product-name> with the name of your product: /store/en-US/achievements/<product-name>

An example achievement details page.

E: Epic Games Review, Approval, and Publish

To publish your Epic Games Store achievements so they are live and available to users: after you have created Epic Games Store Achievements in your Dev sandbox, you take the following steps:

  • Step 1: Push to Stage.
    Push your Epic Games Store achievements to the Stage sandbox and submit them for review by the Epic Games Store team.
  • Step 2: Wait for Epic Games approval.
  • Step 3: Push to Live.
    Once approved, push your achievements to the Live sandbox.

Notes:

  • When you push Epic Games Store achievements from one sandbox to another, they replace any achievements in the deployment linked to the destination sandbox.
  • After you have pushed an Epic Games Store achievement to the live sandbox, it is published and you cannot adjust its XP value or delete it through the Developer Portal.
    To delete a published (live) Epic Games Store achievement, contact Support. To do this, create a private discussion using this support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com.
  • To update any text or images associated with your Epic Games Store achievements, follow the publishing flow as described in this section.

Tip:

  • Because you push to the Dev, Stage, and Live sandboxes sequentially during the publishing process, your product’s build has different sandbox IDs and different deployment IDs at different steps in the process. To ensure that your product’s build is using the correct IDs for each step in the process, pass the IDs as command-line arguments to the Epic Games Launcher.
    For guidance see: Manage Artifacts > Step 5: Select Deployment ID (for Epic Online Services Only).

Step 1: Push to Stage

Push your achievements to Stage in the Developer Portal. To do this, follow the steps below:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Achievements page. To do this:
    • a. Sign into the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. In the left navigation, under Your Products, click the name of your product. (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)
    • c. In the left navigation, click Epic Online Services.
    • d. In the left navigation, in the Player & Game Data section, click Achievements.
  2. Select the Epic Games Store tab.
  3. From the options at the top right, click Push to Stage.

If the Push to Stage button is grayed-out so you cannot click it, your achievements contain one or more errors. An alert box on the page lists the errors. You must resolve the errors before you proceed.

Step 2: Wait for Epic Games Review

After you push your Epic Games Store achievements to Stage, the Epic Games Store team reviews your achievements for any inappropriate content such as mature content, profanity, and asset flips. You do not have to take any additional action to submit your product for review: the Developer Portal submits it automatically when you push to Stage.

Note that Epic Games team does not moderate non-Epic-Games-Store achievements (the team does not review EOS achievements, for example): The team only moderates Epic Games Store achievements. Epic Games Store achievements must abide by the Epic Games Store content guidelines (documentation: Content Guidelines).

If the Epic Games Store team approves your Epic Games Store achievements, a message displays at the top of the Developer Portal Game Services Achievements page, along with a button to push your Epic Games Store achievements live.

If the Epic Games Store team rejects any of your Epic Games Store achievements during the review process, a rejection message displays in the Stage sandbox, as shown in the example below:

To view more information about the rejection, click SEE DETAILS.

To fix the identified issues, switch to your Dev sandbox and complete any necessary updates. Then, resubmit your achievements to Stage for approval.

Note:

  • After pushing updates to Stage, you can still make changes at any time before product approval occurs. To do so, cancel the push to Stage and complete the desired updates in your Dev sandbox. When ready, push your updated product to Stage again.
  • If you make any changes to an achievement set, you must resubmit the full set of achievements to Stage for review.

Step 3: Push to Live

Once your Epic Games Store achievements have been reviewed and approved, you can push them live to make them available to users.

To push them live in the Developer Portal, follow the steps below:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Achievements page. To do this:
    • a. Sign into the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. In the left navigation, under Your Products, click the name of your product. (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)
    • c. In the left navigation, click Epic Online Services.
    • d. In the left navigation, in the Player & Game Data section, click Achievements.
  2. Select the Epic Games Store tab.
  3. In the Deployment drop-down, select the Stage sandbox.
  4. From the options at the top right, click Push to Live.
  5. In the list of entities (artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, pages, and web shop items), select the entities that you want to publish.
    • At a minimum, you must push the following entities, if they are not already in your Live sandbox:
      • A base product offer
      • A set of store settings
      • A product home page
      • An artifact for the base product offer (you do not need to do this for a "coming soon" offer).
      • Any other entities that are mandatory for your product (for example, an FAQ page if your product is a PC (Windows, macOS) product in Early Access).
        • For information on mandatory entities, see documentation: Epic Games Store Requirements.
  6. At the top right, click Push to Live.

Note: After an Epic Games Store achievement is live, you cannot adjust its XP value or remove the achievement through the Dev Portal.

  • To delete a published achievement, contact Support. To do this: create a private discussion using this support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com.
  • To update any text or images associated with your achievements, follow the publishing flow.

Additional Information

XP Requirements for Epic Games Store Achievements

When setting XP (experience point) values for Epic Games Store achievements, you must adhere to the following guidelines:

  • Each product must have exactly 1,000 XP in total, regardless of how many achievements are defined.
    The total XP currently used for a product displays in the Developer Portal on the Achievements page:
    • An error message displays if you try to add more or less than 1,000 XP for a specific product.
    • Achievements cannot be pushed from Dev to Stage if the product’s total XP does not exactly equal 1,000 (i.e., the total XP cannot be more or less than 1,000).
    • To go to the Developer Portal's Achievements page:
      • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
      • b. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product. (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)
      • c. When you are on your product's Developer Portal page, in the left navigation, click Game Services > Progression > Achievements.
  • The XP value for individual achievements must be a multiple of 5 (for example, 5, 10, 15, 100, 110, 200, ...).
  • The minimum XP for a single achievement is 5, and the maximum is 200.
  • Epic Games Store achievements are classified in bronze, silver, and gold tiers, as follows:
    • Bronze: 5-45 XP
    • Silver: 50-95 XP
    • Gold: 100-200 XP
    • An additional platinum tier achievement is available for users who earn a total of 1,000 XP from a single product.

Request Additional XP for Epic Games Store Achievements

You can request to increase the total amount of XP of your product. One reason to do this for example is when your product has more than 1000 XP on another storefront.

The following conditions list how many XP you can request for your product:

  • You can add up to a maximum of 2000 XP for every year your product has been on the Epic Games Store. The year start counts from when you launch your game on the Epic Games Store.
  • There is a maximum of 5000 XP for each product.

You request additional XP in the Developer Portal. To do this:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Achievements page. To do this:

    • a. Sign into the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product. (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)
    • c. In the left navigation, click Epic Online Services.
    • d. In the left navigation, in the Player & Game Data section, click Achievements.

  2. Select the Epic Games Store tab.

  3. Click the three dots ( … ) icon and select Additional XP Request.

  4. Follow the instructions to send the additional XP request with the following information:

    • Title: Additional XP Request
    • Your Product ID. To find your Product ID:
      • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
      • b. On the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of the product.
      • c. On the left navigation, click Product Settings.
      • d. The Product Settings page defaults to the SDK Download and Credentials tab. Scroll down this tab to the PRODUCT section. Look for Product ID.
    • The new total XP you want to request. For example if you have 1000XP and would like an additional 500XP, you would request a new total of 1500XP.
    • A link to the product’s details page on another storefront showing the XP-equivalent value you're requesting, or a written justification as to why you're requesting additional XP for this product.

For more information, contact Epic Games Support. To do this, create a private discussion on the support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com.
