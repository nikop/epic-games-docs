Request Approval to Use the Unreal Engine Logo

Use Unreal Engine trademarks in your product's branding.

2 mins to read

This page applies if your product (game or other software application) uses Unreal Engine.

If you want to use Unreal Engine trademarks in your product’s branding, such as the Unreal Engine logo, the Epic Games team must first approve your assets.

To use Unreal Engine trademarks, follow the steps below to:

  • Accept the Unreal Engine Trademark License Agreement.
  • Submit all assets (images and MP4-format videos) that use Unreal Engine trademarks for review and written approval by the Epic Games team.

Note: Allow up to 30 days for Epic Games to review your request.

Accept the Unreal Engine Trademark License Agreement

Before you can submit an asset for approval, you must first accept the Unreal Engine Trademark License Agreement.

Note: If you do not have a role in your organization that is Admin or Owner, request that an Admin or Owner accept the Unreal Engine Trademark License Agreement. (Documentation: Developer Portal > Organization Page Tabs > Roles.)

To accept the agreement:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. In the left navigation, in the Epic tools section, click Unreal Engine > Trademark License.
  3. Under the Unreal Engine Trademark License Agreement description, click Review and accept the agreement.
  4. Read the agreement and click Accept. (Note that you must scroll to the bottom of the agreement before you can click Accept.)

Results: The Trademark License page displays a green “Accepted” status after you have accepted the agreement.

Request Approval for Unreal Engine Logo Use

After an Owner or Admin has accepted the Trademark License Agreement, any role in your organization can submit assets using the Unreal Engine logo for approval.

Notes:

  • If your organization does not have a product already created in the Developer Portal, the Developer Portal prompts you to create one.
  • For asset guidelines, see brand.epicgames.com/~ Unreal Engine Marketing and Brand Hub.
  • Your request is evaluated for compliance against the brand guidelines on a case-by-case basis.

To submit your assets:

  1. Go to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. In the left navigation, in the Epic tools section, click Unreal Engine > Trademark License.
  3. Under Examples of use, click Submit example and follow the onscreen instructions.
  4. In the Submit logo use example form, complete the following:
    • a. Select Product name. (Note that if your organization does not have a product already created in the Developer Portal, the Developer Portal prompts you to create one.)
    • b. Reason for this request.
    • c. Upload your assets.
  5. Click Submit.

Results: You can check your asset’s status on the Trademark License page under Logo uses. The status changes from In review to Accepted or Rejected. If your asset is rejected, the Epic Games team gives guidance on how to fix your asset. When you are ready to resubmit, follow the steps in Request Approval for Unreal Engine Logo Use above.
