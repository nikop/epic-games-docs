2. Publish Your Product Home Page (Early Awareness)

Epic Games Store - Get Started Steps - 2. Publish Your Product Home Page (Early Awareness).

8 mins to read

Mobile Early Adopter: Do not use this page. Go to the Mobile Early Adopter—2. Publish Your Product Home Page (Early Awareness) page for guidance.

Previous step: 1. Onboarding

Note: If you want to use web shops, you must publish your product home page even if you do not want to distribute your product on the Epic Games Store. If you do not want to publish your product home page right now, you can go right to step 3. Finalize and Test Configurations.

When you have completed onboarding, you can choose to publish your product home page to the storefront for early awareness. If you do not wish to create early awareness at this stage, you can skip this step and proceed to Step 3. Finalize and Test Configurations.

  • If you have not yet launched your product on other storefronts, we recommend that you complete this step to spread early awareness of your product.
    We recommend that you publish your product home page when you announce your product. This ensures users know it will be available on the Epic Games Store. It also allows users to wishlist your product and receive notifications (if they have opted-in).
  • If you have already launched your product on other storefronts, you might choose to skip this step and proceed to Step 3. Finalize and Test Configurations.

To publish your product home page for early discovery, you must provide preliminary configurations for elements of your product's Epic Games Store presence. These elements, called "entities" in the Developer Portal, are as follows:

  • Store Settings (Product Configuration, Regions and Ratings)
  • Base Product Offer and Offer Dates
  • Home Page
  • Optional: Localization

Note: You do not need to fully configure these settings, offers, dates, pages, and localizations in this step. In Step 3. Finalize and Test Configurations, you can provide additional information and upload your product binary files (executable code file and any supporting files) to prepare for launch.

Estimated time to complete: 1 week

Prerequisites:

  • Complete Step 1. Onboarding.
  • For guidance on the sandbox environments and which settings, offers, dates, pages, and localizations you can configure, see documentation: Release Management.

Before you begin: To complete this step, prepare the following:

  • Metadata (for example, genre and features), distribution regions, and pricing information for your product.
  • Storefront content for your product home page: text and media (for example, images, videos).
  • Localized storefront content. (Epic Games offers a free localization service for some text, details are below.)
    • Recommended: Localized text
    • Optional: Localized media

To do this:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal), and make sure you are in the correct organization for your product.
    (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation in the Developer Portal. To change organization, click Organization and follow the onscreen instructions.)
  2. On the left navigation, under Your products, select the name of your product.
  3. Follow the process steps below.

A. Store Settings: Product Configuration, Regions and Ratings

Documentation:

  • Configure Store Settings

To configure store settings, go to the Store Settings page. To do this:

  1. On the left navigation, click Store Settings.
  2. On the Store Settings page, locate the Product configuration tile and click Edit.
  3. Follow the onscreen instructions to add essential details about your product.
  4. Close the Product Configuration page. On the Store Settings page, locate the Regions and ratings tile and click Edit.
  5. Follow the onscreen instructions to obtain an IARC rating (or upload traditional ratings) and select the regions in which you plan to distribute your product.
    Epic Games Store does not require an IARC rating for your product, but we recommend it has one because it provides the best reach for potential users.

B. Base Product Offer

Documentation:

  • Manage Offers

Your base product offer is the product you plan to sell without any add-ons. Add-ons could be editions, season passes, chapters, or whatever you might add to your product. To configure your base product offer, go to the Offers page. To do this:

  1. On the left navigation, click Offers.
    The Offers page defaults to the Sandbox: Dev tab. Your product is listed in the Dev tab with an auto-generated base product offer. Your product is assigned the default Type, Game.
  2. Click on your product's name.
  3. Follow the onscreen instructions to add details to the Required Information section of your base product offer. For guidance on how to add dates, see Step C. Offer Dates below.

A checkmark next to a section tile indicates that you have added details.

C. Offer Dates

Documentation:

  • Manage Offers: Dates

Continuing from B. Base Product Offer, above, in the Offers page of the Developer Portal.

Select the Dates tile and complete the Offer Dates Quick Start questions, using the guidance below.
Note: If you complete the Offer Dates Quick Start questions, and later come back to the Offer Dates panel, you can follow the Offer Dates Quick Start questions again by clicking on Guided Experience.

  • Question: Was this offer previously released on PC or Mac?
    Follow the onscreen instructions. (Note “PC” means Windows.)
  • Question: When do you want this offer to be discovered?
    Determines when your product home page is visible to users on the storefront. This date does not determine when it becomes available for purchase.
    You can set a specific availability date or choose to have your product home page published as soon as your base product offer is pushed to the Live sandbox.
  • Question: Do you want to enable pre-purchase for this offer?
    Documentation for PC (Windows, macOS): Pre-Purchase Guidelines
  • Question: When is this offer officially launching, or anticipating to launch, on the Epic Games Store?
    Determines when your product is officially launched on the storefront.
    If you do not have a product build ready, set the launch date type to Estimated or Launch date unknown.
    • Guide to launch date types:
      • Specific: You must add a release-ready binary to the artifact for this offer.
      • Estimated: You can provide a generalized date to display instead (For example, May 2030).
      • Launch date unknown: The launch date on your product home page displays as "Coming Soon".
      • Estimated or Launch date unknown: You must return during 3. Finalize and Test Configurations and provide a specific launch date to prepare for release.
  • Question: Do you want this offer to expire? Use this to set a time limit on the offer.

D. Product Home Page

Documentation:

  • Manage Pages

To configure your product home page, go to the Pages page. To do this:

  1. On the left navigation, click Pages.
    The Pages page defaults to the Sandbox: Dev tab. Your product home page is listed in the Dev tab.
  2. Click on the page name, Product home.
  3. Follow the onscreen instructions to upload media assets for your product’s store presence.
    • Required:
      • Carousel: Upload videos and images to the featured media section that displays front and center on your product home page.
      • About: Provide additional text that further describes your product or highlights certain features.
    • Optional:
      • Additional Images: Add images to a collapsible media grid on your product home page.
      • Social Preview: Specify images to use for posts on social media about your product.

E. Optional: Localize

Documentation:

  • Localize Store Presence
  • Localize Store Presence—Request Text Translations from the Epic Translation Service

To configure your product's localization, go to the Localization page. To do this:

  1. On the left navigation, click Localization.
    The Localization page defaults to the Sandbox: Dev tab.
  2. Click the store settings, page, or offer that you want to localize.
  3. In the Manage Localization pages, follow the onscreen instructions to upload localized text and media.
  • Notes:
    • Epic Games does not require localization for all products. However, we recommend it to best reach global users. The Epic Games Store currently supports 32 different locales.
    • If you are unable to provide your own translations, the Epic Games Store offers a translation service to localize your product home page text free of charge.
      (See documentation: Localize Store Presence—Request Text Translations from the Epic Translation Service.)

F. Submit for Review

After you have configured these settings, you can submit your product for review by the Epic Games Store team.

To submit your product for review, go to the Release Management page. To do this:

  1. On the left navigation, click Release Management.
  2. On the Release Management page, in the Dev sandbox tab, click Push To Stage.
  3. On the Push to Stage page, check the settings, offers, dates, pages, and localizations you have just configured (Product data: Product configuration, Product home, Regions and ratings and Offer and Page data: The name of your product). The Epic Games Store requires this to publish your product home page.
    Note: Do not check any settings, offers, dates, pages, or localizations in Artifact data.
    (If you have any additional offers or pages that are not ready to be published, leave those items unchecked.)
  4. Click Push to Stage. This action may take a few minutes to complete.
  5. After the Push to Stage is complete, click Submit for Review in the Stage sandbox.
    The Epic Games Store team now reviews your submission to ensure it meets content guidelines and other requirements. The review might take 2 or more days to complete.

The publishing service level agreement (SLA) sets out the response times for Epic Games responses to your requests, including product review times. Documentation: Publishing Service Level Agreement.

G. Publish

Note: When you publish your product home page, it becomes indexable on the public web, even if your viewable date is set to a future time. Make sure you are ready for your product to be public before you complete this step.

After the Epic Games Store team has approved your submission, you can publish your product from the Release Management page. To do this:

  1. In the left navigation, click Release Management.
  2. In the Stage panel, click Push To Live.
    • The availability of your product home page on the storefront depends on how you configured your viewable date in C. Offer Dates on this page.
  3. In the list of entities (artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, pages, and web shop items), select the entities that you want to push to Live.
    • At a minimum, you must push the following entities, if they are not already in your Live sandbox:
      • A base product offer
      • A set of store settings
      • A product home page
      • An artifact for the base product offer (you do not need to do this for a "coming soon" offer).
      • Any other entities that are mandatory for your product (for example, an FAQ page if your product is a PC (Windows, macOS) product in Early Access).
        • For information on mandatory entities, see documentation: Epic Games Store Requirements.
