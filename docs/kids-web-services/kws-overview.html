Kids Web Services (KWS) Overview

KWS can help you create safe and compliant digital experiences.

1 min to read

Kids Web Services (KWS) is an age verification and consent management platform that consists of four modular services. You can use them individually or combine as many as you need. To learn more, visit the KWS website at kidswebservices.com.

The services are as follows:

  • Parent Verification (PV) Service — Verifies that the person designated as a parent or guardian is an adult.
  • Consent Management (CM) Service — Provides a complete end-to-end parental consent management flow.
  • Age Gate Service — Based on age and location, it determines whether a user is above or below the age of digital consent (AODC). The AODC is the age below which a parent or guardian is required to give permission to allow certain processing of their child’s personal information.
  • Age Verification (AV) Service — Verifies that a user is an adult.

Disclaimer: Always consult your legal counsel for guidance relating to your specific use case. Kids Web Services does not offer legal advice.

You can access KWS using a REST API and incorporate the services into your products (game or other software application) across all major platforms including game consoles, PC (Windows, macOS, Linux, Chrome OS), and mobile (iOS, Android). You do not need an SDK to implement KWS.

You must not use KWS to determine if a user can access content related (but not limited) to the following:

  • Financial services (for example, banking, money transfer, cryptocurrency)
  • Pornography
  • Gambling
  • Drugs and illegal substances

If your content falls within one of these prohibited categories, or you are unsure, consult your legal counsel and inform your KWS technical support contact.
