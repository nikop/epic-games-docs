Configure Your Production Environment

After thoroughly testing each service in your test environment, the next step is to set up your production environment.

Follow the same procedure used to set up your product in the test environment:

  1. Configure the webhook. See the following documentation:

    • Configure the Age Verification Service Webhook.
    • Configure the Parent Verification Service Webhook.

  2. Set up the verification response. See the following documentation:

    • Configure the Age Verification Service Verification Response.
    • Configure the Parent Verification Service Verification Response.

  3. Integrate the API. See the following documentation:

    • Integrate the Age Verification Service API.
    • Integrate the Parent Verification Service API.

Note: Your product has a set of credentials for the test environment and a different set for the production environment. Ensure that you are using the correct set.

KWS Approval Process

After you set up your product in the production environment, KWS reviews your content to ensure it does not violate any specified restrictions.

Review Process Time Frames

KWS completes the review process within seven business days (Monday to Friday). Reviews might take longer during periods of high demand. We recommend reaching out to your Technical Account Manager once you have completed the setup process to minimize any potential delays. KWS notifies you when the review process is complete.

If your configuration fails the review process, KWS provides the reason so you can rectify the issue. For more information, see documentation: KWS General Terms (kidswebservices.com/general-terms).

Review Process Specifications

KWS reviews product and brand asset settings to ensure they contain no malicious or inappropriate content.

KWS will not approve your production environment configuration if your content violates any of the following restrictions:

Content Restrictions
Brand or product name
  • No inappropriate words.
  • No blatant plagiarism or copyright violation.
Privacy policy URL
  • Your privacy policy must exist at the link you provide.
  • Your privacy policy must make sense and not be meaningless content.
Customer support or Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) URL
  • Customer support or FAQs must exist at the link you provide.
  • The customer support or FAQs must make sense and not be meaningless content.
Redirect URL (optional) The URL you provide must point to a valid web resource if you provide one.
Banner image The following restrictions apply if you provide a custom banner image:
  • No blatant plagiarism or copyright violation.
  • The image must not be obscene.
  • The image must have some direct relation to your product or organization.
