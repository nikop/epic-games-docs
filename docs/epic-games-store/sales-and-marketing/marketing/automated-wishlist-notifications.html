Automated Wishlist Notifications

Information on wishlist pages and notifications for Epic Games Store users.

5 mins to read

On the Epic Games Store, every Epic Games Store user has a wishlist page where they can track offers they are interested in. When the Epic Games Store user adds an offer to the page and opts in, they get a notification when the offer goes on sale, and when it is available to purchase or pre-purchase.

An Epic Games Store user can add your offer to their wishlist as soon as you make it available to purchase or download on the Epic Games Store. You do not need to do anything else.

How Users Add Your Offer to Their Wishlists

On the Epic Games Store, a user can add your offer to their wishlist by taking one of the following steps:

  • In a carousel (for example, the New Release carousel), they click + on the offer.
  • In the product home page, they scroll down to the offer and click Add to Wishlist.

When the user adds your offer to their wishlist, they can view it in the list of offers on their My Wishlist page. To do this, the user clicks Wishlist on the Epic Games Store.

Wishlist Notifications

Note: A user must opt in to receive email notifications from their wishlist. More information: How Users Manage Their Wishlist Notifications on this page.

When a user adds your offer to their wishlist, they can automatically receive the following notifications:

  • Email Notification: Sent directly to the user's email inbox (if they subscribe to receive email notifications).
  • Pop-Up Notification: This is a real-time alert that appears when the user signs in to the Epic Games Store. Also called a toast notification.
  • Desktop Notification: This is a real-time alert that appears on PC (Windows, macOS) when the user is signed in to the Epic Games Store with the Epic Games Launcher. Also called a toast notification.

Notification Triggers

Note: A user must opt-in to receive email notifications from their wishlist. More information: How Users Manage Their Wishlist Notifications on this page.

When a user adds your offer to their wishlist, they can receive notifications for specific milestones in your offer’s lifecycle. The following milestones trigger notifications:

  • Status Changes
    When your product home page changes from one status to another:
    • Coming Soon to Pre-Purchase.
    • Pre-Purchase to Buy Now.
    • Coming Soon to Buy Now (without changing to Pre-Purchase).
      • For more information about your Product Home Page, see Manage Pages.
  • Discounts When you discount your offer by more than 10%.
    • For more information on setting up discounts, see Manage Discounts.

Users receive email and pop-up notifications up to 24 hours after the trigger.

You do not need to do anything to trigger the notifications—they trigger automatically. Before the trigger happens, make sure your Product Home Page has the latest information about your offer (for example, description, trailer, and screenshots) for when a user visits the page.

If at least one user adds your offer to their wishlist then notifications trigger automatically. Your offer does not need to be on a minimum number of wishlists to do so.

How Users Manage Their Wishlist Notifications

Users can manage their wishlist notification settings in three locations:

  • Account Settings: In their Epic Games account settings.
  • My Wishlist: In the My Wishlist page of the Epic Games Store.
  • Pop-Up Dialog: In a pop-up dialog box when they add an offer to their wishlist.

Account Settings

A user can manage their wishlist notification settings by following these steps:

  1. Sign in to the Epic Games Store.
  2. Click Username.
  3. Click Account.
    This displays the account page in a browser.
  4. In the side navigation, click Email Preferences.
  5. Make sure the Subscribe/Unsubscribe radio button is selected.
  6. Select or clear the Epic Games Store and Epic Games Store Wishlist Alerts checkboxes.

My Wishlist

A user can subscribe or unsubscribe to notifications by following these steps:

  1. Sign in to the Epic Games Store.
  2. Click Wishlist.
  3. At the top of the page, select or clear You are subscribed to wishlist email notifications.

Pop-Up Dialog

A user can subscribe or unsubscribe to notifications when they add an offer to their wishlist. To do this the user selects Receive Wishlist Emails.

How to Use Wishlists to Sell Your Offer

When your offer is on a number of wishlists, it shows how excited users are about it and helps you build an early attentive audience before it launches.

To get the most out of automated wishlist notifications, make sure your Product Home Page is ready with the latest information about your offer (for example, description, trailer, and screenshots) before key moments happen in your sales campaign.

To increase the number of users that add your offer to their wishlist, see the best practices and insights in the following documentation: Product Marketing Tips.

Product Visibility on the Epic Games Store

The Epic Games Store tracks the most wishlisted titles to make sure it surfaces content that is in high demand by the users. Users can see the offers included in the Top Upcoming Wishlisted on the main page of the Epic Games Store and on a dedicated page: Top Upcoming Wishlisted.

Some areas of the Epic Games Store use algorithmic featuring to populate content, and wishlisting can be part of that algorithm. For more information on algorithmic featuring, see the Store Policies documentation in the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal/~/store/policies).

How to Track Wishlist Metrics

Use the Analytics page in the Developer Portal to monitor the performance of your offer on users' wishlists. To do this:

  1. Go to your product's Developer Portal page:
    • In the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal), in the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product. (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)
  2. In the left navigation, click Analytics > Epic Games Store.
  3. Click Wishlists.
