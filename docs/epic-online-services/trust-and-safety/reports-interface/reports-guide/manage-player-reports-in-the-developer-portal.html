Guide: Enable Player Reporting for Your Game — 2. Manage Player Reports in the Developer Portal

Step-by-step guide on how to manage player reports in the Developer Portal.

4 mins to read

Previous section: 1. Send Player Reports From Your Game

You can manage the following aspects of player reports in the Developer Portal:

  • View and download report data.
  • Assign reports to yourself.
  • Resolve reports.
  • Sanction players based on reports against them.

Before You Start

Before you can manage player reports in the Developer Portal, you must complete these steps:

  • Integrate the EOS SDK into your code. (Documentation: Get Started Steps.)

  • Set up the Reports Interface to send player reports from your game. (Section in this guide: 1. Send Player Reports in Your Game.)

  • Verify that you have one of the following roles to manage player reports in the Developer Portal:

    • Admin
    • Player Moderation Tools

    For more information on roles, see the Roles (Developer Portal Access) documentation.

Manage Your Game’s Player Reports in the Developer Portal

You manage your game’s player reports in the Developer Portal, on the Player Reports page. To do this, follow the steps below. (Note that in the Developer Portal, your game is usually known as a “product”.)

To go to the Player Reports page:

  1. Go to your game's product page in the Developer Portal. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal at (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. In the left navigation, under Your Products, click the name of your game.
      • Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.
  2. In the left navigation, click Epic Online Services.
  3. In the Trust and Safety section, click Player Reports.

Note: The steps in this section suggest an order, but you do not need to complete them in order. You can complete the steps that you require.

Complete the following steps to manage your game's player reports:

  • Step A: Download Player Report Data
  • Step B: Review Player Reports

Step A: Download Player Report Data

On the Player Reports page, a bar graph tracks the reports that your players have sent during the last 30 days, per day or hour. Follow these steps to download the player report data:

  1. From the Deployment dropdown, select a deployment that is attached to the same sandbox where your players sent reports.
  2. Use the Daily or Hourly toggle above the bar graph to switch the bar graph to the time interval that you want to view the data in.
    • Note: The interval you choose determines the interval of the data that you download.
  3. Click .csv above the bar graph to download the bar graph data.

Step B: Review Player Reports

You can assign player reports to someone on your team to review and process. For example, someone on your team can review a player report and create a sanction based on it. If the reviewer finishes processing a player report, they can resolve it.

You manage player reports on the Player Reports page in the Developer Portal. To do this:

  1. From the Deployment dropdown, select the deployment that is attached to the same sandbox where your players sent their reports.
  2. Scroll down to the table below the Reported Players heading to see the list of all players with unresolved reports.
    • By default, the table lists all players reports for all assignees, listed by the Reported Player column.
      • Tip: Click Filter to view player reports based on the time frame in which they were sent or the Epic Games account that they are assigned to. For example, you can click Filter and select Last 24 Hours so you just see the player reports from the last 24 hours.
  3. In the Reported Player column, click the PUID of the player who has reports against them that you want to manage.
    • Tip: You can manage multiple reported players at once by clicking the checkbox next to each reported player that you want to manage reports for. Use the buttons that appear to bulk process the selected reported players.
      When you click a Product User ID, the page displays all player reports for the player, including those that are resolved.
      • Note: By default, in the right pane, Reports is selected. You can manage other items from this pane as well, but that is outside the scope of this guide. For more information on the other options, see the following documentation:
        • For information on linked accounts, see Identity Provider Management.
        • For information on Anti-Cheat and detections, see Anti-Cheat Interfaces.
        • For information on sanctions, see Sanctions Interface.
  4. Click one of the following to manage all open reports against the player:
    • Resolve: Resolves all open reports for the selected player.
      • Note: If you are viewing all reports against the player, you have the option to sanction the player when you resolve the reports.
        • This guide does not cover how to sanction players. For more information on sanctions, see the Sanctions Interface documentation.
    • Assign to me: Assigns all open reports for the selected player to your Epic Games account.
    • Unassign: Unassigns your Epic Games account from all open reports for the selected player.

Results

Reports that you mark as resolved no longer display in the Reported Players section. In the full list of reports for a player, the reports that you resolve are marked as Resolved. Any reports that you have assigned to your Epic Games account display you as the assignee.

Notes

  • There is no limit on the number of reports that can be stored by the EOS Player Reports service. However, reports are only stored for 30 days before being deleted.
  • You can view any sanctions created for the reported player from the Sanctions page in the Developer Portal. To query player sanctions from your game, use the Sanctions Interface. For more information on sanctions, see the Sanctions Interface documentation.
