Submit and Manage Unreal Engine Royalties

Submit Royalty Forms to declare your royalties due for Unreal Engine products.

9 mins to read

This page applies if your product (game or other software application) uses Unreal Engine.

This page applies if your product uses Unreal Engine under the Unreal Engine End User License Agreement (EULA). More information: unrealengine.com/…/eula/unreal. If your product uses Unreal Engine under a custom license, or if you have any questions about licensing, open a private support request at this link: eoshelp.epicgames.com~.

For each of your products that uses Unreal Engine, you must do the following for each calendar quarter:

  • Report revenue to Epic Games on a per-product basis, within 45 days of the end of the calendar quarter. You can do this in the Developer Portal, as described on this page.
  • Pay Epic Games any due royalties. After you report your revenue, Epic Games emails you an invoice for your royalty payment.

For more information about royalties, see the Unreal Engine End User License Agreement (EULA): unrealengine.com/…/eula/unreal.

If you no longer distribute your product, you no longer need to report revenue for it. You can notify Epic Games that you have retired the product. To do so, see Retire a Product.

Note: If your product is distributed under a custom license (in other words, if the UELA does not apply), you have your own licensing terms. In this situation, you must report and pay your royalties in accordance with the terms of your custom license. Do not use the processes described in this documentation.

Report Your Revenue

To report your revenue to Epic Games, you must submit a royalty form in the Developer Portal.

Note: If you want to report revenue for a calendar quarter, the following must apply:

  • You can only report revenue after the calendar quarter. For example, if you want to submit a royalty form for Q2 2025, then you can only do this in or after Q3 2025.
    • If the calendar quarter is not complete, you cannot start a new royalty form for that calendar quarter.
  • To submit a royalty form for a calendar quarter, you must have submitted royalty forms for all previous calendar quarters. For example, you cannot submit a form for Q2 2025 if you haven't submitted a form for Q1 2025.

Note: You don't need to report revenue to Epic Games for a calendar quarter if either of the following applies:

  • Your product made less than $10,000 USD gross revenue in the quarter or
  • Your product's lifetime revenue is less than $1,000,000 USD.

In either situation, you can skip reporting for the calendar quarter. To do so, see Skip a Quarterly Revenue Report on this page.

If you have more than one product, you can report revenue for one product at a time, or for all of your products together.

The Royalties page of the Developer Portal shows the status of each of your products for each calendar quarter, as follows:

  • Report royalties: A royalty report is due for this product, for the calendar quarter.
  • Quarter skipped: You have skipped royalty reporting, for this product, for the calendar quarter.
  • Submitted: You have completed a royalty report, for this product, for the calendar quarter.

Start a New Royalty Form

Epic Games Store Submission Fees

If you don't plan to publish your product on the Epic Games Store, you don't have to pay the Epic Games Store submission fee. To report revenue, follow the steps below.

To start a new royalty form:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Royalties page. You can reach the Royalties page in any of the following ways:
    • From the Unreal Engine commercial game deployment guidelines page (unrealengine.com/…/release). In the Royalty payment and tracking panel, click Royalty report.
    • From a link in an email notification from Epic Games.
    • In the Developer Portal:
      • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
      • b. Select the correct organization for your product.
      • c. In the left navigation, in the Epic Tools section click Unreal Engine > Royalties.
      • Note: You can only access the Unreal Engine Royalties page if you have one of the following user roles for your Developer Portal organization: Owner, Admin, or Financial, or any custom role with the UE Royalties permission.
        • For information on how to manage user roles, see documentation: Organizations.
  2. If you want to report revenue for all of your products at the same time for a calendar quarter: On the heading row for that calendar quarter, click Report all <quarter name> royalties. For example, for quarter 4, click Report all Q4 royalties (see screenshot below).
    • You can only report revenue after the calendar quarter. For example, if you want to submit a royalty form for Q2 2026, then you can only do this in or after Q3 2026.
      • If the calendar quarter is not complete, you cannot start a new royalty form for that calendar quarter.
    • To submit royalties for a calendar quarter, you must have submitted royalty forms for all previous calendar quarters. For example, you cannot submit a form for Q2 2026 if you haven't submitted a form for Q1 2026.
  1. If you want to report revenue for an individual product for a calendar quarter: Next to that product click Report royalties.
    • Note: If you cannot see a product in the list, then you need to submit a Release Form for the product before you can submit a royalty form for it. For information on how to submit a Release Form, see Notify Epic Games About Your Product Releases.
  2. Complete stages 1 to 3 described below.

Stage 1: Add Revenue Source

In this stage, the products appear as follows:

  • If you are reporting on one product only, that product appears on its own.
  • If you are reporting on all your products at the same time, there is a separate section for each product.
  • If one of your products is missing from the list, then you need to submit a Release Form for the product before you can report royalties for it. For information, see documentation: Notify Epic Games About Your Product Releases.

For each product, do the following:

  1. If the details of the product are hidden, click the arrow to expand the details for that product:
  1. The Lifetime to Date section displays information based on the revenue information you provided for past quarters, where applicable.
    • You cannot change the information in this section.
  2. In the Revenue sources section, declare revenue for each revenue source. A revenue source is a platform on which you sell your product. For each revenue source, do the following:
    • a. In Revenue source, select the revenue source from the dropdown list:
      • Console (includes Xbox, PlayStation and Switch)
      • PC (includes Windows, macOS)
      • Mobile (includes iOS and Android)
      • Other (for any other revenue source)
    • b. In Advances received, enter the funds you have received for future sales of the product, such as pre-orders and recoupable publisher advances, in the calendar quarter you are reporting on.
    • c. In Gross revenue, enter the revenue you have received for the product in the calendar quarter you are reporting on. Gross revenue is the total amount you received from sales, after refunds, chargebacks, and sales or value added taxes.
      • Include revenue for the following:
        • Downloadable content
        • In-app purchases
        • Microtransactions
        • Virtual currency redemption
        • Subscription fees
        • Kickstarter funds related to game access or content (but not Kickstarter funds related to non-gameplay physical or digital goods offered as backer rewards)
        • Revenue from in-app advertising and affiliate programs
    • Note:
      • You don't need to include the first USD$5 million of revenue on lifetime Oculus sales.
      • You don't need to include any revenue from the Epic Games Store.
    • d. In Units sold, enter the total number of units you have sold for this revenue source, in the calendar quarter you are reporting on.
    • Platforms: Select the platforms on which you sold the product during the calendar quarter.
  3. For each additional revenue source for this product, click Add additional revenue source and repeat step 3 above for that revenue source.
  4. For each additional product, repeat steps 1–4 above.

Add details for all the products you want to declare. Then, at the bottom of the form, click Next.

Stage 2: Attach Supporting File (Optional)

Attach a file that provides evidence for the figures you entered in Stage 1, such as a report from a storefront. Your file can be in any of these formats:

  • Microsoft Excel (.XLSX)
  • PDF (.PDF)
  • Comma-separated values (.CSV)
  • PNG image (.PNG)
  • Microsoft Word (.DOCX)

To attach a supporting file:

  1. Drag and drop the file into the files area.
    • Alternatively, click browse and select the file.
  2. If you want to provide additional information about the file, or its contents, type the explanation in the Additional details box.
  3. At the bottom of the page, click Next.

Stage 3: Summary

This stage shows the details you entered in the previous stages. It shows a royalty calculation, so you know immediately what royalty amount you owe Epic Games for the calendar quarter.

The calculation takes into account whether your product is in the Launch Everywhere with Epic program (more information: Submit a Release Form > Stage 4: Launch Everywhere with Epic).

Check that all of the details you have entered are correct and complete. If you need to change anything, click Previous to go back.

If everything is correct and complete, click Submit royalty report to submit your report.

You can edit the report up to three business days after you submit it. For more information, see Edit a Quarterly Revenue Report on this page.

Skip a Quarterly Revenue Report

You don't need to report revenue (complete a royalty form) to Epic Games for a calendar quarter if either of the following applies:

  • Your product made less than $10,000 USD gross revenue in the quarter or
  • Your product's lifetime revenue is less than $1,000,000 USD.

In either situation, you can skip reporting for the calendar quarter in the Developer Portal. Do not complete a revenue report for the calendar quarter. Use the procedure described below to skip the revenue report.

Note: You can only skip a revenue report if you have one of the following user roles for your Developer Portal organization: Owner, Admin, Financial, or any custom role with the UE Royalties permission. For information on how to manage user roles, see documentation: Organizations.

To skip revenue reporting for a calendar quarter:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. Select your organization.
  3. In the left navigation, in the Epic Tools section click Unreal Engine > Royalties.
  4. On the same row as the product, click the three dots ( ... ):
  1. Select Skip quarter.

Edit a Quarterly Revenue Report

You can edit a revenue report you previously submitted, up to three business days after you submitted the report.

Note:

  • If you need to make changes to a revenue report more than three business days after you submitted the report, open a private case with Epic Online Services support on eoshelp.epicgames.com/~/eos-case-page.
  • You can only edit a revenue report if you have one of the following user roles for your Developer Portal organization: Owner, Admin, Financial, or any custom role with the UE Royalties permission. For information on how to manage user roles, see documentation: Organizations.

To edit a revenue report:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. Select your organization.
  3. In the left navigation, in the Epic Tools section click Unreal Engine > Royalties.
  4. On the same row as the report you want to edit, click Edit Report.
  5. In the dialog, click Yes, edit report.
  6. Complete the report in the same way as you complete a new revenue report. For information, follow the stages on this page, starting at Stage 1: Add Revenue Source.
