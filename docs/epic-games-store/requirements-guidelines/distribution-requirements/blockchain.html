Blockchain Technology Guidelines

Requirements for distributing blockchain technology products on the Epic Games Store.

5 mins to read

Mobile Early Adopter: If you are in the Mobile Early Adopter program, go to the Mobile Early Adopter — Requirements and Guidelines > Blockchain, NFT, or Cryptocurrency Technology documentation for guidance.

On the Epic Games Store, you can publish products that support blockchain, NFT, or cryptocurrency technology (known as “blockchain technology”—see the following glossary entry for a full definition: blockchain technology). However, these products must follow specific guidelines and requirements.

Note: If you are a Mobile Early Adopter, you cannot make your mobile products on the Epic Games Store available for pre-purchase, and your products cannot use NFT or blockchain technology.

Blockchain Technology Requirements

Product Requirements

You must follow the requirements below when you distribute a product on the Epic Games Store that leverages, contains, interfaces with, or acts as an intermediary or medium for blockchain, non-fungible tokens (NFTs), cryptocurrency, or any decentralized autonomous organizations blockchain technology (known as blockchain technology):

  • Rider agreement
    • You must accept and sign the latest version of the Blockchain Rider (in addition to the distribution agreement) if your product contains blockchain technology. For more information on how to accept and sign riders and agreements, see the following documentation: Manage Agreements.
    • Find the latest Blockchain Rider in Developer Portal dev.epicgames.com/~/epic_games_blockchain.
  • Price and payments
    • Base product offer
      • You must distribute your base product offer for free.
    • Additional offers
      • You can use only the following offer types and you must distribute them for free:
        • Edition
        • Demo
          Note: This includes beta versions.
      • You must not use any other offer types (such as, add-on, season pass, consumable, or digital extra). The Epic Games Store team rejects these offer types during the review process. (However, you can distribute these offer types outside of the Epic Games Store, for example on your own website.)
    • You understand and accept that you are 100% responsible for payments, customer support, fraud detection, and refunds associated with the product.
  • Linked-to content
    • Links to any blockchain, NFT, or cryptocurrency marketplaces are not allowed on any Epic Games Store pages.
  • Regional restrictions

    • You cannot make any products leveraging blockchain technology available to users in China and South Korea. In other words, blockchain products will be blocked in this region.

    • The product must be rated in all regions where it will be distributed. This applies to any purchasable release state, such as Early Access and pre-purchase. It does not apply to Early Store Discovery. More information:

      • Early Access Program Requirements
      • Manage Pages > Enabled for Early Store Discovery

    • You must be in compliance with all relevant blockchain technology laws and regulations for the regions in which your product will be distributed.

  • Consistent in-game experience
    • To avoid player confusion, your product submission materials must be consistent with your product experience. When you create a new product and indicate that your product uses blockchain technology, Epic Games will review it as such. If, when you submit this product for review by Epic Games Store, it then does not contain blockchain technology, you must ensure there are no features or references to blockchain technology in the product. If there are any, your product is blocked from release. The list of situations that indicate a product leverages blockchain technology include, but is not limited to, the following:
      • You select “Yes” in the NFT and Blockchain Usage field in Offer Details > General Details.
      • Your build contains blockchain features, such as a wallet.
      • Your social links contain blockchain content.
    • If you launch a version of your product on the Epic Games Store that does not use blockchain technology but your product has a version on a different storefront that does use blockchain technology, you must indicate that the Epic Games Store version does not use blockchain technology. Do this on your product’s Epic Games Store FAQ page. See the section on this page for more information: FAQ Page
    • If you want to submit a product to the Epic Games Store that has two versions, blockchain technologies version and a non-blockchain technologies version, you must create a separate product for each version. You cannot submit both versions as one product.
    • If you want to update your live product to add blockchain technology, you must retire the current product and submit a new product for review.
  • Additional requirements
    • Products must not facilitate gambling. Documentation: Content Requirements > Prohibited Content.
    • You must make conspicuous disclosures of all terms, conditions, risks, and other relevant information related to the use of blockchain technology in the product.
    • You must tag your product as a Blockchain/NFT Product and create an FAQ page. See the section on: Storefront Configurations.
    • Epic Games Store reserves the right to remove products leveraging blockchain technology at any time, based on actions in the market (for example, in response to issues that negatively impact players).

Storefront Configurations

When publishing blockchain technology products to the storefront, the following configurations are required.

Blockchain/NFT Product Tag

When configuring your offer, you must select "Yes" for the NFT and Blockchain setting located in the General Details section. This setting promotes transparency to users by adding the Blockchain/NFT tag and disclaimer text to your product home page. If you select yes, you must add a mandatory FAQ page to your product. For information about FAQ pages, see documentation: Manage Pages > Create an FAQ Page.

More information: Manage Offers

FAQ Page

You must create an FAQ page that provides users with additional information about your product. For products using blockchain technology, FAQs should inform users about the specific technology being used as well as how it may impact them as consumers.

As a guideline, the following questions are required and must be included in your FAQ page. You are welcome to add additional questions or information to your FAQ page, as needed.

  • How is blockchain, NFT, or cryptocurrency used in this product?
  • What utility does this bring to the product?
  • Does this product require me to connect an external wallet? What wallets does this product support?
  • Can I use the product without interacting or engaging with blockchain, NFT, or cryptocurrency?
  • What chain does this product use? Is the chain proof-of-work or proof-of-stake?
  • Does this product have a marketplace? What can I do with the things that I earn (or purchase)?
  • Are there in-app purchases within the gameplay? How are payments processed?

If your product contains in-app purchases, you must clearly share additional information about these purchases with users as part of the product’s store presence. This helps foster a transparent and trustworthy store experience for Epic Games Store users.
