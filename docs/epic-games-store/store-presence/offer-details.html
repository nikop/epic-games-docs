Offer Details

Understand the details that you can configure on your offers.

24 mins to read

This page describes the details that you can set on an offer when you create or edit the offer. For information on how to create or edit an offer, see the Manage Offers page.

Note: Some of these details are mandatory, and some are optional. The Developer Portal displays the offer details in sections called Required Information and Optional Information. This varies, depending on the type of offer.

You can clone (copy) offer details from one offer to other offers in the same product. For more information, see Clone Offer Details Between Offers.

General Details

For information on how to create and edit General Details for an offer, see documentation: Manage Offers.

You can set the following general details for an offer:

  • Name: The name of the offer. This is displayed to users on the storefront.
  • Short Description: A simple description of your product that appears throughout the storefront as well as in the SEO and sharing settings.
  • Base Game Dependency: (Only available in the Mobile Early Adopter program). Select the platforms on which you want the offer to be available: PC (Windows, macOS), mobile (iOS, Android).
    • You can only select platforms for which you have created a base product offer. For more information on base product offers, see Mobile Early Adopter: Manage Offers—Offer Types.
  • Early Access Product: Determines if your product displays as an Early Access title on the storefront. Documentation: Early Access Program Requirements.
  • Metadata: General information about your offer such as genres, features, and developer and publisher names. This information is visible on the storefront, and helps Epic Games Store users find and understand your offer.
    • To quickly import this information from your Store Settings, click Use Product Metadata. Documentation: Configure Store Settings.
    • Note the following about the Platforms setting:
      • If an offer has an estimated or unknown available date, you can set platforms (such as Windows) for that offer in the Metadata section.
      • If an offer has a specific available date, the Developer Portal automatically pulls platform information from the offer's active binary.
      • You cannot set platforms individually for offer types that do not have an associated artifact, such as editions, season passes, or consumables. Instead, these offers use the platforms assigned to your base product offer. To ensure your offers without artifacts are assigned the appropriate platforms, when you update the platforms assigned to your base product offer, you must push the offer to the Stage sandbox. (For more information about push to Stage, see Get Started Steps—Submit for Final Review).
  • NFT and Blockchain Usage: If your product contains NFT, cryptocurrency, or blockchain technology in any capacity, you must select Yes. This information is shown in the storefront.
    • As a requirement for distributing this type of product, you must disclose this information to users. Documentation: Blockchain Technology Guidelines.
  • Promotional Content: If your offer promotes your own (or someone else's) brand, product, or business, then you must select Yes. If you select Yes, the offer is labeled on the Epic Games Store as promotional content.
    • Documentation: Requirements Overview–Promotional Content.
  • Offer Content: Set this to Yes if the offer contains virtual currency or other consumable items. This setting is only available for add-on type offers.

Dates

For information on how to create and edit Date details for an offer, see documentation: Manage Offers.

Use offer dates to control when your offer is available to buy or download on the Epic Games Store.

Note—when you update the dates on existing offers: You can update the dates for an existing offer at any time. However, the process is slightly different depending on which sandbox the offer is currently in. For more information, see Update Offer Dates on this page.

When you create a new offer or configure dates for the first time on an existing offer, the Developer Portal asks you a series of questions. These questions ensure that you provide the relevant details. For example: Was this offer previously released on PC or Mac? Your answers are stored in the settings described below. You can edit them at any time as needed. For information on how to edit offer settings, see documentation: Manage Offers–Edit an Existing Offer.

Note: This series of questions is called the "guided experience". You can use the guided experience to change your offer's dates at any time. To do so, when you edit the offer dates, click Guided experience.

The available date settings are as follows:

  • Initial release date on another platform: The date the product was originally available for purchase on another storefront, if applicable. If this is your first release, leave this setting blank.
  • Early store discovery: viewable date and time: The date and time on which the offer is discoverable (visible to users) on the Epic Games Store.
    • This is also known as a viewable date.
    • From this date and time, users can add the offer to their wishlists.
    • This date and time does not determine when the product becomes available for purchase.
    • If a viewable date occurs before an available date, users can review the offer and add it to their wishlists. However, they cannot purchase the offer until the available date.
  • Pre-purchase date (optional): Indicates the date from which Epic Games Store users can purchase your offer. This can be before your available date: users can pay for your product, even though you have not yet released it.
    • There are guidelines and best practices for pre-purchase offers. More information: Pre-Purchase Guidelines. For more information about pre-purchase bonuses, see Included Offers on this page.
    • After you set a pre-purchase date on an offer and then push the offer to your Stage or Live sandboxes, you cannot change the pre-purchase date in the Developer Portal to a date in the past. However, you can still change the pre-purchase date to a future date. To change the date, contact your Release Manager.
  • Available date on the Epic Games Store:
    • Available date type: Select one of the following options:
      • Specific
        • You must provide a specific available date and time for the offer.
        • You must attach a binary to the artifact for your offer if you want to display the offer as Coming Soon on the storefront.
      • Estimated
        • You do not need to attach a binary to the artifact for your offer.
        • You must provide a viewable date (see above).
        • You must manually set the appropriate platforms for the offer (such as Windows). The Platforms field is in the General Details section. For more information, see General Details on this page.
        • You must update your offer with a specific available date and attach at least one binary to the offer's artifact at least one week before your intended available date.
          • When your offer has an estimated available date and the offer did not launch, the estimated available date is automatically removed and the offer appears on the storefront as "Coming Soon". To release your product, add a specific available date and resubmit your product for review.
      • Available date unknown
        • The offer is displayed on the storefront as "Coming Soon".
        • You do not need to attach a binary to the artifact for your offer.
        • You must provide a viewable date (see above).
        • You must manually set the appropriate platforms (such as Windows) for the offer. The Platforms field is available in the General Details section. For more information, see General Details on this page.
        • At least one week before your intended available date, you must update your offer with a specific available date and attach at least one binary to the offer's artifact.
    • Available date and time: The date and time from which Epic Games Store users can purchase your offer.
      • This setting is available only if you select Specific in Available date type.
      • If your release date changes and your product has pre-purchase enabled, then you might need to give refunds to product buyers. Documentation: Pre-Purchase Guidelines—Delayed Release Date (Delayed Available Date).
    • The following settings are available only if you select Estimated in Available date type:
      • Estimated date type: Specifies whether you want to set the estimated available date as a specific day, month, quarter, or year. Depending on which option you choose, in the following options you then set the specific date, month, quarter, or year.
        • Available date: The date on which you estimate that the offer will launch.
          • This setting is available only if you select By day in Estimated date type.
        • Month: The month in which you estimate that the offer will launch.
          • This setting is available only if you select By month in Estimated date type.
        • Quarter: The quarter in which you estimate that the offer will launch.
          • This setting is available only if you select By quarter in Estimated date type.
        • Year: The year in which you estimate that the offer will launch.
          • This setting is available only if you select By month, By quarter, or By year in Estimated date type.
  • Expiration date and time: The date that the offer is no longer available to users on the Epic Games Store.
    • This setting is available only if you select Specific in Available date type.
    • Expired offers are no longer discoverable or purchasable on the Epic Games Store. However, they remain accessible to users who previously purchased them.

When you create an offer, you can add the dates in two ways:

  • Enter them manually. This lets you enter different dates on the offer from the dates on the base product offer.
    • Note: This is useful for digital extras, such as soundtracks and art books, where the customer does not need the base offer. In this situation, the offer available date can be before the base product offer's available date. For offers that are not digital extras, such as consumables, customers need the base product offer. Therefore, you must select an available date that is the same as or later than the available date on the base product offer.
  • Import dates from your base product offer.
    • Note: When you define your base product offer, you cannot import dates.

To import dates from your base product offer:

  • In the Offer Dates panel, click Use Base Offer Dates.

Note: When you publish your offer (when you push it to your Live sandbox), the Epic Games Store automatically updates any invalid dates in the offer. The Epic Games Store displays a notification to inform you that it has updated one or more dates.

For example, when you update your offer, if your offer's available date is in the past and the offer is not yet available for purchase on the store, then Epic Games Store updates the available date to the date and time that you make the update.

Update Offer Dates

You can update an offer's dates at any time. However, the process is different depending on which sandbox the offer is currently in.

Note:

  • You cannot directly update offer dates in the Stage sandbox. To update an offer in Stage, make the changes to the offer in your Dev sandbox. Then push the changes to Stage when appropriate.
    • For more information, see Offers in the Dev Sandbox on this page.
  • If your available date changes and your product has pre-purchase enabled, you might need to give refunds to people who bought the offer.
    • Documentation: Pre-Purchase Guidelines—Delayed Release Date (Delayed Available Date).
  • After you set a pre-purchase date on an offer and then push the offer to your Stage or Live sandboxes, you cannot change the pre-purchase date in the Developer Portal. To change the date, contact your Release Manager.
  • If you change the available date on an offer and then push the offer to the Live sandbox, this might affect any discounts on the offer. The Developer Portal automatically sets any discounts on the offer that have dates set in the future to a draft state (documentation: Manage Discounts–Discount Statuses). This means that the discounts do not apply to the offer unless you apply them again. To apply these discounts to the offer again, do the following:
    • i. Check the discounts.
    • ii. Adjust the discount dates if necessary.
    • iii. Resubmit the discounts for review. Documentation: Manage Discounts > Submit Discounts for Validation and Review.

Offers in the Dev Sandbox

To update offer dates in your Dev sandbox:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Offers page. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. Select the correct organization for your product. (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the on-screen instructions.)
    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.
    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Offers.
  2. Select the Dev sandbox.
  3. Click the name of the offer you want to update.
  4. Click Dates.
  5. To edit a date, click its calendar icon.
  6. Click Save Changes.

Offers in the Stage Sandbox

To update the offer in your Stage sandbox, follow the procedure in Offers in the Dev Sandbox on this page, then push the changes to Stage.

  • For more information about push to Stage, see Get Started Steps—Submit for Final Review).

Offers in the Live Sandbox

You might want to update offer dates for a product that you have already pushed live. For example this might happen when your release schedule changes. This is called a "live update". Live updates take immediate effect on the storefront. They do not require approval by the Epic Games Store team.

Note:

  • You can only make live updates to future offer dates. You cannot change past offer dates (that is, offer dates that have already occurred).
  • You cannot add additional dates to an offer with the live update process. You can only make live updates to dates that you have already configured. For example, if an offer does not contain an expiration date, you cannot add an expiration date with this process.
  • You cannot use a live update to change the type of available date set for an offer. For example, if an offer's available date type is set to estimated, you cannot change the type to specific through this process. To change available date types, follow the publishing process.
    • Documentation: Publishing Tools Workflow.
  • When you update offer dates in your live sandbox, you automatically overwrite the corresponding offer date information in all sandboxes (Dev, Stage, and Live). For example, if you update the available date for an offer in the Live sandbox, the versions of that offer in the Dev and Stage sandboxes also receive that update. Exception: the Stage sandbox is not updated if the offer is currently under review by the Epic Games Store team.

To update your offer dates in the Live sandbox:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Offers page. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. Select the correct organization for your product. (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the on-screen instructions.)
    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.
    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store.
  2. To the right of your product’s name, click Make Live update.
    • Note: Make Live update is also available on the Release Management page.
  3. On the Live Update screen, select Offer Dates.
  4. Follow the on-screen instructions.

Store Visibility

For information on how to create and edit Store Visibility details for an offer, see documentation: Manage Offers.

You can choose whether or not Epic Games Store users can find the offer with a search on the Epic Games Store. The available settings are as follows:

  • Searchable in store: Epic Games Store users can find the offer page with the Epic Games Store's search feature.
    • The offer might also be available by other methods, such as with an in-game redemption code.
    • TIP: To go directly to the offer page in the Developer Portal, for example to edit the page, click the offer page's name.
  • Direct link only: The offer page can only be viewed by Epic Games Store users who have the direct link. It cannot be found with the Epic Games Store's search feature. You can use this option for offers that you want to be exclusively available for in-game redemption, or for Epic Games Store users with promo codes.
    • People can still find your offer page by searching in external search engines, such as Google.
    • The offer might also be available by other methods, such as with an in-game redemption code.
    • You cannot use this feature with your base product offer.
  • Not Visible: The offer page is completely hidden and the offer is not discoverable. You can use this option for offers that you want to be exclusively available for in-game redemption, or for Epic Games Store users with promo codes.
    • People will not find your offer page with the Epic Games Store search feature or in external search engines, such as Google.
    • You cannot use this feature with your base product offer.

Price

For information on how to create and edit Price details for an offer, see documentation: Manage Offers.

In the Base Price panel, in Price tier select the price you want to charge for the offer. You select the base price in USD. For more information, see documentation: Pricing Overview—Pricing Restrictions.

When you select the price, the Regional pricing section displays the equivalent prices in the local currency for each available region. These are the prices that Epic Games Store users will pay when they buy the offer. Note: These prices are fixed at the time you create the offer, and do not automatically change to match changes in currency value.

You can create your own custom price tiers to determine your own regional pricing, instead of the Epic-delivered price tiers. For more information, see documentation: Create Custom Price Tiers.

There are some limitations on pricing, including when you can change the price for an offer. For information, see Pricing Overview-Pricing Restrictions.

Artifact Settings

For information on how to create and edit Artifact Settings for an offer, see documentation: Manage Offers.

On the Epic Games Store, an artifact represents a single downloadable piece of content in your product, such as a game client or mod editor.

The Developer Portal automatically creates artifacts for your product in the following situations:

  • When you create the product.
  • When you create an additional offer that features downloadable content, such as demos or digital extras.

Artifact Settings on the Base Product Offer

For your base product offer, you must configure the following settings:

  • LINKED ARTIFACT: (Optional) To upload a binary for the artifact, and change other artifact settings, click the arrow ( -> ).
    • Note: This takes you to the Manage Artifact panel. Documentation: Manage Artifacts.
  • Default folder: Specifies the name of the default folder where the artifact associated with the offer is installed on users' devices. The folder name must follow these rules:
    • It must be alphanumeric.
    • It must begin with a letter.
    • It must be 25 characters or less in length.
    • It cannot contain spaces.
  • EAS Integration: If you use an Epic Account Services application of your product, enter the application ID.

Artifact Settings on Other Offer Types

Demo or Digital Extra Offers

When you create a Demo or Digital Extra type offer (Documentation: Offer Types), the Developer Portal automatically creates an artifact and links it to the offer. You can upload a binary for the artifact from the Manage Artifact panel.

To quickly go to this panel from the Artifact Settings panel, in the LINKED ARTIFACT section click the arrow ( -> ).

Add-On Offers

The Developer Portal does not automatically create an artifact for add-on offers, because add-on offers might not feature downloadable content.

If you need an artifact for an add-on offer, in the Artifact Settings panel click Create Artifact. You can manage the artifact from the Manage Artifact panel. To quickly go to this panel from the Artifact Settings panel, in the LINKED ARTIFACT section click the arrow ( -> ).

More information:

  • Documentation: Manage Artifacts.

Advanced Item Config

To enable advanced item config, contact your release manager or create a private discussion on eoshelp.epicgames.com.

After advanced item config is enabled, for information on how to create and edit the advanced item config for an offer, see documentation: Manage Offers.

Advanced item config is a set of advanced settings related to processes and installation. You can use it for various advanced configurations of your product. For example, you could use it to add the following functionality to your product:

  • Configure a separate launcher for your game.
  • Make the launcher aware of the .exe folders in the game file.
  • Allow updates to go through your launcher instead of the Epic Games Launcher.

If you have advanced item config enabled, the advanced item config settings are visible in the Artifact Settings panel. All advanced item config settings are optional.

The advanced item config settings are as follows:

  • Process name: Specifies the pathname to a separate launcher in your product’s installation folder, such as an .exe file.
  • Main Window Process Name: Use this setting to minimize the Epic Games Launcher when your product runs. Specify the main executable for your product. When this executable runs, the Epic Games Launcher minimizes.
  • Background Process Names: Specifies an .exe file that will not count as a user's time using your product (that is, it doesn't count against playtime).
    • A user can refund a product purchase if they have used the product for two hours or less. If you don't use this option, then for products with a launcher, any elapsed time during which the launcher runs counts as playtime. If you use this option, you can exclude the launcher or any other specific background processes from the user’s accumulated playtime for your product.
  • Launcher updates after the first install: If you set this option to Disabled, product updates go through your launcher, not the Epic Games Launcher.
    • Note: Launcher updates after the first install is not set by default, but acts as though it is set to Automatic. This means that the product will update when an update is available through the Epic Games Launcher.
  • DLC Installation: If you set this option to Disabled, downloadable content (DLC) does not automatically install on users' machines. Instead, users of your product can opt in to DLC installations. Use this option to reduce the initial download size if your product has large add-ons.
    • Note: DLC Installation is not set by default, but acts as though it is set to Automatic. This means that when an Epic Games Store user downloads the product, any DLC that they are entitled to is automatically installed.
  • Denuvo Anti-Tamper Integration: if you set this option to Pass Token, the Epic Games Store passes the .ovt file to the Denuvo service.
  • Notarized and signed by Apple: if you set this option to Yes, your product follows the launch behavior on macOS for signed or notarized games.
  • Multiple game Instances: if you set this option to Allowed, multiple instances of your product can run simultaneously.

Included Offers

For information on how to create and edit Included Offers for an offer, see documentation: Manage Offers.

Base product offers, editions, and season passes can have included offers. When an Epic Games Store user buys an offer (or downloads it for free if the offer is free of charge) that has one or more included offers, they get entitlements for the offer and all the included offers.

There is no limit to the number of included offers you can have on an offer. You can add a maximum of five included offers at once. You can repeat this process as many times as you like to add the number of included offers you need on the offer.

Mobile Early Adopter program only: You can only select an offer as an included offer if it has a binary that matches the platform of the base product offer. For example, if the base product offer is on Windows, you can only select an offer as an included offer if it has a Windows binary.

Free offers with included offers

If you make an offer available free of charge on the Epic Games Store, then anyone who downloads it also gets any included offers free of charge.

Note: When you create an edition, you must specify the included offers.

For example:

When you create an edition, you first select a base offer for the edition. This base offer can be your base product offer or another edition. You can also add included offers to the edition. These might be for season passes, add-ons, digital extras, and so on. When an Epic Games Store user buys your edition, they receive the base offer and the included offers.

Add an Included Offer

To add an offer as an included offer, you must do so from the Offers page.

To add an offer as a pre-purchase bonus:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Offers page. To do this:

    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

    • b. Select the correct organization for your product.

      (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the on-screen instructions.)

    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.

    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Offers.

  2. Select the sandbox for the offer you want to add an included offer.
  3. In the Offers list, click the offer corresponding to the product that you want to add an included offer.
  4. In the Offer Details panel, in Optional information, click Included Offers.
  5. From the included offer dropdown, select the offer you want to associate.
  6. Click Save Changes.

Considerations When You Want to Update the Base Offer for an Edition

The main component of an edition is the base offer. The base offer can be one of the following:

  • Your base product offer. This grants users access to your primary product.
  • Another edition. This grants users access to all offers included in that edition.

You might want to make changes to the base offer for an edition. For example, you might want to update the artifact for a base offer or add additional included offers to a base offer.

However, If you make changes to a base offer, then push the base offer to Stage, this also pushes the associated edition to Stage.

If you want to update the base offer but do not want to push the associated edition to Stage, you must do the following:

  1. Unlink the base offer from its associated edition.
    • More information: see Unlink a Base Offer from an Edition below.
    • Note: you might want to unlink the base offer from more than one edition.
  2. Make your updates to the base offer.
  3. Push the base offer to your Stage sandbox.
    • (For more information about push to Stage, see Get Started Steps—Submit for Final Review).
  4. Relink the base offer to your edition or editions.
    • More information: See Link a Base Offer to an Edition below.

This ensures that your updates to the base offer are also reflected in the edition. If you do not follow the above process, then the associated edition might be linked to an outdated version of the base offer.

Unlink a Base Offer from an Edition

To unlink a base offer from an edition:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Offers page. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. Select the correct organization for your product. (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the on-screen instructions.)
    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.
    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Offers.
  2. Select the Dev sandbox.
  3. Click the edition that you want to update.
  4. Click Included offers.
  5. In the Base Offer section, to the left of the offer name click Unlink:
  1. Click Save Changes.
  2. Click Close.

Link a Base Offer to an Edition

To link a base offer to an edition:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Offers page. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. Select the correct organization for your product. (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the on-screen instructions.)
    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.
    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Offers.
  2. Select the Dev sandbox.
  3. Click the edition that you want to update.
  4. Click Included offers.
  5. In the Base Offer section, to the left of the offer name click Link:
  1. Click Save Changes.

Nested Pre-Purchase Bonuses

The Epic Games Store does not support nested pre-purchase bonuses. For example:

  • A product has two editions called “Silver Edition” and “Gold Edition”.
  • A user pre-purchases a Gold Edition of a product.
  • The Gold Edition comes with a bonus.
  • The Gold Edition does not automatically inherit any pre-purchase bonus associated with the Silver Edition.
  • If you want to provide the same bonuses with the Gold Edition and the Silver Edition, you must add the bonuses to both editions.

Regions

For information on how to create and edit Region details for an offer, see documentation: Manage Offers.

The Regions details inherit information from your product’s Store Settings. You can customize the regions on an offer in the Regions section of the Offer Details panel.

Note:

  • Product age ratings are related to regions, but you can only set age ratings on a product level, not an offer level.
  • Mobile Early Adopter program only:
    • When you set regions details for an offer, this applies to all the platforms supported by the offer: PC/Mac (for Windows and/or macOS), iOS, or Android. Regions details do not apply to other offers on the same product.
    • If you want to set the regional availability for the entire product across all platforms, use the product's store settings. For information, see documentation: Configure Store Settings.
