Manage Pages

Create and manage your product's storefront pages.

14 mins to read

On the Epic Games Store, your product's store presence consists of the following pages. All of these pages can feature visual media and written content that describe your product to Epic Games Store users.

  • The main page for your product (called the product home page):
    • When you create a new product, configure the product home page. See the Configure the Product Home Page section on this page.
  • A page for each offer (called an offer page):
    • When you add an offer to your product, such an edition, demo, or season pass, configure an offer page. See the Configure and Publish a Page for an Offer section on this page.
  • An FAQ page:
    • You can create an FAQ page to answer questions that you anticipate about your product. This is usually optional, but In some scenarios you must create an FAQ page. See the Create an FAQ page section on this page.

Note: To help your product reach a wide audience of global users, the Epic Games Store supports localization for over 15 languages. Documentation: Localize Your Store Presence.

Configure the Product Home Page

The product home page is the first page that users see when they view your product on the Epic Games Store. Use this page to engage users and highlight the various elements of your product.

When you create your product, the Developer Portal (Dev Portal) automatically creates a product home page. You can edit this page to tailor the page to your product.

To configure your product home page:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Pages page. To do this:

    • (a) Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

    • (b) Select the correct organization for your product.

      (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the onscreen instructions.)

    • (c) In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.

    • (d) In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Pages.

  2. Select the Dev sandbox.
  3. In the list, click Product home.
  4. Provide the information that you want to display on the product home page.
    • For detailed information about the settings available for a page, see the section Page Details on this page.
    • Your product home page also displays information about your product's age ratings. For information on how to edit your product's ratings, see documentation: Ratings Overview.

Configure and Publish a Page for an Offer

When you create an offer for your product, the Developer Portal automatically creates a page to display that offer to Epic Games Store users. This page is called an "offer page". The offer page provides Epic Games Store users with tailored information about each offer available. You can edit the page to suit your needs.

You can clone (copy) assets from one page to other pages in the same product. For more information, see Clone Assets Between Pages.

Edit an Offer Page

To edit an offer page:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Pages page. To do this:

    • (a) Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

    • (b) Select the correct organization for your product.

      (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the onscreen instructions.)

    • (c) In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.

    • (d) In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Pages.

  2. Select the Dev sandbox.
  3. In the list of pages, click the page you want to edit.
  4. Provide the information that you want to display on the offer page.
    • For detailed information about the settings available for a page, see the Page Details section on this page.

When you have configured the page to your liking, you can clone (copy) assets from it to other pages in the same product. For more information see Clone Assets Between Pages.

Create a Page for an Edition-Type Offer

Note: This section applies only for edition-type offers that you created before March 28, 2023.

When you create an edition-type offer, the Developer Portal automatically creates a page for the edition. However, if you created your edition-type offer before March 28, 2023, then the Developer Portal did not automatically create a page for the edition. In this situation, you can manually create a page for the edition.

To manually create a page for an edition:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Pages page. To do this:

    • (a) Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

    • (b) Select the correct organization for your product.

      (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the onscreen instructions.)

    • (c) In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.

    • (d) In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Pages.

  2. Select the Dev sandbox.
  3. Click Create page.
  4. Select Edition.
    • Note: This option is only available if you have one or more edition-type offers without a page.
  5. In the list, select one of your available edition-type offers.
    • Note: Only Edition offers without a dedicated page appear in this list.
  6. Click Create.
  7. Provide the information that you want to display on the edition offer's page.
    • For detailed information about the settings available for a page, see the Page Details section on this page.

Publish an Offer Page

The process to publish an offer page is as follows:

  1. Push the page to the Stage sandbox.
  2. Submit the Product for Review.
  3. Push the page to the Live sandbox.

For more information on this process, see Publishing Tools Workflow.

Note: the page might not be visible on the storefront after this process, depending on the dates you have set on the offer that the page relates to.

After you push the page to the product's Live sandbox, it has one of the statuses described in the Publishing Statuses of Offer Pages section on this page.

Publishing Statuses of Offer Pages

An offer page that you push to your product's Live sandbox can have one of the following statuses:

  • Enabled for early store discovery
  • Pushed to Live sandbox but not yet available to users
  • Released and available for purchase

The status is determined by the dates that you set for the offer. For more information about dates on offers, see documentation: Offer Details > Dates.

Enabled for Early Store Discovery

This status means that the offer page is available before the release of your product.

Publish your offer pages as early as possible to get the following benefits:

  • Generate user interest in your product prior to release.
  • Boost awareness of your product’s availability on the Epic Games Store.
  • Users can add your product to their wishlist and receive notifications about your product.

To publish an offer page before the release of your product, set a viewable date on the offer. Documentation: Offer Details > Dates.

If you set a viewable date on an offer, your product gets the following benefits:

  • The offer page has Coming Soon text in place of its Buy Now button.
  • The offer page can display an estimated, specific, or unknown available date to users.
  • The product is included in the Coming Soon filter on the Browse page of the Epic Games Store.

Note: If you set a viewable date on your offer, the Epic Games Store automatically associates the offer with an automatically-generated artifact. You do not need to attach a binary to that artifact to publish the offer page as Coming Soon. Documentation: Manage Artifacts.

Pushed to Live Sandbox, Not Yet Available

This status happens when both of the following conditions apply:

  • The offer has an available date set in the future.
  • You push the offer from the Stage sandbox to the Live sandbox.

This status means that the page is not yet available to Epic Games Store users. However, it can be indexed on the public internet and is therefore public information.

If you don’t want the page to be public information before launch, wait until immediately before your offer's available date and time before you push the page to your Live sandbox. Documentation: Publishing Tools Workflow

Released and Available for Purchase

This status means that the offer page is only visible to Epic Games Store users when the product is released. The offer appears on the page as available to purchase.

An offer page has this status when both of the following conditions apply:

  • The offer's available date is the same as the product's available date.
  • You do not enable early store discovery.

Clone Assets Between Pages

You can clone (copy) assets between pages in the same product. This saves time, because you don't need to upload the same assets on two or more pages. Instead, you can upload the assets for one page and then clone them to other pages.

When you clone assets, you choose the source page (the page that the details are cloned from) and one or more destination pages (the page or pages that the details are cloned to). The source page and destination pages must be in the same product. The Developer Portal replaces the assets in the destination pages with the assets from the source page.

You can clone the following asset types between pages:

  • Images from the carousel: see Carousel on this page.
  • Images from the about section: see About on this page.
  • Additional images: see Additional Images on this page.
  • Social Preview images: see Social Preview on this page.

You don't have to clone all of these asset types. You can choose a selection of asset types to clone to the destination pages.

Example:
If you choose to clone carousel and additional images, then the Developer Portal replaces the carousel images and additional images in the destination pages with the carousel images and additional images in the source offer. It doesn't replace the images from the about section or the social preview images.

Note:

  • To clone assets between pages, you need at least two pages in your product, because you must select a source page and at least one destination page.
  • The clone operation is a one-time copy. In other words, after you clone assets, the source and destination pages do not stay synchronized. When you make changes to the source page, the destination pages do not automatically change.
  • You can only select source pages and destination pages in the Dev sandbox.
  • For the source page, you can only select a page that has the Ready for Stage status.
  • Depending on the settings in your selected source page and destination pages, you might not be able to clone your selected assets. In this situation, the Developer Portal displays a message that explains why the clone operation is not possible.

To clone assets between pages:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Pages page. To do this:

    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. Select the correct organization for your product.
    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.
    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Pages.

  2. Select the Dev sandbox.

  3. At the top right, click Clone content.

  4. Select the source page. This is the offer you want to copy the details from.

    • Use the Search page box to find pages by page name.

  5. Click Next.

  6. In DESTINATION PAGES, select the page, or pages, you want to clone to. You can select more than one page.

    • To help you find specific pages in the list, you can use the following tools:
      • Use Filter by page type to only show pages of specific types.
      • Use the Search page box to find pages by page name.

  7. For each destination page you select, choose the asset types (media carousel, about, additional images, or social preview) you want to copy to the destination page or pages. By default, all asset types are selected.

    • Note: The DESTINATION PAGE PREVIEW shows the effect that the clone operation will have on the selected page, based on the source page and the asset types you have selected. The preview shows the most important details of the page, including the details that will be changed by the clone operation.

      • Removed text is struck through:

      • Added text is shown in green:

      • Removed images have a red X icon:

      • Added images have a green check icon:

      • If you select more than one destination page, the preview displays one destination page at a time. To cycle through each preview, use the left and right arrows at the top right of the preview:

  8. At the top right of the page, click Clone content.

Page Details

This section describes the details you can set for product home pages and offer pages. The details in this section do not apply to FAQ pages.

The page details panel opens when you edit a page. (For information on how to edit a page, see the Configure the Product Home Page and Edit an Offer Page sections on this page.) In the page details panel, click the category that you want to edit, such as Carousel or Social preview. In the panel, mandatory details are marked with an asterisk (*). Different details are mandatory or optional depending on the type of page.

Note: When you edit the details of a page, you can view a preview of the page as it will appear on the Epic Games Store. To do so, click Preview in Store.

Carousel

The carousel is an area at the top of the page that shows a series of assets such as videos, screenshots, and key art in sequence, one after the other. These assets are the first items that users engage with when visiting your store presence.

For maximum effect, use the carousel to highlight not only the art style of your product, but also the features and experiences that users can expect. To best reach potential users, we recommend that you include at least one video.

For asset specifications and other guidelines, see documentation: Storefront Media Guide > Media Carousel.

You can clone (copy) assets from one page to other pages in the same product. For more information, see Clone Assets Between Pages.

Videos and Closed Captioning in the Carousel

When you upload a video asset to the carousel, you can also upload a closed caption file to support accessibility for your video. Closed captions are optional.

  • Submit closed captions as a .VTT file.
  • Closed caption files cannot exceed 4 MB.

About

In the About section of your page, you can add a detailed description of your product or offer. This allows you to provide much more detail than in the offer's short description.

You can write up to 12 blocks of content. Each block can include text or an image. You must include at least one text block.

For information on the specifications for assets, and other guidelines related to pages, see documentation: Storefront Media Guide > About Section.

You can clone (copy) assets from one page to other pages in the same product. For more information, see Clone Assets Between Pages.

Additional Images

Use Additional Images to add images to the bottom of your product or offer page. For example, you might add gameplay screenshots, artwork, or other marketing materials.

Additional Images are optional, but we recommend that you add at least 1-6 images.

For information on the specifications for assets, and other guidelines related to pages, see documentation: Storefront Media Guide > About Section Additional Images.

You can clone (copy) assets from one page to other pages in the same product. For more information, see Clone Assets Between Pages.

Social Preview

The Social Preview option allows you to upload assets for use with social media posts. Social Preview assets are not displayed directly on the storefront and are instead used by third parties when linking to social media posts.

For asset specifications and other guidelines, see documentation: Storefront Media Guide > Social Preview.

You can clone (copy) assets from one page to other pages in the same product. For more information, see Clone Assets Between Pages.

Create an FAQ Page

Use your product's FAQ (frequently asked questions) page to provide additional information to users and potential users of your product. You can only create one FAQ page for your product, but you can include multiple questions and answers on it.

Common details included on FAQ pages include the following:

  • Responses to commonly asked user questions and concerns.
  • Ongoing promotions.
  • Details about closed access phases.
  • Opportunities to provide feedback about your product.
  • The state of your product and what users can expect in future updates.
  • Information about core features that require additional steps, such as online play or in-app purchases.

Note: FAQ pages are not mandatory for mobile (iOS, Android) products on the Epic Games Store.

For some types of product, an FAQ page is mandatory. For more information, see the FAQ requirements section on this page.

Configure Your FAQ Page

To create or edit an FAQ page for your product:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Pages page. To do this:

    • (a) Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

    • (b) Select the correct organization for your product.

      (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the onscreen instructions.)

    • (c) In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.

    • (d) In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Pages.

  2. Select the Dev sandbox.
  3. If your product does not have an FAQ page yet:
    • (a) Click Create Page.
    • (b) Select FAQ page.
    • (c) Click Create.
  4. If your product already has an FAQ page:
    • In the list of pages, click FAQ page.
  5. Click Add Question.
  6. In Question and Answer, enter the desired question and answer text.
  7. Repeat steps 5-6 to add additional questions and answers to your page.
  8. (Optional) When your page has two or more questions, you can reorder the list of questions. You can use the following methods to reorganize the list:
    • (a) Click the grip icon to the left of a question and drag the question and answer upwards or downwards in the list of questions.
    • (b) Use the arrow icons to move individual questions and answers up or down in the list of questions.
  9. (Optional) Use the delete icon to the right of a question to delete that question and answer.
  10. Click Save Changes.

Which Products Require an FAQ Page?

In the following scenarios, you must create an FAQ page for your product:

  • If you plan to release your product as an early access title.
  • If your product includes blockchain technology.
  • If your product includes in-app purchases.

In these scenarios, an FAQ page improves transparency and helps set users’ expectations for your product.

More information about specific FAQ requirements:

  • Documentation: Early Access Program Requirements
  • Documentation: Blockchain Technology Guidelines
  • Documentation: Epic Games Store Requirements > In-App Purchases
