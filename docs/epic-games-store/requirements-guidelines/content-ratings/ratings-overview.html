Ratings on the Epic Games Store

Rating options and requirements for the Epic Games Store.

6 mins to read

Overview

On the Epic Games Store, age ratings determine which regions you can and cannot distribute your products in. Some regions require region-specific ratings (for example, South Korea requires GRAC ratings), while other regions require International Age Rating Coalition (IARC) ratings. Other regions do not require age ratings at all. In addition, age ratings determine which users can see your product's product home page on the Epic Games Store.

You can choose to leave your product unrated, but doing so restricts your product's distribution regions and limits the visibility of your product on the storefront. See the Unrated Products section of this page for more information.

Because of these restrictions, we highly recommend obtaining age ratings for your product. Age ratings allow you to distribute your product in more regions and make your product more accessible to users on the storefront. You can obtain an IARC rating for your product using the Developer Portal.

For more information on obtaining an IARC rating for your product, see the documentation: Manage Regions and Ratings > Obtain a New IARC Rating.

Ratings Requirements

Products published on the Epic Games Store require ratings in the following circumstances:

  • If you distribute products in one of the following countries, you must get IARC or region-specific ratings. See the IARC Ratings section of this page for more information.
    • Russia
    • Brazil
    • Germany
    • Australia
  • If you distribute products in South Korea, you must get a GRAC (Game Rating and Administration Committee) rating. This requirement applies to any playable builds that are available to the public, including demo and early access builds.
    • See the GRAC Ratings section of this page for more information.
  • If your product has received ratings from any official rating authority, you must display those ratings on the product home page in the storefront.
    • See the Traditional Ratings section of this page for more information.
  • If your product leverages blockchain or NFT technology in any capacity, your product must be rated to be distributed on the Epic Games Store.
    • Documentation: Blockchain Technology Guidelines.

Adults Only Ratings

Products with Adults Only (AO) ratings cannot be distributed on the Epic Games Store.

The Epic Games Store makes an exception for products in cases where an AO rating was applied solely due to the usage of blockchain or NFT technology. However, these products must comply with our Blockchain Technology Guidelines and Content Requirements, including our prohibitions on pornography, gambling, and hateful content.

IARC Ratings

The International Age Rating Coalition (IARC) provides a globally streamlined age classification process for digital products. This process helps provide users with consistent access to established and trusted ratings across digital storefronts and platforms.

If you want to distribute your product in one of the following regions, your product must have IARC ratings or region-specific ratings:

  • Russia
  • Brazil
  • Germany
  • Australia

The Epic Games Store welcomes and encourages partners to obtain IARC ratings for the products they distribute on the storefront.

Documentation: IARC Ratings

GRAC Ratings

GRAC (Game Rating and Administration Committee) ratings are legally required to distribute products in South Korea for any playable builds (including demo and early-access builds) that are available to the public.

To ensure a successful release in South Korea, allow enough lead time to acquire a GRAC rating for your product before its release. As a general guideline, non-mature (age 15 and under) titles require at least one week of lead time. Mature (age 18+) titles require at least six weeks.

The process to obtain a GRAC rating varies between non-mature and mature titles. The distinction between these categories follows the GRAC guidelines.

Documentation: Distribution in Korea

Traditional Ratings

If you have obtained ratings for your product from any official rating authority, you must display that information on your product home page in the storefront. Uploading these ratings is required regardless of whether you opt in or out of using IARC ratings. Traditional ratings must be for a platform that Epic Games supports (PC: Windows, macOS, Linux. Mobile: iOS, Android). However, if you have a GRAC certificate for a platform that Epic Games does not support, you can use that certificate on the Epic Games Store.

Content Descriptors and Interactive Elements

In addition to the official rating classification, some traditional rating authorities also require descriptors, interactive element disclosures, or both. Descriptors and interactive elements indicate to users why your product received its specific rating. If your rating certificate includes any descriptors or interactive elements, you must provide this information along with your rating classification.

To upload traditional ratings or edit content descriptors for your traditional ratings, see the documentation: Manage Regions and Ratings > Manage Traditional Ratings.

Unrated Products

If your product is unrated, you can only distribute your product in regions without specific rating requirements. You cannot distribute an unrated product in any region that requires a rating. In addition, unrated products are more difficult for users to access on the Epic Games Store, even in regions that do not require ratings.

See the Ratings Requirements section of this page for a list of regions and situations where age ratings are required.

If your product is unrated, your product home page is restricted in the following ways:

  • In countries where ratings are required for distribution, your product home page is blocked. Users cannot see your product home page in these regions.
  • In all other regions, your product defaults to the highest age rating available. This can have the following impact:
    • Users who are not logged in to the Epic Games Store are prompted to enter their birthday when they attempt to view your product home page. If the user's birthday is below a certain threshold, they cannot view your product home page.
    • Users who are logged in to the Epic Games Store and have parental controls set might not be allowed to view your product home page.
      • Documentation: Parental Controls.
    • Adult users who are logged in to the Epic Games Store can only view your product home page after viewing an unrated product warning.

To promote a positive player experience, we highly recommend that you obtain ratings for your product. If you do not have ratings for your product, you can obtain an IARC rating through the Epic Games Store publishing tools.

For more information on obtaining an IARC rating for your product, see the documentation: Manage Regions and Ratings > Obtain a New IARC Rating.
