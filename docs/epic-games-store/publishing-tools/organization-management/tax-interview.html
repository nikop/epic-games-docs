Complete the Tax Interview

Set up a tax profile for your organization on the Epic Games Store.

4 mins to read

The second step in the financial onboarding process is the tax interview. In the tax interview, you answer a series of questions to gather the necessary information for your tax identity, known as your "tax profile". Epic Games uses this tax profile to generate a Form W-9 (for partners in the United States) or a Form W-8 (for partners outside the United States), and determine whether U.S. tax withholdings need to be applied to your payments.

After you receive your W-9 or W-8 form, you can complete the final step of the financial onboarding process: submitting your payout information. See the Next Steps section of this page for more information.

When you complete the tax interview and submit your tax profile, Epic Games will attempt to verify your tax profile. Verification can take up to five business days, depending on your country of residence. Epic Games will email you when your tax information is verified, with a link to return you to the Developer Portal so you can complete the financial enrollment process.

Prerequisites

  • Complete the trader verification process.
    • Documentation: Trader Verification.
  • Access to one of the following:
    • Social Security number (United States)
    • National ID number
    • Tax ID

Start the Tax Interview

Begin the tax interview process from your organization's dashboard in the Developer Portal.

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to your Organization page. To do this:
    1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    2. Select the correct organization for your product.
    3. In the left navigation, click Organization.
  2. In your Organization page, click Tax Info. The Your Tax Details page displays.
    • Note: If you have not completed trader verification, you might not be able to start the tax interview. See the Trader Verification documentation for detailed instructions on completing trader verification.
  3. In the START TAX INTERVIEW section, select the type of tax profile you want to create:
    • Personal: Select this option to create an individual tax profile. You need your personal Social Security number (SSN), or the equivalent number for tax reporting in your country of residence.
    • Sole Proprietor: Select this option if you have an unincorporated business that you own and operate alone.
    • Single-Member LLC: Select this option if you have a Limited Liability Company (LLC) in the United States. To set up a single-member LLC tax profile, you must have already set up your LLC and you must be the only owner or partner in the company.
      • Note: Do not select this option if you live outside of the United States.
    • Business: Select this option if you have a business with one or more business partners. You must provide a separate company email address if you select this option.
  4. If you selected Business, enter your company email address in Email address and your company name in Company Name. Your company email address must be different from the organization owner's email address.
    • All other tax profile types use the organization owner's email address as the contact email address. Epic Games uses this email address to contact you about the status of your tax verification and provide you with the link to proceed with the financial onboarding process.
  5. Click Start Tax Interview. The tax interview displays in the Developer Portal window, starting with the What to Expect page.

Provide Tax Interview Information

After you select the type of tax profile you want to set up, the tax interview begins in the Developer Portal. Complete the following steps to submit your tax interview and create your tax profile.

You can click Help at any time during the tax interview to display extra information about the page that you are on. Some fields also display more information about the field if you hover over them.

  1. Read the information in the What to Expect page. This page gives you instructions and tips for completing the tax interview successfully.
  2. Click Continue.
  3. In the Profile page, fill out the required information.
  4. Click Continue when you have filled out all the required fields.
  5. In the Consents page, read the instructions carefully. Click Yes to consent, or click No to decline consent.
  6. If you declined consent to any of the items in the Consents page, read the provided stipulations and make sure you can provide the required information.
  7. Click Continue.
  8. Enter the email address you used in the Start the Tax Interview section of this page.
    • If you selected Business, enter the company email address you provided.
    • If you selected an option other than Business, enter the email address of the organization owner.
  9. Sign and submit your tax interview.

Next Steps

After you submit your tax interview, the tax profile verification process begins. You will receive tax profile emails at the email address you provided. These emails might include requests for more information or requests for supporting documentation.

If you encounter issues during the tax interview or the verification process, contact Epic Games Support for help. To do this, create a private discussion on the support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com. Under Topic, select Epic Games Store.

After Epic Games has successfully verified your tax profile, you will receive instructions for completing the financial onboarding process at the email address you provided.
