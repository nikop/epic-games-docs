Storefront Media Guide

Specifications and recommendations for storefront media assets.

12 mins to read

Mobile Early Adopter: Do not use this page. Go to the Mobile Early Adopter section for guidance.

You can display a variety of media assets relating to your product to Epic Games Store users. This page provides recommendations and specifications for each of the assets, as well as some best practices and examples of each asset's placement as storefront media. We recommend that you follow these recommendations to ensure an optimal user experience.

Content Requirements

There are some content requirements that your product must adhere to. During the review of your product prior to release or release update, the Epic Games Store team evaluates media assets to ensure they meet these requirements.

For a list of these requirements, see the following documentation:

  • Content Requirements > Media Asset Policies
  • Brand Guidelines Overview

Localization

The Epic Games Store supports language localization of both text and media assets. Only English is a required language. However, by uploading localized assets to your offers, you can tailor the store experience for global users by presenting them with images and videos in their preferred language.

For more information, see the following documentation:

  • Localize Your Storefront Assets (Images and Video)
  • Content Requirements > Localization Policies
  • Best Practices > Localization

About This Page

This page provides the following guidance:

  • Asset Recommendations and Specifications — Guidance on required and optional assets for your product's storefront media.
  • Storefront Media Placement Examples — Examples of media assets in the Epic Games Store website and Epic Games Launcher.
  • Best Practices — Advice on how to implement media assets on the store.

Asset Recommendations and Specifications

You can display all of the assets listed below; however, not all of the assets are required. The assets marked with an asterisk (*) are required.

  • As part of your product's store settings:
    • Product Logo*
  • On your product's offer page:
    • Offer Images*
  • On your product home page (also known as the “product home page” and “product home”):
    • Media Carousel*
    • About Section*
    • About Section Additional Images
    • Social Preview

Product Logo

You must provide a product logo image as part of your product's store settings. (Documentation: Configure Store Settings)

Your product logo displays in various locations throughout the storefront, such as at the top of your product home page.

Requirements

  • Your product logo image must meet the requirements of the media asset policies. (Documentation: Content Requirements > Media Asset Policies).
  • Product logo images must adhere to the file specifications listed below under Product Logo Image File Specifications.

How to Upload Your Product Logo Image to the Store

You upload your product logo image in the Developer Portal. To do this, follow these steps:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. In the left navigation, select your product under Your products.
  3. Click Epic Games Store, then select Store Settings.
  4. Under Product configuration, click Edit.
  5. On the Product Configuration panel, in the Required Information section, click Product details and follow the onscreen instructions.

Offer Images

You must provide images as part of your product's offers. You can upload multiple images for each offer you create. (Documentation: Manage Offers)

Requirements

  • Offer images must meet the requirements of the media asset policies. (Documentation: Content Requirements > Media Asset Policies.)
  • You must upload at least one landscape image and one portrait image for each of your offers.
    • Landscape offer images display on your product home page, below the base product offer, where additional offers are listed, in areas such as the editions section and add-on section. Landscape offer images are also the product logo image for edition offers and add-on offers.
  • Portrait offer images display in areas such as the checkout flow and wishlist notifications.
  • Offer images must adhere to the file specifications listed below under Offer Image File Specifications.

Offer General Images and Offer Library Images

There are two types of offer images:

  • Offer general images — These represent your offer throughout the store.
  • Offer library images — Users who have purchased your offer (or received it as a gift) see these images in the library section of the Epic Games Launcher.

For all offer types, you must add at least one general image in landscape and one general image in portrait.

You must also add at least one library image in landscape and one library image in portrait for any offer that can show in the Epic Games Store. This applies to the following offer types:

  • Base game (also known as the base product offer)
  • Demo offer
  • Digital extra
  • Edition
  • Add-on

Tip: You can add new library images or reuse (known as "copy" in the Developer Portal) general images to be library images.

How to Upload Offer Images to the Store

You upload offer images in the Developer Portal. To do this, follow these steps:

For base product offers, demo offers, and digital extras, you have the option to automatically copy your offer's general images to your offer's library images.

Media Carousel

You must provide assets for your product's media carousel. These assets can be either or both images and videos.

The media carousel (also known as “the carousel”) displays at the top of your product home page (also known as the “product home page” and “product home”) and on your offer pages. Media carousel assets are the primary visual assets that users see when they visit your store presence. Users scroll through the media carousel while they review your product.

Requirements

  • You must provide a minimum of one asset.
  • Media carousel assets must meet the requirements of the media asset policies. (Documentation: Content Requirements > Media Asset Policies)
  • Media carousel assets must adhere to the file specifications listed below under Media Carousel Assets File Specifications.

Best Practices

See the section of this page: Best Practices > Media Carousel.

How to Upload Your Media Carousel Assets to the Store

You upload your media carousel assets in the Developer Portal. To do this, follow these steps:

  • Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  • In the left navigation, select your product under Your products.
  • Click Epic Games Store, then select Pages.
  • On the Pages page, select Product home or any offer. (Note that you might have to scroll down the page.)
  • On the Offer panel, in the Required Information section, click Media carousel and follow the onscreen instructions.

Tip: After you have uploaded assets, you can drag each asset in the list to define the order that the carousel displays them to users.

About Section

You must provide text and images for the About section.

Use the About section to show images relating to your product and describe your product to users. The text limit is 10,000 characters or 30 images. Note that images count towards the character limit but you can upload optional additional images. Documentation: About Section Additional Images.

Requirements

  • You must provide a block of text and a minimum of one asset.
  • About section images must meet the requirements of the media asset policies. Documentation: Content Requirements > Media Asset Policies.
  • About section images must adhere to the file specifications listed below under About Section Image File Specifications.

How to Add Text and Upload an Image to the About Section of the Store

You add text and images to the About section in the Developer Portal. To do this, follow these steps:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. In the left navigation, select your product under Your products.
  3. Click Epic Games Store, then select Pages.
  4. On the Pages page, select Product home or any offer. (Note that you might have to scroll down the page.)
  5. On the Offers panel, in the Required Information section, click About and follow the onscreen instructions.

About Section Image File Specifications

Specification Requirement
Dimensions At least 700 px wide
File Size (Max) 4 MB
File Type .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .gif

About Section Additional Images

The About section's additional images are optional. You can add up to 20 images.

On the store, additional images display below the About section of your product home page. They display in a grid. The size and layout of the grid depend on the number of images.

Tip: By default, additional images are collapsed when users visit your product home page. To ensure users see these images when they first visit your product home page, upload them to the media carousel. (Documentation: Storefront Media Guide > Media Carousel).

Requirements

  • About section additional images must meet the requirements of the media asset policies. (Documentation: Content Requirements > Media Asset Policies)
  • About section additional images must adhere to the file specifications listed below under About Section Additional Image File Specifications.

How to Add Additional Images to the About Section of the Store

You add additional images in the Developer Portal. To do this, follow these steps:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. In the left navigation, select your product under Your products.
  3. Click Epic Games Store, then select Pages.
  4. On the Pages page, select Product home or any offer. (Note that you might have to scroll down the page.)
  5. On the Offers panel, in the Optional Information section, click Additional images and follow the onscreen instructions.

Tip: After you have uploaded and saved an image, click Preview in Store (next to the panel) to see what it will look like on the store.

Best Practices

The following is a list of best practices for your media assets. See also the following documentation:

  • For requirements and policies: Content Requirements > Media Asset Policies
  • For branding and marketing information: Brand Guidelines Overview

Additional Offer Names

For additional offers, we recommend that you include the name of the base product offer in the offer name.

This not only makes your additional offer more searchable, it also makes it distinguishable from unrelated offers.

For example, for a Fortnite additional offer, use any of the following conventions:

  • Demo:
    • Fortnite - Demo
    • Fortnite Demo
  • Soundtrack:
    • Soundtrack for Fortnite
    • Fornite's Original Soundtrack
  • Content pack:
    • Fortnite Content Pack

Offer Images

For additional offers images, including general images and library images, we recommend that you display the offer type as part of the image.

This makes what your additional offer is clearer to users, increases its visibility, and distinguishes it from the base product offer.

For example:

  • A demo offer has the text “Demo” on the offer's images.
  • An edition offer has the text “Edition” on the offer's images.
  • An add-on has the text “Add-on” on the offer's images.

Media Carousel

For an optimal user experience and to maximize the impact of your product's media carousel, we recommend the following practices:

  • Upload a minimum of five assets to the media carousel, including at least one video.
  • Update your media carousel assets periodically, especially when you release new product updates.
  • Minimize the text on your media carousel assets to appeal to users across all regions who have differing languages.
  • Order assets on your media carousel so that key images display first. For example, set up images that promote a beta, early access, or a special offer to display first.
  • Include product screenshots in your media carousel. Statistics show that a media carousel with no product screenshots negatively impacts a product's rate of purchase.
  • Include at least one video in your media carousel.
    • We highly recommend this because Epic Games Store users often use videos to guide their purchasing decisions. Statistics show that a media carousel with no video asset negatively impacts a product's rate of purchase.
    • Order your media carousel assets to display video assets first.
    • You must include a legal notice with your video. (Documentation: Content Requirements > Media Legal Requirements)

Localization

We strongly recommend that you localize your product's store pages to provide the best experience for users globally. To reduce localization lead time, consider supporting some popular languages when you first release your product, and then add additional language support later.

The languages we recommend when you first release your product are as follows:

  • English
  • French
  • German
  • Russian
  • Spanish

If you need localization assistance, we can help. Documentation: Localize Your Store Presence.
