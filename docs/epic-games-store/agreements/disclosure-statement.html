Disclosure Statement

This disclosure statement discusses ranking practices, data access policies, and EU Platform to Business Regulation complaint handling procedures of the Epic Games Store.

Ranking

To help our Users more easily navigate the Epic Games Store to find products that are interesting and relevant, we sometimes organize products for display to users. We weigh a variety of parameters that inform the display order of Products to Users in the following contexts:

a. Algorithmic Featuring. When Epic features Products algorithmically (see Section (c) below), the main parameters that factor into its featuring include:

  • User-applied filters (like price, relevant promotions, Product genre, Product features, platforms, alphabetical order, and Product types)
  • User text search terms
  • Product page views
  • Wishlists
  • Active users
  • Entitlements
  • Number of downloads
  • Revenue
  • Playtime
  • User-submitted ratings
  • Product rating
  • User age
  • User region language preferences
  • When a product was last updated
  • Whether a product can be acquired for free or is acquired by purchase
  • Whether a product is a demo/beta or is a full release
  • Product release date
  • Product discounts and promotions
  • Whether a product has achievements

b. Editorial Featuring. Epic Games uses its editorial and business judgment to decide and periodically update its decisions to feature a mix of first-party Epic Games published Products and third-party published Products on the Epic Games Store. When Epic curates Products for editorial featuring, the main parameters that our editorial team focuses on include:

  • New releases on the Epic Games Store
  • Updates and new content for existing Products
  • Sales or active promotional offers
  • Whether a Product drives or is expected to drive User engagement
  • Whether a Product is published by Epic Games or its subsidiaries
  • Uniqueness
  • Accessibility
  • Innovation
  • Thoughtful or unique design
  • Compelling user interfaces
  • Appeal to current and prospective Users

c. For interactive entertainment Products, our editors additionally look for Products that have:

  • Replayability
  • Novel mechanics
  • Creative stories

d. Editorial featuring may vary by territory and language (and the availability of a Product in given territories and languages) and may consider regional Product ratings and actual or expected User engagement. Further, we periodically enter into negotiated agreements with third-parties which include terms related to their Products and terms such as revenue guarantees which may influence editorial featuring decisions.

e. Where Algorithmic Featuring and Editorial Featuring Are Used. On the Epic Games Store, User search results and the following lists: (i) Top Trending; (ii) Top Sellers; (iii) Top Upcoming Wishlisted; (iv) Top Player Rated; (v) Most Played; (vi) Last Month’s Top Sellers, (vii) Most Popular Free-to-Play; (viii) Recently Updated; (ix) New Releases: Add Ons; (x) Games on Sale; (xi) New To the Epic Games Store; (xii) New Releases; (xiii) Coming Soon; (xiv) Most Popular; (xv) Top Free-to-Play; (xvi) Top Selling Free-to-Play; (xvii) Most Popular Free-to-Play; (xviii) Most Played Free-to-Play; and (xix) Top New Releases are all exclusively algorithmically determined using the main parameters described above in Section (a) (Algorithmic Featuring). Epic Games may curate results on other surfaces on the Epic Games Store, like the main product carousel on the front page of the Epic Games Store, promotional Product Blades, Breakers, and landing pages using the editorial featuring parameters described above in Section (b) (Editorial Featuring). On the Epic Games Store, a "Blade" is a horizontally organized grouping of Products with Product images in a portrait orientation, a "Breaker" is a horizontally organized grouping of Products with Product images in a landscape orientation, and a “landing page” is a secondary page apart from the homepage used to promote sales, discounted content, events, apps, or free games.

f. Advertising. At this time, we do not permit developers to pay Epic for advertisements to increase the visibility of their Products in the Epic Games Store.

Data Access

You may access data about your Product’s sales and performance by logging into the Developer Portal and from the dashboard display clicking the “Reports” tab. From there, you can access sales reports including date of purchase, country code of purchases, product SKU of purchases, units purchased, refunds, chargebacks, net units, and net sales. From the Developer Portal dashboard, you may also review and generate payout reports about revenue earned and paid on given titles. To see payout reports, from the dashboard view of your Developer Portal, click the “Payout” tab. Sales reports and payout reports may be downloaded from the Developer Portal for recordkeeping. In addition to sales data and payout reports, your Developer Portal also lets you view tracked marketing campaigns, conversion rates, and user retention in your products on the Epic Games Store. Other than the aggregate information described below, Epic does not provide access to personal or other data generated by other third-party developers through their use of the Epic Games Store to you. Other than the aggregate information described below, Epic does not provide access to personal or other data you generate through your use of the Epic Games Store to other third-party developers on the Epic Games Store. Epic does provide access to users of the Epic Games Store to certain aggregated information, like a Product's presence in categories on the Epic Games Store (like "Top Sellers," "Most Played," and "Top Upcoming Wishlisted") and may use aggregated information in creating public-facing content, like its "Year in Review" (e.g., https://store.epicgames.com/en-US/news/epic-games-store-2021-year-in-review?lang=en-US). For information on how Epic handles user information, including its Users and business users on the Epic Games Store, see our privacy policy, available at https://www.epicgames.com/site/en-US/privacypolicy.

Complaints Handling and ADR

Under the EU Platform to Business Regulation 2019/1150, and as retained in United Kingdom law ("P2B Regulation"), if you are established in the European Union / United Kingdom and sell to Users in the European Union / United Kingdom on the Epic Games Store, you can submit complaints to us at P2BComplaints@epicgames.com with the subject “EGS P2B Complaint” about:

  • Epic’s alleged non-compliance with the P2B Regulation that affects you
  • Technological issues directly related to Epic’s provision of the Epic Games Store that affect you
  • Epic’s behavior or measures taken by Epic which relate directly to Epic’s provision of the Epic Games Store and that have affected you.

While we hope we’re able to resolve complaints via the complaint-handling system above, the P2B Regulation also allows you to pursue mediation as a means of resolving any disputes with us, including complaints that could not be resolved by means of our internal complaint-handling system. The P2B Regulation requires that each of us engage in any mediation in good faith.

If you would like to mediate a claim, we would be willing to try and reach an agreement by mediation administered by a panel of at least 2 mediators from the International Centre for Dispute Resolution. For more information on the International Centre for Dispute Resolution, please see: https://www.icdr.org/about_icdr.
