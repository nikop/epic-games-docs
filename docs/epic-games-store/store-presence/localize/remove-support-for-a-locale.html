Remove Support for a Locale (Language)

Remove a locale from your storefront presence.

Under 1 min to read

You can remove a locale (language) from your product. This replaces the localized images and text with default images and English text.

Warning: The following procedure deletes all text and media asset localizations for the selected language. You cannot undo this deletion.

To remove support for a locale:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Localization page. To do this:

    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

    • b. Select the correct organization for your product.

      (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the on-screen instructions.)

    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.

    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Localization.

  2. Click the name of a page, offer, or set of store settings.
  3. In the panel to the right, find the language you want to remove.
  4. Click Delete:
