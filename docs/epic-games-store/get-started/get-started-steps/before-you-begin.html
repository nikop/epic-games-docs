About the Developer Portal

You are going to use the Epic Games Developer Portal to set up and manage your product (game or other software application). The Developer Portal is a browser-based tool you use to set up and configure your product on the Epic Games Store, but you can also use it to configure additional Epic services and tools.

Note: You do not need to configure any additional Epic services or tools to follow the Epic Games Store get started steps and add your product to the store.

Additional Epic Tools and Services

You can use the Developer Portal for the following purposes:

  • Set up Epic Online Services (EOS), for your product.
    Documentation: Epic Online Services (EOS) Overview.
  • Set up Kids Web Services (KWS) for your product.
    Documentation: Kids Web Services (KWS)
  • Set up Epic Tools (Unreal Engine, Twinmotion, or RealityScan) to configure other Epic Games features (for example, Royalties and Service Partners).
    Documentation: Developer Portal Introduction – information on the Developer Portal and links to Epic Tools documentation.

Support and What’s New

You can get help from Epic Games Support and keep up-to-date with what’s new (recent updates, known issues, and limitations) through the Epic Games Store developer documentation.

Limitations

The text below is a duplicate of the Limitations page. We strongly recommend that you review the limitations before you follow the Get Started process.

Epic Games is committed to building the Epic Games Store distribution toolset to fit the needs of our developer partners. We regularly ship new features and updates to existing functionality that benefit our partners and promote a quality store experience for users.

At this time, some types of products and features are not supported by the Epic Games Store publishing tools, or are request-only features. See the lists below for details.

Support and Feature Information

  • If you want to distribute your product onto the Epic Games Store but your product is affected by any of the situations listed below, contact Epic Games Support. To do this, create a private discussion on the support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com.
  • For the latest information on updates to supported products and features, see the Recent Updates page
Request-Only Features

The following features are only available on request to the Epic Games Store team. You cannot set them up yourself.

Enable Epic Games Store for Existing EOS Products

If you have an existing product that uses Epic Online Services (EOS) and want to use the Epic Games Store publishing tools with your product, submit a request to the Epic Games Store team to have your product enabled for publishing.

To submit this request, create a private discussion using this support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com.

Enable External Anti-Tamper Tools

Your product can use external anti-tamper tools such as Denuvo (irdeto.com/denuvo).

To use them with your product, in your offer's Advanced Item Config setting, enable the option Denuvo Anti-Tamper Integration (documentation: Offer Details > Advanced Item Config).

Note: To use Advanced Item Config, you must first enable it for your organization. To enable it, contact your release manager or create a private discussion on eoshelp.epicgames.com.

Enable Advanced Launcher or Build Configurations

To distribute products with the configurations listed below on the Epic Games Store, you must have Advanced Item Config access. (Documentation: Offer Details > Advanced Item Config.)

The following product configurations require Advanced Item Config access:

  • Products with their own launchers.
  • Products that download or update builds outside of the Epic Games Store.
  • Products requiring multiple processes to be launched from the Epic Games Launcher.

To request Advanced Item Config access, create a private discussion using this support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com.

Unsupported Product Types

At this time, the Epic Games Store publishing tools do not allow the following types of product:

  • Non-game products
  • VR-only products
  • Mod editors and mod kits
  • Product bundles (multiple different products sold together as a single offer)
  • Products with edition or add-on offers that have more mature ratings than the base product
  • Products that do not support mouse and keyboard controls and require a third-party device (for example, a webcam or joystick)
    • Note: The Epic Games Store allows products that support common game console controllers. However, if your product does not support mouse and keyboard controls, you must explain this in the following areas of your store presence:
      • In the banner (Documentation: Configure Store Settings > Banner).
      • In the Additional notes field of your product specifications (Documentation: Configure Store Settings > Specifications).
Unsupported Features

The Epic Games Store publishing tools currently do not support the following features:

  • Automatic bundle discounts for already-purchased offers.
  • Certain launcher options, such as:
    • Rolling release times. (For example, a product releases at midnight in each time zone.)
    • Enabling players to selectively install downloadable content (DLC) during base product installation.
  • Offers of specific builds for individual geographical locations.
  • Displays of custom end-user licensing agreements (EULAs) in the Epic Games Launcher.
  • Mod configuration and management.

Requirements and Guidelines

Before you use the Epic Games Store publishing tools, we strongly recommend that you review the requirements and guidelines for distributing products on the store. Reviewing these policies early in the process ensures you know the expectations and criteria that you and your product must meet in order to distribute your product on the store.

The text below is a summary of the essential information. There is more detail in the Requirements and Guidelines documentation.

Distribution Requirements

To promote a trustworthy and consistent experience for users, the Epic Games Store maintains a number of requirements for distribution. When your product is reviewed by the Epic Games Store team prior to launch, we will verify that your submission is complete and compliant with these policies.

Requirements for distribution include the following:

  • Products featuring multiplayer functionality must support crossplay across all PC storefronts where the product is distributed.
  • Products that offer achievements on other PC storefronts that sell 3rd-party products must have Epic Games Store achievements enabled.
  • Products with in-app purchases must disclose that information on their product home page in the storefront.
  • Products leveraging blockchain, NFT, or cryptocurrency technology (known as blockchain technology) must follow specific guidelines to provide transparency to users.

More information: Epic Games Store Requirements

Ratings

The Epic Games Store does not require products to be rated. However, ratings are required in the following circumstances:

  • Some regions require ratings from specific authorities in order to distribute products in those areas. Such regions include South Korea, Russia, Brazil, Germany, or Australia.
  • Products that have received ratings from any official rating authority must display that information on their product home page in the storefront.
  • Products leveraging blockchain or NFT technology in any capacity must be rated in order to be distributed on the Epic Games Store.

While you can distribute your product as an unrated title in regions without specific requirements, be aware that unrated products are treated as having the highest-level maturity rating for each region where they are distributed.

We highly recommend that you obtain ratings for your product to best reach potential users on the storefront. If you do not have ratings for your product, you can quickly obtain an IARC rating through the Epic Games Store publishing tools.

More information: Ratings on the Epic Games Store

Content Guidelines

The Epic Games Store maintains a set of content guidelines for products on the storefront. These guidelines cover a variety of topics, including:

  • Storefront content
  • Prohibited content
  • Linked-to and user-generated content
  • Localization

More information: Content Guidelines

System Requirements for the BuildPatch Tool

You will use the BuildPatch Tool to upload your product's files to the Epic Games Store.

For information about the system requirements to run the BuildPatch tool, see BuildPatch Tool Release Notes > Important Information.
