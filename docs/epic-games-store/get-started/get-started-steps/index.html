Get Started Steps Introduction

Overview of the Get Started process.
Before You Begin

Get ready for the Get Started process.
1. Onboarding

Get set up on the Epic Games Store.
2. Publish Your Product Home Page

Set up your product and publish a page about it on the Epic Games Store.
3. Finalize and Test Configurations

Make sure your product is ready for launch on the Epic Games Store.
4. Submit for Final Review

Before you release, submit your product for review by the Epic Games Store team.
5. Release Your Product

Release your product on the Epic Games Store.
