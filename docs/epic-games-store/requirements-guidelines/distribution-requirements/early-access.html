Early Access Program Requirements

Requirements for releasing Early Access products on the Epic Games Store.

2 mins to read

Mobile Early Adopter: If you are in the Mobile Early Adopter program, go to the Mobile Early Adopter—Requirements and Guidelines—Early Access Requirements documentation for guidance before you use this page.

You can make your product available to users in the Epic Games Store while it is still in development. This is known as Early Access.

Use Early Access to collect and incorporate user feedback, and evolve your product by focusing on the features and functionality that most interest your users. Making your product available for Early Access offers an iterative approach to building, testing, improving, and ultimately providing a complete, high-quality experience to users.

To make your product available for Early Access, follow the requirements below. After you have submitted your product for review, the Epic Games Store team confirms whether your product meets these requirements.

Early Access Requirements

To make your product available for Early Access, you must configure your product as listed below. The configuration provides information about your product's Early Access status to users. For example, if your Early Access product is under active development, you can provide this information so users can make an informed decision when purchasing it.

You must complete the following items to make your product available for Early Access:

  • Enable Early Access for Your Product.
  • Create an FAQ page.

Note: FAQ pages are not mandatory for mobile (iOS, Android) products on the Epic Games Store.

Enable Early Access for Your Product

When you configure an offer, you must enable the Early Access Product toggle. You do this on the Developer Portal's Offers page (see the documentation link below for guidance on how to access this page).

When you enable the Early Access Product toggle, the Epic Games Store adds the “Early Access" tag to your product's store presence, along with the Early Access Game disclaimer.

Documentation: Manage Offers > Configure Your Product's Base Product Offer

Create an FAQ Page

You must create an FAQ page that provides users with additional information about your Early Access product. This FAQ can inform users about the current state of your product, the types of updates and changes they can expect, and any opportunities to provide feedback.

Required Questions

You must answer the following questions on your FAQ page:

  • What is included in Early Access?
  • What is the current state of the product?
  • Will I have access to the product during the entire Early Access phase?

Additional Optional Questions

You can give users more insight into your product's Early Access details by answering additional questions in your FAQ. Answering more questions can improve your product's transparency for users and give you the opportunity to address common concerns.

The following questions are examples of useful additions to your FAQ page:

  • How long is the product in Early Access?
  • How often do you update the product?
  • Is my progress saved between updates?
  • Will the price change when the product leaves Early Access?
  • Can I provide feedback and bug reports?

Documentation: Manage Pages > Create an FAQ Page
