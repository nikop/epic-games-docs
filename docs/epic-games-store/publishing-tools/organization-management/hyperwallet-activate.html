Activate Your Hyperwallet Account for Payout

Set up and activate a Hyperwallet account for your organization to receive payouts.

4 mins to read

The third and final step in the financial onboarding process is activating your Hyperwallet account. Hyperwallet is a third-party payout application that distributes payouts from purchases of your products in local currencies. Hyperwallet collects and stores personal information in order to handle distribution of payouts on behalf of Epic Games.

Hyperwallet is not a digital wallet. No funds are held in your Hyperwallet account. Instead, your Hyperwallet account receives funds from Epic, then transfers your funds to an account you set up to receive the funds. For more information on Hyperwallet, go to the Hyperwallet homepage at pay.hyperwallet.com.

After your tax profile is created and successfully verified, your tax profile is sent to Hyperwallet. Hyperwallet then creates an account using the information in your tax profile (Documentation: Complete the Tax Interview). After Hyperwallet creates your account, you must activate the account and provide payout information before you can receive payments for your products.

Some organizations, such as those based in Turkey, do not need to create a Hyperwallet account. Instead, you can complete an alternate registration process to provide your payout account information. After completing the tax interview, go to the Payout Info tab in the Developer Portal to complete the alternate registration process.

Prerequisites

  • Complete the trader verification process.
    • Documentation: Trader Verification.
  • Complete the tax interview and create a tax profile.
    • Documentation: Complete the Tax Interview.

Access the Hyperwallet Activation Page

You activate your Hyperwallet account using Hyperwallet's activation page. To access the Hyperwallet activation page, click the link in the activation email you received after you completed the tax interview. Documentation: Complete the Tax Interview > Next Steps.

Alternatively, you can access the activation page using the Developer Portal. To do this:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to your organization's dashboard. To do this:
    • Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • Select the correct organization for your product.
    • In the left navigation, click Organization.
  2. In the organization dashboard, click Payout Info. The Payout Info page displays.
    • Note: You must complete the tax interview and create a tax profile before you can access the Payout Info page. If you have not created a tax profile, a Verified Tax Profile Required popup displays. Click Complete Your Tax Profile to take the tax interview before proceeding. Documentation: Complete the Tax Interview.
  3. Click Activate Hyperwallet Account to open the activation page on the Hyperwallet site.

Provide Account and Payout Information

Use the Hyperwallet activation page to provide Hyperwallet with your payout information and finish setting up your Hyperwallet account.

If you encounter any issues during this process, see the Epic Games Hyperwallet FAQ for more information on Hyperwallet and payouts. If you still have questions, contact Epic Games Support for help. To do this, create a private discussion on the support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com.

  1. When you first open the Hyperwallet activation page, the Account Profile form displays. Fill out the fields under the Personal Information header.
    • Note: Some fields are already populated with the information from your tax profile.
  2. (Optional) In Occupation, enter the description that is closest to your occupation.
  3. Click Submit when you have filled out all the required fields. The Activate Account form displays.
  4. Enter and confirm a password for your Hyperwallet account.
  5. Select two security questions and provide an answer to each question. You can use these security questions to access your account if you forget your password.
  6. Read through the provided legal agreements, then click Confirm to agree.
  7. Click Add Transfer Method. A transfer method lets you choose where you receive payouts and the currency of your payouts.
  8. Set a country or region for this transfer method.
  9. Set the currency type for this transfer method.
  10. Follow the on-screen instructions to provide any required information for the transfer method.
    • Note: The required information varies depending on the type of transfer method you select.
  11. Repeat steps 7 through 10 to add additional transfer methods.
  12. Click Confirm when you are done adding transfer methods.

After you click Confirm, your information is automatically submitted to Hyperwallet for verification. Depending on your location, the verification process might take up to 7 days or more. You can monitor the status of your account at any time through the Hyperwallet site dashboard.

When your information is verified, Hyperwallet sends a confirmation email to the email address you provided during your tax interview. Additionally, an overview of your account displays on the Payout Info tab of the Developer Portal.

After you activate your organization's Hyperwallet account, you can make changes to your Hyperwallet account using the Hyperwallet account portal. For more information on changing your Hyperwallet account, including adding new transfer methods, see the documentation: Update Your Hyperwallet Information.
