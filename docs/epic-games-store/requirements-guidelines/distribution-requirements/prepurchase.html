Pre-Purchase Guidelines

Guidelines and best practices for pre-purchase.

8 mins to read

Mobile Early Adopter: If you are in the Mobile Early Adopter program, go to the Mobile Early Adopter—Requirements and Guidelines—Pre-Purchase documentation for guidance before you use this page.

Pre-purchase sales (known as “pre-purchase”) means that users can pay for your product, even though you have not yet released it. Use the following best practices and guidelines to help ensure a successful pre-purchase launch of your product on the Epic Games Store.

Note: Mobile (iOS, Android) products and products that are free on the Epic Games Store cannot be available for pre-purchase.

Check if Pre-Purchase is Enabled for Your Offer

The pre-purchase functionality is only available for select offers at this time.

To check if pre-purchase is enabled for your offer:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Offers page. To do this:

    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

    • b. Select the correct organization for your product.

      (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the on-screen instructions.)

    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.

    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Offers.

  2. Select the sandbox for the offer you want to check.
  3. In the Offers list, click the offer corresponding to the product that you want to check.
  4. In the Offer Details panel, if pre-purchase is enabled for your offer you see the following note: "Pre-purchase has been enabled for this offer.".

The pre-purchase functionality is only available for select products at this time.

Add an Offer as a Pre-purchase Bonus

Offers can be added as an included offer or as a pre-purchase bonus to any offer for your product. Pre-purchase bonuses are only available for purchase before the associated offer's available date. If you want a pre-purchase bonus to be available after the associated offer's available date, you must add it as an included offer (more information: Offer details > Included Offers).

To add an offer as a pre-purchase bonus:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Offers page. To do this:

    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

    • b. Select the correct organization for your product.

      (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the on-screen instructions.)

    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.

    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Offers.

  2. Select the sandbox for the offer you want to add a pre-purchase bonus.
  3. In the Offers list, click the offer corresponding to the product that you want to add a pre-purchase bonus.
  4. In the Offer Details panel, in Optional information, click Included Offers.
  5. From the pre-purchase bonus dropdown, select the offer you want to associate.
  6. Click Save Changes.

Set up a Pre-Purchase

You can set up pre-purchase for base games, editions, add-ons with artifacts, and digital extras. Note that a binary is not required when you set up pre-purchase, but you must provide a binary before the pre-purchase date begins.

You can set up pre-purchase between 24 hours and 90 days before the date and time on which you want the pre-purchase to become available (the pre-purchase date and time). This limitation is due to the following reasons:

  • Planned release dates are less likely to change 90 days or less in advance.
  • There are payment provider restrictions for refunds.

There might be exceptions in cases where an appropriately scaled marketing plan can support a longer duration of pre-purchase. To contact the Epic Games Store team for assistance with this, create a private discussion using this support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com.

You cannot remove a pre-purchase date after you push an offer to the Stage sandbox.

Incentives

We have seen greater success when incentives are included with pre-purchases. Potential incentives could be:

  • Early access (prior to available date).
  • Closed Beta access or Early access to an Open Beta.
  • Additional content such as add-ons or in-game bonuses.
  • Digital extras such as soundtracks, art books, or others.
  • A substantial price discount.
    • More information: Create Introductory Offer
  • Any combination of the above.

Preload

Preload is an option for pre-purchase offers on Windows. When preload is enabled, Epic Games Store users can download the offer before its available date. This means that on the available date they can use the product immediately, without waiting for a download to complete.

Note: Preload functionality is only available for select products at this time. In addition, you can only enable preload on an offer if pre-purchase is enabled on that offer. For more information, see Check if Pre-Purchase is Enabled for Your Offer on this page.

Preload gives the following benefits:

  • For products with large download sizes, it improves accessibility and release-day readiness.
  • It gives smoother access for product users in regions with slower internet connections or restrictive data plans.
  • It improves the experience for multiplayer games with engaged communities, where playing together at release is a key part of the experience.
  • It lets you support your product with an active pre-launch marketing campaign, where pre-purchase and preload are essential for momentum.

On users' PCs, the download is locked (encrypted and unusable) until the available date. On the available date, the download is automatically unlocked.

You can set the date and time from which the preload is available to Epic Games Store users, up to 120 hours (5 days) before your offer available date.

When you communicate with your product users about preloading your offers, we recommend, for clarity, that you use the following terms:

  • Preload: the time when they can download your offer.
  • Unlock: the time when they can run your product.

Preload Requirements

The following requirements apply to preload:

  • You can only enable preload if pre-purchase is enabled on the offer. You have until 12 hours before the release of your offer to enable preload. After this time, preload is not available. For more information on pre-purchase, see Check if Pre-Purchase is Enabled for Your Offer on this page.
  • The preload date and time you set must not be more than 120 hours (5 days) before the available date and time of your offer.
  • You must upload your binaries with BuildPatch Tool (BPT) version 1.8 or higher.
    • You must encrypt every binary for the entire product, not just the binaries in the offer. To request information on how to encrypt a binary, create a private discussion on the support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com.
  • You can enable preload for the following offer types:
    • Base product offer
    • Editions that share the same preload date as their base product offer.
    • Add-ons that have an artifact.
    • Digital extra
    • Documentation: Manage Offers > Offer Types.
  • For additional information or guidance, contact Epic Games Support. To do this, create a private discussion on the support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com.

Enable Preload

Note: Preload functionality is only available for select products at this time. In addition, you can only enable preload on an offer if pre-purchase is enabled on that offer. For more information, see Check if Pre-Purchase is Enabled for Your Offer on this page.

To enable preload on an offer, contact Epic Games Support. To do this, create a private discussion on the support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com. In the DESCRIPTION field, specify when you want preload to become available on your offer. You can specify a maximum of 120 hours (5 days) before your offer's available date. Example: "Enable preload 4 days before the available date".

Ratings

Pre-purchase offers do not require ratings. However, if your product has been rated by a ratings board, then you must display the rating (you must include a “Rating Pending” or similar icon if appropriate).

If your product is not expected to meet the rating requirements for a certain region by the available date, then you must block the pre-purchase offer for that specific region.

More information: Ratings on the Epic Games Store

Refunds

Users can cancel their pre-purchase of a product and get a refund at any time before the available date. After the product is released, users can request a refund within 14 days if they have less than two hours of runtime on record.

In cases where there is a bundle of pre-purchase products, such as a base product and a pre-purchase DLC, the same rules apply. If a user requests a refund after 14 days since their purchase or has a runtime of more than 2 hours, they will not receive a refund.

More information: Epic Games Store Refund Policy

Pre-Purchase Changes

Delayed Release Date (Delayed Available Date)

We understand that plans change and delays happen. Let us know as soon as possible if your product's release date (in the Developer Portal, this is called available date on the Epic Games Store) changes when pre-purchase is enabled on your product. For assistance, contact the Epic Games Store team. To do this, create a private discussion using this support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com.

The Epic Games Store team informs the users who bought the pre-purchase offer as follows:

  • If the delay is less than 30 days from the original available date, the Epic Games Store team notifies the users of the new available date and gives your user the opportunity to cancel their pre-purchase.
  • If the delay is more than 30 days from the original available date, the Epic Games Store team notifies users of the new available date and automatically refund the pre-purchase. The user can pre-purchase again if they choose to do so.
  • If the delay is indefinite and the available date is unknown, the Epic Games Store team notifies users and automatically refund the pre-purchase. Your Product home page returns to a "Coming Soon" state because the available date is unknown.

We suggest that you also communicate any date changes directly to your user base as soon as possible.

Blocked Pre-Purchase Release

You must follow the Epic Games Store’s requirements and guidelines before your available date. (Documentation: Requirements and Guidelines.) If you do not follow the requirements, your product is blocked from release by the Epic Games Store team. Your users that pre-purchased your product are notified as outlined above.
