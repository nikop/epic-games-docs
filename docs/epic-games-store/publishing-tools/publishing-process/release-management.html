Release Management

Manage releases in the Epic Games Store publishing tools.

7 mins to read

The Epic Games Store publishing tools provide you with three sandbox environments to work in when you create and manage your product.

You can view and manage your product throughout the publishing process on the Release Management page of the Dev Portal.

When you set up your product on the Epic Games Store, you must configure the following:

  • Artifacts and Binaries:
    • An artifact represents a single downloadable piece of content in your product, such as a game client or mod editor.
    • A binary is a versioned bundle of files.
    • Documentation: Manage Artifacts.
  • Store settings:
    • High-level details and other configurations that are shared across the whole of your product. Examples: product specifications, ratings, and distribution regions. Documentation: Configure Store Settings .
  • Offers:
    • An offer is an item that Epic Games Store users can buy or download. Examples: your base product, add-ons. Some product details are stored at the offer level, such as dates of availability and pricing. Documentation: Manage Offers.
  • Pages:
    • Your product’s presence on the Epic Games Store, including the product home page, offer pages, and an optional FAQ page. Documentation: Manage Pages

On the Release Management page, you can view the progress of your artifacts and binaries, settings, offers, pages, and web shop items through the publishing workflow.

Sandboxes

When you create a product, the Dev Portal creates a set of sandboxes. You can use these sandboxes to configure, test, and publish your product on the Epic Games Store. On the Release Management page, you can promote your product’s artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, pages, and web shop items between these sandboxes.

The Dev Portal creates the following sandboxes:

  • Dev: The environment where you create and configure the store settings, offers, pages, and artifacts and binaries for your product.
    • Artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, pages, and web shop items begin in the Draft status. After you provide all the required information for one of these items, the status automatically updates to Ready for Stage. To promote the item to the Stage sandbox, click Push to Stage.
    • The Dev sandbox is a private environment. This means that only members of your organization with the right permissions can access the artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, and pages in it. Use the Dev sandbox to configure and test your product securely with your team.
    • The Dev and Stage sandboxes separate any sensitive data (such as offers and artifacts) from the public environment. They are private environments, which means that you can choose who has access to the configuration, testing, and review process for your product before its launch.
  • Stage: The environment where you test and submit finalized configurations for review by the Epic Games Store team before these configurations enter the Live environment.
    • After you test the configurations of your artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, and pages, click Submit to Review to send your product to the Epic Games Store team for review.
      • After you send your product for review, if you realize that you want to make changes, click Rescind. Make any necessary updates and resubmit your product for review when you are ready. You cannot rescind a review request after the review has begun.
      • During review, the Epic Games Store team ensures that your product is configured properly and that it meets all the requirements for distribution. If the team finds any issues, it provides feedback to you. You must complete any necessary updates in the Dev sandbox, push the changes to the Stage sandbox, then submit your product for another review.
      • When your product passes review by the Epic Games Store team, the store settings, offers, pages, web shop items, and artifacts and binaries in the product have the Ready for Live status. For more information, see the Status Definitions section on this page.
    • The Stage sandbox is a private environment. This means that only members of your organization with the right permissions can access the artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, and pages in it. Use the Stage sandbox to check and test your product securely with your team.
    • The Dev and Stage sandboxes separate any sensitive data (such as offers and artifacts) from the public environment. They are private environments, which means that you can choose who has access to the configuration, testing, and review process for your product before its launch.
  • Live: Represents the public Epic Games Store environment. Artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, pages, and web shop items in this sandbox are accessible to Epic Games Store users on the storefront and indexable on the public web.
    • You can push artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, pages, and web shop items to the Live sandbox only after the Epic Games Store team has reviewed them.
      • When your artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, pages, and web shop items have the Ready for Live status, you can push them to the Live environment. To do so, click Push to Live.
      • If you have preload enabled on any offer in a product, then you cannot push any unencrypted binaries to Live for the entire product. In other words, you must encrypt all binaries in the entire product before you can push to Live. For more information, see Pre-Purchase Guidelines > Preload.
      • You can publish your artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, and pages immediately, or you can select a specific date and time for the push to Live. For more information on how to schedule a push to Live, see documentation: Release Your Product > Process.
        • At the scheduled publish date and time, the Developer Portal promotes the selected entities (artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, pages, and web shops) in the Stage sandbox in the state they are in at that time. If any of these entities have not passed review at that time, then the scheduled publish fails. To avoid this situation, if you push any updates to these entities to the Stage sandbox, make sure they pass review before the scheduled publish time.
          • For more information on reviews, see Submit for Final Review > Submit Your Product For Review > Submit for Review Through the Developer Portal.
        • You can cancel a scheduled publish on the Release Management page before its specified date. To do this, on the Release Management page, click Cancel.
      • You do not have to push all of your entities (artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, pages, and web shop items) to the Live sandbox. You can push a selection of these to Live.
        • Note: At a minimum, you must push the following entities, if they are not already in your Live sandbox:
        • A base product offer
        • A set of store settings
        • A product home page
        • An artifact for the base product offer (you do not need to do this if the base product offer is a "coming soon" offer, in other words if the release date is unknown).
        • Any other entities that are mandatory for your product (for example, an FAQ page if your product is a PC (Windows, macOS) product in Early Access).
          • For information on mandatory entities, see documentation: Epic Games Store Requirements.

Status Definitions

Each set of artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, and pages in your product has a status that shows its current position in the publishing workflow. The icon and meaning of each status is as follows.

Dev Sandbox Statuses

Draft: The set of artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, or pages is incomplete. You must carry out additional configuration on it before you push it to the Stage sandbox.

Ready for Stage: The set of artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, or pages is complete. You can push it to the Stage sandbox.

Pushed to Stage: You have pushed the set of artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, or pages to the Stage sandbox.

Stage Sandbox Statuses

Ready for Review: You can submit the set of artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, or pages to the Epic Games Store team for review.

Queued for Review: You have sent the set of artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, or pages for review by the Epic Games Store team. The team has not reviewed it yet.

In Review: The Epic Games Store team is currently reviewing the set of artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, or pages.

Issues Found: The set of artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, or pages has not passed review. This means it has one or more issues that you must resolve before you can push it to the Live sandbox.

Ready for Live: The set of artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, or pages has passed review. This means it does not have any issues. You can push it to the Live sandbox.

Pushed to Live: You have pushed the set of artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, or pages to the Live sandbox.

Processing: The Epic Games Store is currently copying the set of artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, or pages between sandboxes.

Live Sandbox Statuses

Live: The set of artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, or pages is currently in the Live sandbox.
