Introduction

Introduction to the Epic Games account ecosystem and the social features it gives your players access to.

Access the Epic Games Account Ecosystem

EOS Epic Account Services

EOS Epic Account Services provide player accounts: either an Epic Games account or a proxy account. This enables your game to tap into a player account ecosystem of nearly a billion players across multiple platforms and stores.

EOS Epic Account Services includes the following topics, among others:

  • Get Started with the Epic Games account ecosystem.
  • Auth Interface [EOS Epic Account Services Authentication] — how-to guide and reference information.
  • Brand Review — reference information and guidance on putting your game through the brand review process and domain verification.
