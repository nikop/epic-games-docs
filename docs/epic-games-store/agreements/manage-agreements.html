Manage Agreements

Manage and accept Epic Games Store agreements and disclosures.

4 mins to read

Your organization must accept the appropriate agreements in order to distribute your product on the Epic Games Store. Your organization’s owner or a team member with Agreements Management permissions (previously called Legal Management permissions) can accept agreements on behalf of your organization. You cannot use the Epic Games Store publishing tools until this step is complete.

The following points are guidance on accepting agreements:

  • You must be 18 or older to accept the required agreements and distribute your product on the Epic Games Store. If your organization owner or a team member with Agreements Management permissions is unable to accept agreements on behalf of your legal entity, create a private discussion using the support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com.
  • If Epic Games makes a major version update to the End User License agreement (more information: store.epicgames.com/~/eula), your organization must accept the updated agreement in order to continue using the publishing tools. You will not be able to access the tools until this step is complete.
  • When Epic Games updates a specific feature agreement that you have accepted, you will be notified when you next attempt to access the publishing tools. Your organization owner or a team member with Agreements Management permissions must accept the updated agreement in order to maintain use of the associated feature.

Review and Accept Agreements

You can review the Epic Games Store agreements at any time in the Agreements tab on the Organization page in the Developer Portal. (There is guidance on how to go to the page below.)

This tab contains a number of agreements related to both the Epic Games Store and other services offered by Epic Games. The following agreements are relevant to the Epic Games Store publishing tools:

  • Epic Games Store Distribution Agreement: Required to use the Epic Games Store publishing tools. For more information, see the distribution agreement in the Developer Portal at: dev.epicgames.com/portal/~/distribution-agreement (you do not need to be signed in to see this).
  • Epic Games Store Disclosure Statement: Epic Games maintains a disclosure statement discussing ranking practices, data access policies, and the EU Platform to Business Regulation complaint handling procedures of the Epic Games Store. Epic Games will provide you with a written notice of any updates to this disclosure. For more information, see the following documentation: Disclosure Statement.
  • Epic Games Store - Email Sharing Rider: Required to request and leverage the Email Marketing Subscriber List. For more information, see the following documentation: Email Marketing Subscriber List.
  • Epic Achievements: Required to use Epic Games Store Achievements. For more information, see the following documentation: Epic Games Store Achievements.

How to Access Agreements
To read and accept an agreement, follow the steps below. You can also use this process to review or download previously accepted agreements.

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Organization page. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. Select the correct organization for your product.
      (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization in the top left navigation and follow the on-screen instructions.)
    • c. Click Organization further down the left navigation (under Dashboard).
  2. In the Organization page, click Agreements.
  3. Scroll down to locate the appropriate agreement and click Review and accept.
  4. Review the terms of the agreement. You must view the entire agreement before you can click Accept.
  5. (Optional): To save an offline copy of the agreement, click Download .zip on the right-side pane.
  6. To accept the agreement, click Accept.

Notes:

  • Agreements are only available in English.
  • When you accept an agreement, your name and the timestamp of your signature are recorded.
  • You must accept the Blockchain Rider agreement if your product contains blockchain, cryptocurrency, or non-fungible tokens (NFT) technologies.
  • If your organization is unable to accept an agreement for any reason, create a private discussion using the support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com.

Review Your Organization’s Agreement History

The Agreements tab on the Organization page in the Developer Portal provides an overview of your organization’s historical interaction with each agreement. The following details are available for each agreement:

  • The specific versions of the agreement that your organization has accepted.
  • The organization member who accepted each version.
  • The date and time each version was accepted.

To review a specific agreement's history, follow the steps below:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Organization page. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. Select the correct organization for your product.
      (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization in the top left navigation and follow the on-screen instructions.)
    • c. Click Organization further down the left navigation (under Dashboard).
  2. In the Organization page, click Agreements.
  3. Scroll down to locate the appropriate agreement and click the three dots ( … ), then click the type of information you want and follow the onscreen instructions.
  4. (Optional): To save a copy of a specific agreement version, in the agreement history pane, click Download .zip.

Tip: To review the current terms of an agreement, see Review and Accept an Agreements on this page.
