Publishing Service Level Agreement

Details of minimum response times to your support requests.

2 mins to read

Support

To get help, do either of the following:

  • Post in the Epic Developer Community forums: forums.unrealengine.com/~/epic-games-store.
  • Create a private discussion on the support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com.

Service Level Agreement

The service level agreement (SLA) is the minimum amount of time that it takes Epic Games support to reply to your request.

The SLA below is the default you have when you sign up to use Epic Games publishing tools.

SLA for General Support and Submissions

SITUATION SLA
General Support 1 - 3 business days
Build Testing (See related documentation.) 2 - 3 business days
Epic Games Store Achievements (See related documentation, specifically the how to test achievements section.) 2 - 3 business days
Multiplayer Titles - Crossplay is required if your product is distributed on other PC platforms (see related crossplay documentation). Note: You must submit your product binaries (your build) for final review 4 weeks before you launch to allow Epic Games to test them.

Crossplay is not mandatory for mobile (iOS, Android) products.

 3 - 5 business days
Epic Account Services Brand Review (See related documentation.) 2 - 3 business days
Final Review Before Launch (See related documentation.) 3 - 5 business days

SLA for Other Requests

SITUATION SLA
Customize Pricing (See related documentation.) Note: New prices are not available for sale submission until the price has been live for 30 days. 1 - 2 business days
Localization (See related documentation.) Note: You can add your own localized content or contact Epic Games to localize content for you. 3 - 5 business days
Retail Key Request (See related documentation.) (Requires approval.) 2 - 3 business days
Keyless Activation (See related documentation.) (Requires approval.) 3 - 5 business days
Product Organization Migration (See guidance on the transfer process documentation.) (Requires approval.) 2 - 4 weeks

SLA Notes

  • Holidays might alter the SLAs.
  • To receive updates on Epic Games Store, enable notifications on your account in the Developer Portal. To do this:
    1. Sign in to the Developer Portal at dev.epicgames.com/portal.
    2. Click your account name in the top right of the page.
    3. Select Manage Account from the drop-down menu.
    4. In the ACCOUNT SETTINGS page, select EMAIL PREFERENCES.
    5. On this page, scroll down to subscribe to the Epic Games Store.
