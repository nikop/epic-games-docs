Achievements Overview

Enhance your product's user experience with Epic Games Store achievements.

2 mins to read

For products published to the Epic Games Store, you can enhance your users’ experience with Epic Games Store achievements. These achievements extend the functionality of Epic Online Services achievements and can be used to reward users with experience points (XP) for interacting with your product.

Products that support achievements on other PC storefronts must also support Epic Games Store achievements. The achievements available to users on the Epic Games Store must be substantially similar to those offered on other PC storefronts. The following product types are exempted from having achievements on the Epic Games Store:


  • Mobile (iOS, Android) products.
  • Web-based and HTML products because they are not currently supported by the Epic Online Services (EOS) SDK.
  • Products that are distributed on macOS because this operating system is not currently supported by the Epic Online Services (EOS) Overlay.
  • If a product in early access does not yet have a finalized set of base product achievements defined, you only need to implement Epic Online Services achievements for the early access release. When your product is ready for release, Epic Games Store achievements must be enabled. For information, see the documentation:
    • EOS Achievements Interface
    • EGS Achievements Setup Guide

Note: This requirement does not retroactively apply to any existing products available on the Epic Games Store or any products that were onboarded in the Developer Portal prior to March 9, 2023. However, enabling achievements is recommended to promote an optimal user experience.

Product users can review both their own and their friends' achievements through the My Achievements page of the Epic Games Store. Additionally, products with Epic Games Store achievements enabled feature a dedicated Achievements page on their store presence that highlights the achievements available to users. Users can also choose to browse games with achievements on the Epic Games Store, promoting greater visibility for your product.

When you implement Epic Online Services achievements in your product, you can add Epic Games Store achievements directly in the Developer Portal. Additional code is not required.

Notes:

  • If Epic Games Store achievements are added to your product on PC, these achievements display in users’ in-game social overlays in place of any Epic Online Services achievements.
  • The EOS Overlay is not available on mobile (iOS, Android) or macOS, which means achievements do not display on mobile or macOS unless you render them in your in-game UI.

Publish Epic Games Store Achievements

To publish Epic Games Store achievements, you need to do the following:

  1. Push the achievements to the Stage sandbox.
  2. Submit the achievements for review.
  3. Push the achievements to the Live sandbox.

For more information, see the following documentation:

  • Publishing Tools Workflow
  • Epic Games Store Achievements Setup Guide
