Manage Offers

Create and manage offers for your product.

13 mins to read

Mobile Early Adopter: Do not use this page. Go to the Mobile Early Adopter section for guidance.

Offers represent the saleable content associated with your product. The Epic Games Store associates essential information about your product, such as pricing and availability dates, with offers.

When you first set up your product, the Developer Portal automatically creates an offer for your main product, known as the “base product offer”. After you set up your product, you can create additional offers that allow users to purchase or download supplemental content that enhances the experience of your product. These additional offers can be demos, editions, add-ons, season passes, consumables, or digital extras.

When you create an offer, the Developer Portal automatically creates a corresponding Epic Games Store page, called an offer page. This page publicizes your offer on the Epic Games Store and lets Epic Games Store users buy or download the offer.

To control when your offer is available for purchase on the Epic Games Store, set the appropriate availability dates on the offer. For more information, see Offer Details–Dates.

After you push an offer to your Stage or Live sandboxes, you cannot delete it. However, you can use the offer's availability dates to make it no longer available for purchase.

Offer Types

When you create an offer, you must assign an offer type, based on the type of content that you want to provide to people who buy the offer.

Every product has a base product offer. The Developer Portal creates a base product offer automatically when you create a product.

The base product offer always has the "game" offer type. Typically, you use the base product offer for the main edition of your product. Your product can only have one offer with the "game" offer type.

You can also create other offers for your product. These offers are called "additional offers".

Note:

  • After you create an offer, you cannot change its offer type.
  • For information about what content you can include in your offers, see the Content Guidelines page.

The available offer types are as follows:

  • Game: Your base product offer. Every product has one base product offer. You would typically use this for the main edition of your product.
  • Edition: A version of your product that has different content from the base product offer. An edition is always based on another offer of the same product. It can have different binaries from the offer it is based on.
    • You must select a base offer for the edition. This can be the base product offer or another edition offer of the same product.
    • Edition-type offers always include the base game artifact.
    • For more information on editions, see Editions below.
  • Demo: A partial version of your product that lets Epic Games Store users try the product before they buy it.
    • A demo always has an associated artifact.
  • Add-on: Additional content that a product user can acquire separately from the main product, such as maps, characters, weapons, or skins.
    • You must declare whether or not the offer includes virtual currency or other consumable items (such as loot chests or potions).
    • An add-on optionally has an associated artifact.
  • Season pass: A collection of add-ons available within a certain time period.
  • Consumable: An offer that product users can purchase multiple times, such as virtual currency.
  • Digital extra: Additional media such as a soundtrack or an art book.
    • A digital extra always has an associated artifact.

Configure Your Product's Base Product Offer

After you create your product, the Developer Portal automatically creates the base product offer and assigns Game as its type. You can then add relevant details to the offer.

To add details to your base product offer:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Offers page. To do this:

    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

    • b. Select the correct organization for your product.

      (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the on-screen instructions.)

    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.

    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Offers.

  2. Select the Dev sandbox.
  3. Click the name of your base product offer (the offer with the type Game).
  4. In the Offer Details panel, use the various buttons to go to the different settings categories for your offer, where you can view and change the settings in that category. For information about a specific settings category, see the appropriate documentation:
    • General details
    • Dates
    • Store visibility
    • Price
    • Images
    • Artifact settings
    • Included Offers
    • Cloud save
    • Regions

Create Additional Offers

In addition to your base product offer, you can create offers that let users purchase or download different content associated with your product.

You can clone (copy) offer details from one offer to other offers in the same product. For more information, see Clone Offer Details Between Offers.

To add an offer to a product:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Offers page. To do this:

    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

    • b. Select the correct organization for your product.

      (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the on-screen instructions.)

    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.

    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Offers.

  2. Select the Dev sandbox.
  3. Click Create Offer.
  4. Follow the on-screen instructions to enter the details of the offer. Use the following guidance to help you enter the details:
    • Enter offer name: Enter the text that you want to display to users in the Epic Games Store as a name for your offer.
      • Tip: To improve the SEO performance of offers for items such as add-ons and season passes, include your product name in the offer name. Examples: Rocket League–Painted Prestige Bundle, Fall Guys–Season 2 Starter Pack.
      • The Epic Games Store uses the text that you enter here to create the URL for the offer. You can update the offer name at a later time, but you cannot change the URL in the Developer Portal after you create the offer. If you need to change an offer URL, see documentation: Product Names and URLs.
    • Offer type: Select the appropriate category for your offer. You cannot change the offer type after you create the offer.
      • For more information, see Offer Types on this page.
  5. If you selected Edition as the offer type, do the following:
    • Click Next.
    • Select the base offer for your edition. The base offer can be your base product offer or another edition. For more information, see documentation: Offer Details–Included Offers.
  6. If you selected Add-on as the offer type, do the following:
    • Click Next.
    • Declare whether the add-on includes virtual currency or other consumable items. Documentation: General Details.
  7. Click Create.
  8. To edit the details of your new offer, on the Offer Details page, click your product.
  9. In the Offer Details panel, use the various buttons to go to the different settings categories for your offer, where you can view and change the settings in that category. For information about a specific settings category, see the appropriate documentation:
    • General details
    • Dates
    • Store visibility
    • Price
    • Images
    • Artifact settings
    • Included Offers
    • Cloud save
    • Regions

Consumable Offers

Consumable offers let product users buy and redeem in-game items that have finite lifespans, such as currency.

  • You can specify suggested pricing for consumable offers as part of your product configuration. Documentation: Pricing Overview.
  • You cannot assign included offers (more information: Included Offers) to consumable offers.
  • You cannot assign artifacts to consumable offers.
  • You cannot include consumable offers in bundles.
  • You cannot select consumables as included offers.

In your product, if you want to list the entitlements in a user’s account, use the Epic Online Services QueryEntitlements API. If you want to mark entitlements as redeemed by a user, use the RedeemEntitlements API.

Price Option — Not Available for Direct Purchase

When you create a consumable offer, you can set the offer price to Not Available for Direct Purchase. If you do this, then the offer is not available for purchase on the Epic Games Store. Instead, users can only redeem this offer with an access key. To redeem the offer, they must have previously purchased or downloaded the base product offer.

Editions

The following limits apply for edition-type offers:

  • A product can have up to 10 edition-type offers available on the storefront at the same time.
    • Your base product offer does not count towards this limit.
  • You can create more than 10 editions for your product, but only 10 can be active in your Live sandbox at the same time.
    • If you push an 11th edition-type offer to the Live sandbox, the oldest available edition is automatically hidden on the storefront. In the Dev Portal, this offer is tagged as Not Visible on Store.
    • To choose which edition is hidden, do the following:
      • In your Live sandbox, add an Expiration Date to the edition you want to hide. Documentation: Offer Details–Dates.
      • When that expiration date occurs, push your 11th edition-type offer to the Live sandbox.
  • If you have more than 10 edition-type offers in your Live sandbox, one of your hidden editions might automatically become available if an active edition expires.
    • An "active edition" is an edition where the following applies:
      • It has not expired yet.
      • You have not marked it as Not Visible on Store.
    • In this scenario, when an active edition expires, the hidden edition in the Live sandbox with the nearest viewable date becomes active. If no editions have an viewable date, then the edition with the nearest available date becomes active.
    • Offers are not visible to users before their viewable date.

Example situation:

  • Your Live sandbox contains 12 edition-type offers.
    • 10 of these offers are currently available on the storefront.
    • The other two offers are marked as Not Visible on Store.
  • One of the 10 active offers expires and is automatically removed from the storefront. In its place, the hidden offer with the nearest viewable date automatically becomes active. The Not Visible on Store tag is automatically removed from this offer, and the offer becomes visible on the storefront when its viewable date occurs.

Delete an Offer

When you delete an offer, you permanently remove the offer, and any corresponding pages and artifacts, from your product. You cannot undo this action.

You can only delete offers that are in your Dev sandbox.

You cannot delete offers in the following situations:

  • Stage or Live sandboxes
    • You cannot delete offers that are in your Stage or Live sandboxes.
  • Certain types of offer
    • You cannot delete the following types of offer:
      • Your base product offer (also known as a game-type offer). More information: see Offer Types on this page.
      • Edition-type offers
      • Offers that are configured as Not Available for Direct Purchase. More information: see Consumable Offers on this page
  • If another offer depends on it
    • If an offer has one or more included offers, then you must first remove these included offers from the offer. Documentation: Offer Details–Included Offers.
    • You can delete any included offers . When you do this, the included offer is automatically removed from its parent offer or offers. You do not need to manually remove the included offer from its parent offer or offers.
    • If an offer is the base offer for another offer, then you must first remove it as that base offer. You cannot delete an offer that is currently set as a base offer.
    • Documentation: Offer Details–Unlink a Base Offer from an Edition.

To delete an offer:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Offers page. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. Select the correct organization for your product. (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the on-screen instructions.)
    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.
    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Offers.
  2. Select the Dev sandbox.
  3. Find the offer you want to delete and click the three dots (...).
  4. Select Delete.

Clone Offer Details Between Offers

You can clone (copy) offer details between offers in the same product. This saves time, because you don't need to set the same offer details on two or more offers. Instead, you can set the offer details on one offer and then clone them to other offers.

When you clone offer details, you choose the source offer (the offer that the details are cloned from) and one or more destination offers (the offer or offers that the details are cloned to). The source offer and destination offers must be in the same product. The Developer Portal replaces the offer details in the destination offers with the offer details from the source offer.

You can clone the following offer details between offers:

  • General details: see Offer Details > General Details.
    • Note: The Developer Portal does not clone NFT and blockchain usage declarations and promotional content declarations to the destination offers. If either of these declarations applies to your destination offers, you need to make these declarations in the destination offers after you clone the offer details.
  • Dates: see Offer Details > Dates.
  • Price: see Offer Details > Price.
  • Images: see Offer Details > Images.
  • Regions: see Offer Details > Regions.

You don't have to clone all of these offer details. You can choose a selection of details to clone to the destination offers.

Example:

If you choose to clone price and images, then the Developer Portal replaces the price and images in the destination offers with the price and images in the source offer. It doesn't replace the general details, dates, or regions details.

Note:

  • To clone content between offers, you need at least two offers in your product, because you must select a source offer and at least one destination offer.
  • The clone operation is a one-time copy. In other words, after you clone offer details, the source and destination offers do not stay synchronized. When you make changes to the source offer, the destination offers do not automatically change.
  • The clone operation does not clone content that is not applicable to the destination offer type. Check the preview (see below) to see what will change in your destination offer.
  • You can only select source offers and destination offers in the Dev sandbox.
  • For the source offer, you can only select an offer that has the Ready for Stage status.
  • Depending on the settings in your selected source offer and destination offers, you might not be able to clone your selected offer details. In this situation, the Developer Portal displays a message that explains why the clone operation is not possible.

To clone offer details between offers:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Offers page. To do this:

    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. Select the correct organization for your product.
    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.
    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Offers.

  2. Select the Dev sandbox.

  3. At the top right, click Clone content.

  4. Select the source offer. This is the offer you want to copy the details from.

    • Use the Search offer box to find offers by offer name.

  5. Click Next.

  6. In DESTINATION OFFERS, select the offer, or offers, you want to clone to. You can select more than one offer.

    • To help you find specific offers in the list, you can use the following tools:
      • Use Filter by offer type to only show offers of specific types.
      • Use the Search offer box to find offers by offer name.

  7. For each destination offer you select, select which offer details (general details, dates, price, images, or regions) you want to copy to that offer. By default, all details are selected.

    • Note: The DESTINATION OFFER PREVIEW shows the effect that the clone operation will have on the selected offer, based on the source offer and the offer details you have selected. The preview shows the most important fields in the offer, including the fields that will be changed by the clone operation.

      • Removed text is struck through:

      • Added text is shown in green:

      • Removed images have a red X icon:

      • Added images have a green check icon:

      • If you select more than one destination offer, the preview displays one destination offer at a time. To cycle through each preview, use the left and right arrows at the top right of the preview:

  8. At the top right of the page, click Clone content.
