Parent Verification Service Verification Methods

How parents or guardians can verify that they are adults.

7 mins to read

The Parent Verification (PV) Service supports several verification methods depending on the location you provide to the API. When configuring your PV Service settings, you can choose which verification methods to offer parents or guardians depending on their child’s location. KWS verification methods are designed to provide global coverage in all countries and regions recognized under the ISO 3166-1 standard, unless otherwise specified for a particular method.

KWS supports the verification methods described below:

Verification Method Availability
Credit Card Worldwide except in Belarus, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Russia, Syria, and Ukraine
Debit Card Worldwide except in Belarus, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Russia, Syria, Ukraine, and the United Kingdom
ID Scan Worldwide except in the United States and the Republic of Korea
Face Scan Worldwide except in the United States and the Republic of Korea
Social Security Number (SSN) United States and US territories
CPF Brazil
CURP Mexico
i-PIN Republic of Korea
Cell Phone Republic of Korea

Credit Card

Credit card verification is available worldwide, except in Belarus, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Russia, Syria, and Ukraine.

A parent or guardian in all regions except Belarus, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Russia, Syria, and Ukraine can verify that they are an adult using a credit card. Stripe (stripe.com) verifies credit card details.

  • For parents or guardians in the United States, KWS temporarily charges $0.05 to their credit card and refunds the charge after verification is complete.
  • For parents or guardians outside the United States, KWS authorizes a small charge in the local currency but does not withdraw the money from their credit card. For example, KWS authorizes a £0.05 charge for parents or guardians in the United Kingdom.

KWS uses GeoIP to determine the parent or guardian's current location (country or region) and uses the local currency for these charges.

Debit Card

Debit card verification is available worldwide, except in Belarus, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Russia, Syria, Ukraine, and the United Kingdom.

A parent or guardian in all regions except Belarus, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Russia, Syria, Ukraine, and the United Kingdom can verify that they are an adult using a debit card. Stripe (stripe.com) verifies debit card details.

  • For parents or guardians in the United States, KWS temporarily charges $0.05 to their debit card and refunds the charge after verification is complete.
  • For parents or guardians outside the United States, KWS authorizes a small charge in the local currency but does not withdraw the money from their debit card. For example, KWS authorizes a ¥0.05 charge for parents or guardians in Japan.

KWS uses GeoIP to determine the parent or guardian's current location (country or region) and uses the local currency for these charges.

ID Scan

ID scan is available worldwide, except in the United States and the Republic of Korea.

A parent or guardian in all regions, except the United States and the Republic of Korea, can verify that they are an adult by scanning the front and back of their ID documentation. This method supports various forms of ID documentation from multiple jurisdictions, including passports, driver’s licenses, and government-issued ID cards.

Veriff (veriff.com) verifies scanned ID documentation.

Face Scan

Face scan is available worldwide, except in the United States and the Republic of Korea.

A parent or guardian in all regions, except the United States and the Republic of Korea, can verify they are an adult using a face scan. KWS prompts the parent or guardian to hold their device’s camera in front of their face, and a machine-learning algorithm estimates their age.

Yoti (yoti.com) provides face scan verification.

Social Security Number (SSN)

The Social Security number (SSN) verification method is only available in the United States and US territories.

A parent or guardian in the United States and US territories can verify that they are an adult by entering the last four digits of their Social Security number (SSN). An SSN is a 9-digit identification number issued to United States citizens and permanent residents for taxation and other purposes.

The parent or guardian must enter the following information:

  • First name
  • Last name
  • Date of birth
  • Address
  • The last four digits of their SSN

Veratad (veratad.com) provides SSN verification.

CPF

The CPF number (Cadastro de Pessoas Físicas or Natural Persons Register) is available only in Brazil.

A parent or guardian in Brazil can verify that they are an adult using their CPF number. The CPF (Cadastro de Pessoas Físicas or Natural Persons Register) is an identification number that the Brazilian Revenue Agency assigns to Brazilian citizens and resident aliens subject to taxes in Brazil.

KWS prompts the parent or guardian to enter their CPF number and date of birth.

Serpro (serpro.gov.br) provides CPF verification.

CURP

The CURP number (Clave Única de Registro de Población, Unique Population Registry Code, or Personal ID Code Number) is available only in Mexico.

A parent or guardian in Mexico can verify that they are an adult using their CURP. The CURP (Clave Única de Registro de Población or Unique Population Registry Code or Personal ID Code Number) is an 18-character alphanumeric string used as a unique identification code for Mexican citizens and permanent residents.

The parent or guardian must enter the following:

  • Their 18-character CURP
  • First name
  • Last name

Nubarium (nubarium.com) provides CURP verification.

i-PIN

i-PIN is available only in the Republic of Korea.

A parent or guardian in the Republic of Korea can verify that they are an adult using i-PIN authentication. i-PIN is a digital identification system used as an alternative to the Korean Resident Registration Number (RRN) for online services. KWS directs the parent or guardian to enter their i-PIN ID and password.

The Korea Credit Bureau (KCB) (koreacb.com) provides i-PIN verification.

Cell Phone

The cell phone verification method is only available in the Republic of Korea.

A parent or guardian in the Republic of Korea can verify that they are an adult using a cell phone number. KWS prompts the parent or guardian to input additional information to confirm that they are an adult using the PASS authentication application or SMS.

For the PASS application, the parent or guardian must enter the following:

  • First name
  • Last name
  • Cell phone number

For SMS, the parent or guardian must enter the following:

  • First name
  • Last name
  • First seven digits of their Korean resident registration number (RRN)
  • Cell phone number

The Korea Credit Bureau (KCB) (koreacb.com) provides cell phone verification.
