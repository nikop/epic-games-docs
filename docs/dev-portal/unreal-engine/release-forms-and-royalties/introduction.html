Release Forms and Royalty Reporting for Unreal Engine Products — Introduction

Overview of Release Forms and royalty reporting.

Under 1 min to read

This page applies if your product (game or other software application) uses Unreal Engine.

When you make a product (game or other software application) in Unreal Engine, you must carry out the following revenue reporting activities:

  • When you distribute your product:
    • Submit a Release Form to Epic Games.
  • For each calendar quarter in which you earn revenue:
    • Report revenue to Epic Games on a per-product basis, within 45 days of the end of the calendar quarter. You can do this in the Developer Portal. For more information, see Submit and Manage Unreal Engine Royalties.
    • Pay Epic Games any due royalties. After you report your revenue, Epic Games emails you an invoice for your royalty payment.
    • Note: For detailed information about Unreal Engine royalties, including your responsibilities to report and pay them, see the Unreal Engine End User License Agreement (EULA): unrealengine.com/…/eula/unreal.

You can complete the Release Form and report revenue in the Epic Games Developer Portal. For more information about these, see the following documentation:

  • Notify Epic Games About Your Product Releases
  • Submit and Manage Unreal Engine Royalties
