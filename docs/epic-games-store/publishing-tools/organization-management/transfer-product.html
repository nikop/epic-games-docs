Transfer a Product to Another Organization

Transfer a Product from one Dev Portal Organization to another.

2 mins to read

To transfer your product to another organization, you must complete and submit the Product Migration Request form linked below.

Lead Times

Note: We recommend that you submit the form as soon as possible to ensure the transfer is complete by your target date. If you have legal requirements or deadlines, you must submit the information at least 30 days before the relevant deadlines.

Complete the Product Migration Request Form

Complete and submit the Google form at google.com.

You must fill out the form with the following information:

  • The product name
  • From (Old) Publisher Name
  • From (Old) Publisher Address
  • From (Old) Publisher Signatory Name and Title
  • From (Old) Publisher Signatory Email
  • To (New) Publisher Name
  • To (New) Publisher Address
  • To (New) Publisher Signatory Name and Title
  • To (New) Publisher Signatory Email
  • Effective (Legal) date of transfer
  • (Optional) New Organization ID
  • (Optional) New Product ID

To find your Product ID, follow these steps:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. Select the correct organization for your product.
    (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the on-screen instructions.)
  3. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of the product.
  4. In the left navigation, click Product Settings.
  5. The Product Settings page defaults to the SDK Download and Credentials tab. Scroll down this tab to the PRODUCT section. Look for Product ID.

To find your Organization ID, follow these steps:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. Select the correct organization for your product.
    (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the on-screen instructions.)
  3. Click Settings in the Organization tab.
  4. You can find your Organization ID in General Settings.

Next Steps

After Epic Games receives your form, you will receive a response from an Epic Games Release Manager and Business Development contact. The response contains a Transfer or a Termination Agreement, as appropriate. All parties relating to the transfer must sign the agreement before Epic Games can begin the transfer.
