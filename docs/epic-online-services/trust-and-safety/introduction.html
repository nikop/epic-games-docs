Trust and Safety Introduction

Protect players, build trust, and safeguard your game's integrity.

Under 1 min to read

Build player trust by safeguarding your community and promoting a fair and safe experience for everyone. Easy Anti-Cheat (the Anti-Cheat Interfaces), player reports, and sanctions protect game integrity and help curb problem behavior.

Trust and Safety are EOS Game Services — players can connect to your game with an account from any supported identity provider. For details, see Epic Online Services (EOS) Overview > Type of Service.

Manage trust and safety features for your game with the following interfaces:

  • Anti-Cheat (also known as “Easy Anti-Cheat”): Prevent and detect players cheating within your game.
  • Reports (also known as “Player Reports”): Collect and publish player behavior to player reports. View details about player cheating, verbal abuse and offensive profiles.
  • Sanctions: Define and manage sanctions for your game. In the Developer Portal, you can view the details of all sanctions placed on a player.

Get Started

To integrate EOS with your game, see the following documentation:

  • Guide: Get Started with EOS — a step-by-step set-up guide with example code snippets and code explanations.
  • How-To Guides — After you have followed the Get Started with EOS guide, use the How-To guides to integrate EOS services with your game. They are step-by-step guides with example code snippets and code explanations.

About Kids Web Services (KWS)

Kids Web Services (KWS) is also available but is a separate service set from EOS. KWS provides parent verification so you can manage youth audiences at scale. KWS greatly reduces the complexity and cost of global verification and consent management. (Documentation: Kids Web Services (KWS))
