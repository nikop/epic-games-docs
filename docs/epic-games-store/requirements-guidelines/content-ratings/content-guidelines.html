Content Requirements

Requirements for product and store presence content.

14 mins to read

The Epic Games Store has product and storefront content requirements that apply to your product when you distribute it on the Epic Games Store. Prior to your product's launch, the Epic Games Store team evaluates the product and storefront content to confirm that it adheres to the following content requirements.

Accuracy and Consistency

  • Your product content and binary (executable file) must be accurate and consistent with the product description presented to users on your product home page.
  • Your product content must focus on the product you intend to release, and not future iterations, to avoid misleading users about what is available at the time of purchase.
  • Your rating must be accurate and consistent with the nature of your product. Providing false or misleading information to achieve a different rating is prohibited. (Documentation: IARC Ratings)
  • Using pre-made assets is acceptable as long as your product has some playability or interactive experience. Asset flipping is not allowed.

Rating Authority Compliance

  • If your product is pending or has received a final rating from a ratings authority (for example, ESRB, PEGI, ClassInd, or USK), your product home page content must adhere to any guidelines provided by the rating authority. See the ratings authority guidelines before publishing content on your product home page. For more information, see the ratings authorities' documentation. For example (not a comprehensive list):
    • PEGI: pegi.info/~/pegi-age-ratings
    • ESRB: esrb.org/ratings-guide
    • GRAC: grac.or.kr/
    • USK: usk.de/
  • You are welcome and encouraged to obtain IARC ratings. You can display IARC ratings on your product home page in the storefront. (Documentation: IARC Ratings)

Age Considerations

The following policies apply to all products distributed on Epic Games Store:

  • Products with Adults Only (AO) ratings cannot be distributed on the Epic Games Store.
    • Note: The Epic Games Store makes an exception for products that have an AO rating only because they use blockchain, NFT, or cryptocurrency technology with no other AO content. (Blockchain, NFT, and cryptocurrency is known as “blockchain technology” — see the following glossary entry for a full definition: blockchain technology.) However, these products must comply with the store blockchain technology and content guidelines, including the prohibitions on pornography, gambling, and hateful content. (Documentation: Blockchain Technology Guidelines.)
  • Unrated products are treated as having the highest-level maturity rating available in each region where they are distributed. (Documentation: Ratings on the Epic Games Store > Unrated Products)

Content Placement and Ratings

There are also age considerations specific to your product's rating and the placement of its content, which includes text, offer images, web shop item images, product logos, and video trailers.

After you submit your product for review, you may receive an “issues found” notification. If you receive an “issues found” notification for one of the following reasons, you must update your submission. (Documentation: Submit for Final Review > Receive the Review Results.)

  • If your storefront content or binary exceeds your product's ratings.
  • If the storefront content exceeds the ratings limit for that content placement.

Exceeds Product's Ratings

Content in your product's storefront and binary must not exceed the rating of your product. For example, if your product has a rating of IARC 7, but an image on the storefront has a rating of IARC 12, your product has a ratings mismatch.

You must update your submission by taking either of the following steps:

  • Adjust your storefront content and/or binary so it is aligned with your product's rating.
  • Retake the IARC questionnaire to update your product's rating, ensuring you provide accurate and complete information to the best of your ability. (Documentation: IARC Ratings)

Exceeds Ratings Limits

  • Main storefront content:
    • Your main storefront content must be suitable for general audiences. This means that the content on your product home page must not depict content that exceeds IARC 7 or ESRB Everyone 10+ or lower.
    • Note: "main storefront" refers to the Epic Games Store home page (store.epicgames.com) and the Discover tab in the Epic Games launcher. In the Developer Portal, the main storefront content consists of:
      • Offer titles
      • Offer images
  • Product home page:
    • The content on your product home page must not:
      • Exceed the age rating of your product.
      • Exceed an age rating of IARC 12 or ESRB Teen.
    • For example, if your product is rated IARC 16, the content on your product home page must not exceed IARC 12. If your product is rated IARC 7, the content on your product home page must not exceed IARC 7.
    • Note:
      • The product home page is the page on the Epic Games Store that showcases your product. For an example of a product home page, see Fortnite's product home page at store.epicgames.com/fortnite. In the Developer Portal, product home page content consists of:
        • All text (excluding the product name)
        • Carousel images and videos
        • Library images (Note: Library images are not displayed on your product home page, but are included here for the purposes of this policy.)
        • Product logo
        • Product icon
        • Additional images
  • The categories of content that are relevant to ratings include:
    • Fear or Gore
    • Violence
    • Controlled substances
    • Gambling
    • Language
    • Nudity and sexual content
    • Obscene content
  • Documentation:
    • Storefront Media Guide
    • Submit for Final Review > Receive the Review Results

Prohibited Content

Products that contain prohibited content cannot be distributed on the Epic Games Store. Epic Games reserves the right to decline or request changes to content that contains sensitive subject matter. This includes, but is not limited to, the following:

  • Products with an Adults Only (AO) Rating or Adult Only Content
    • Note: The Epic Games Store makes an exception for products that have an AO rating because they use blockchain, NFT, or cryptocurrency technology with no other AO content. (Blockchain, NFT, and cryptocurrency is known as “blockchain technology”. See the following glossary entry for a full definition: blockchain technology.) However, these products must comply with the store's blockchain technology and content guidelines, including the prohibitions on pornography, gambling, and hateful content. (Documentation: Blockchain Technology Guidelines.)
  • Hateful or Abusive Content
    • Contains content that promotes hatred, abuse, racism, or discrimination against groups or individuals.
    • Depictions of — or allusions to — self-harm.
    • Content that promotes terrorist or extremist organizations.
    • Real-life threats.
    • Real-life harassment.
    • Real-life animal abuse.
  • Inappropriate Content
    • Contains explicit or frequent depictions of sexual behavior that are not appropriately labeled, rated, or age-gated.
    • Pornography.
  • Illegal content
    • Contains content that violates the laws in your jurisdiction.
  • Child Exploitation
    • Contains content that exploits children in any way.
  • Fraud or Malware
    • Product attempts to gather sensitive information or modifies your computer in unexpected or harmful ways.
    • Scams, frauds, or deceptive practices, including impersonation of Epic Games.
    • Promotion of cheat mechanisms, exploits, or glitches.
  • Infringement of Intellectual Property and other protected rights
    • Content that infringes on the rights of others, including intellectual property or protected interests.
    • Content that you do not own or have the authorization to use.
  • Gambling, including but not limited to:
    • A product where a user stakes or wagers something of real-world value on an outcome determined by chance, skill, or both to win something of real-world value.
      • “Real-world value” includes real money, cryptocurrency, NFTs, other blockchain-based assets, and digital assets that can be exchanged for any other item with real-world value.

Linked-to Content

When you configure a product, you can provide external content links that display on your product home page. These external links could be to social media pages or your product's privacy policy.

If you provide a link to external content, that linked-to content must follow these requirements:

  • All links must:
    • Be consistent with the content in your store presence.
    • Be publicly accessible through a direct URL link and not result in a forced sign-in or error. For example, you may not provide a link to a downloadable PDF or to a web-hosted PDF.
    • Follow the content requirements for Epic Games Store pages listed in the rest of this document.
    • Use the dedicated fields provided when configuring your product home page in the Developer Portal. You may add links to your product's or company website's top-level domain (for example “epicgames.com” and not “dev.epicgames.com”), social media, support page, and privacy policy. You can also add any of these links to your About section or banner, but they must be present in the respective dedicated field.
      • For example, your social media link must be in the dedicated social media field. However, you can link to your social media in the About section as long as the same link is present in the dedicated social media field.
      • Documentation: Configure Store Settings > Social Media
  • Your product website link must:
    • Direct to your product's or company website's top-level domain (for example “epicgames.com” and not “dev.epicgames.com”).
  • Social media links must:
    • Link out to the product's or organization's official channel on the platform's official domain. For example, your YouTube link must direct users to your YouTube channel. In Fortnite's product home page, the Youtube link points to: https://www.youtube.com/epicfortnite.
    • By-invitation social links should not be set to expire.
  • Web Shops
    • Your product must follow these requirements:
      • Your product must be publicly accessible through a direct URL link.
      • Your product must be consistent with the content in your Epic Games Store presence.
    • If your product is not available on the Epic Games Store and you've configured a Web Shop offer, we strongly recommend that you create an FAQ page with the question "How can I purchase the base game product?". In the answer to this question, link to wherever your product is purchasable.

Notes:

  • Blockchain products have certain restrictions for external content. If your product does not use blockchain technology, you must remove all mentions of blockchain technology in your linked-to content. (Documentation: Blockchain Technology Guidelines.)
  • When they review your product submission, the Epic Games Store team rejects links if the external content seems suspicious or otherwise puts the privacy or security of Epic Games Store users at risk. For example, links are not permitted if they redirect to products or services that are not hosted on domains owned by you or your company, including the domain of a cloud-based application provider, a file-sharing website, or a collaborative document service. (Documentation: Configure Store Settings > Manage Product Configuration.)

Content on Your Product Home Page

You must follow the requirements in this section on your product home page.

Text Elements

About Section and Short Description

In your product's About section (Documentation: Manage Pages > About) and your offer's short description (Documentation: Offer Details > General Details), you must:

  • Only describe your product's or offer's gameplay, features, and experience.
  • Only reference and promote your other products if your other products are available on the Epic Games Store.
  • Not reference or promote other third-party storefronts.
  • Not reference products that are not on the Epic Games Store, except for the situations described below.

The following list contains the situations where you are allowed to reference your other products that are not on the Epic Games Store. You must clearly state to users that your product is not available on the Epic Games Store in your product's FAQ page. (Documentation: Manage Pages > Configure Your FAQ Page.)

  • The product has a web shop on the Epic Games Store but your base product offer is not available on the Epic Games Store. (Documentation: Web Shop Guidelines).
  • The product has crossplay functionality and you want to mention the other platforms your product is available on.
  • The product has features that the Epic Games Store does not currently support.

The policy above only applies to text on the storefront. For the policy regarding videos and other media, see the Media Asset Policies section of this page.

Media Asset Policies

In addition to the prohibited content guidelines described above, the following policies apply to videos and images:

  • All media assets:
    • If your media asset references other third-party platforms, you must either:
      • Include a reference to the Epic Games Store with the following legal notice:
        • ©2026 Epic Games, Inc. All rights reserved. Epic, Epic Games, Epic Games Store and their respective logos are trademarks or registered trademarks of Epic Games, Inc. In the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office and elsewhere.
      • Remove the reference to the other platform(s) entirely.
  • Video assets:
    • Must not have a black cover image. This is to ensure that the thumbnail in the carousel does not appear blank.
    • Must be owned by you; you must not upload videos owned by a third-party, such as a content creator.
    • If you include links in your video, the linked-to content must follow our Linked-to Content policies. (Documentation: Content Requirements > Linked-to Content)
    • Must not include content ratings from a ratings authority.
  • Image assets:
    • Can include links but they must follow our Linked-to Content policies above.
    • Offer and Library images:
      • Must include your product logo, which clearly displays the product name.
      • Must have your key art and logo within the critical area of the image.
    • Product logos:
      • Must be optimized for landscape aspect ratio and not contain a significant amount of negative space.
      • Must be legible and clearly visible on a dark page background.

Product Names and URLs

Product URLs affect how users find and search for your product on the Epic Games Store and through external search engines. Review the requirements below to ensure your requested update aligns with our guidelines.

Note that if you change your product URL, users are not automatically redirected from the old URL to the new one. If a user accesses the old URL, such as through a saved bookmark, it will result in an error message.

  • Your product URL must be the same as your product name.
    • Note: In some scenarios, your Product URL and product name may not match. For example, you might have used a temporary title or codename prior to product release and have now finalized your product name.
  • If your product URL and product name do not match, contact Epic Games for assistance. Do this by creating a private discussion at eoshelp.epicgames.com. Changes may take up to 2 weeks.
  • Your product URL must be localized in English.
  • Your product name and product URL cannot include promotional or marketing language.
  • Product name changes should be infrequent and only out of necessity.

Blockchain Policies

Products that include blockchain technology are allowed on the Epic Games Store if you have signed the Blockchain Addendum. (Documentation: Blockchain Technology Guidelines)

Digital Rights Management

The Epic Games Store does not require products to include digital rights management. You can implement third-party digital rights management at your discretion.

Localization Policies

The following policies apply to all products distributed on the Epic Games Store, including products that are not localized to English. Localizing your product fully into English is optional. However, your product home page must meet the following English-language requirements:

  • Your product logo must be in English.
  • For video assets, the audio must be in English, subtitled to English, or exclude dialogue audio entirely.
  • All copy text must be in English.

Support for any additional languages is optional.

Documentation:

  • Supported Languages
  • Localize Your Store Presence
  • Storefront Media Guide > Localization

User-Generated Content

User-Generated Content (UGC) within your product is treated as unrated. The Epic Games Store treats unrated content as having a mature rating. It is your responsibility as the developer or publisher of the product to moderate UGC.

User-Reported Content

Storefront users can report content on any product home page, which is then subject to re-review and potential removal from the Epic Games Store. We might contact you to remove or adjust your content in order to comply with our policies if we find any violations.

Media Legal Requirements

You must include the text listed below, in the circumstances specified.

Place the following text on the physical packaging of Epic Games Store keys:

  • REQUIRES INTERNET AND FREE EPIC GAMES ACCOUNT TO ACTIVATE. Prior to purchase, please review Terms of Service and EULA for Epic Games account and store terms. If you do not agree with these terms, you should return this game unopened to your retailer in accordance with their return policy.

When you use the Epic Games Store Logo in any way (for example, on physical packaging or in promotional materials), include the following text:

  • Epic, Epic Games, Epic Games Store, the Epic Games Store logo, and Epic Online Services are trademarks and/or registered trademarks of Epic Games. All other trademarks are the property of their respective owners.

Consequences

Content that does not conform to these requirements might be subject to re-review by the Epic Games Store team and potential removal from the Epic Games Store. When reviewing content, we may look at a variety of factors, including the severity of the issue and whether this issue has happened before. Action taken by Epic Games can be anything from removing content, a warning, all the way up to a permanent account ban for more severe situations.

Appeals

If your content does not conform to these requirements, you must make modifications to address the feedback and resubmit your content for review. However, if you believe a mistake was made, you can challenge the Epic Games Store team's decision by making an appeal.

To appeal a decision, create a private discussion on the support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com

For the topic, select "Epic Games Store" and include the following details in your case:

  • Product name
  • Part of the product related to the appeal
  • Your reason for appeal
