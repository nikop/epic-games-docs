Epic-managed distribution on the Epic Games Store
2 mins to read

Only use this “Epic-managed distribution” section of the Epic Games Store documentation if your product has Epic-managed distribution.

What is Epic-managed distribution?

Epic-managed distribution is one of two methods to add your product to the Epic Games Store and configure its settings. The other method is self-managed distribution. Most products have self-managed distribution.

  • Epic-managed distribution

    The Epic Games Store team adds your product to the Epic Games Store and configures it on your behalf. In the Developer Portal, you can see only limited settings for your product.

  • Self-managed distribution

    You add your product to the Epic Games Store and configure it yourself. In the Developer Portal, you can see a full range of settings for your product including the following: product home page and offer pages, achievements, pricing, offers, artifacts and binaries.

Note: Different products that belong to the same organization might have different distribution methods.

How to Check Which Distribution Method Your Product Has

You can check which distribution method your product has by viewing the Epic Games Store settings available for it in the Developer Portal.

The settings available depend on the distribution method your product has. These are as follows:

  • Epic-managed distribution settings: Limited options: Builds, Pages, and Discount Management Tool.

  • Self-managed distribution settings: Many options, including: Release Management, Store Settings, Offers.

To see your product’s Epic Games Store settings, go to your product’s Developer Portal page.

To do this:

  1. In the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal), in the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product. (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)

  2. When you are in your product's Developer Portal page, in the left navigation, click Epic Games Store.

  3. The settings for your product’s distribution method are listed in the left navigation.

Distribution Agreements

You can see your distribution agreements on the Agreements tab of your Organization page in the Developer Portal.

To do this:

  1. In the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal), in the left navigation, click Organization. (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.

  2. On the Organization page, click Agreements and scroll down to Epic Games Store Distribution.

  3. Click the three dots ( … ) on the right-hand side of the Epic Games Store Distribution entry to see your viewing options.

Notes:

  • The distribution agreements do not reference "Epic-managed distribution" or “Self-managed distribution”.

  • Your organization might have multiple agreements, relating to both distribution methods.
