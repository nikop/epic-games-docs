View Your Localized Content

Use the Developer Portal to view your localized content.

1 min to read

To view your product's localized content:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Localization page. To do this:

    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

    • b. Select the correct organization for your product.

      (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the on-screen instructions.)

    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.

    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Localization.

  2. Click the name of a page, offer, or set of store settings to access its Manage Localization page.
  3. In the panel on the right, click the language you want to view.
  4. In the panel on the right, click the type of localization you want to view, such as Text, Offer images, or About.

View Your Localization Upload History

You can view the history of the localization templates that your organization has uploaded. This includes details such as file names, upload dates, locales added, statuses of uploads (in other words, if an upload was successful or had issues), and any associated errors.
