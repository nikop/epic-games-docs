Sales Campaigns Playbook

Overview of the campaigns you can use to sell your product.

3 mins to read

Epic Games runs a number of sales campaigns throughout the year. You can also create your own sales campaign or participate in evergreen campaigns like Deals of the Week. Promote your product and gain exposure by joining campaigns that fit your product and release strategy.

Use the sales campaign guides below to:

  • Find out about our annual sales campaigns.
  • Learn details of each campaign’s size and the type of campaign available.
  • Develop a discount strategy for your product and craft an effective approach to sell your product.

Epic Games Sales Campaigns

Tentpole Campaigns

  • Two campaigns annually: MEGA Sale and Holiday Sale.
  • Four weeks long.
  • High traffic with new users.
  • Includes unique user incentives.

Seasonal Campaigns

  • Multiple campaigns annually: Peak Season and Special Events.
  • Two weeks long.
  • Good sales cadence throughout the year.
  • Hit sales beats in popular holiday periods.
  • Ideal for your first discount.
  • Align with your product release beats.

  • Ideal for your first discount.

Tentpole Campaigns

Drive millions of excited users to the Epic Games Store and help engage them with thousands of amazing deals. Tentpole campaigns are Epic Games biggest campaigns of the year and run twice annually:

  • MEGA Sale—Epic Games' biggest sale of the year, with a four-week campaign, thousands of discounts, and other engaging content for our users.
  • Holiday Sale—The premier event of the year, with thousands of discounts, weekly giveaways, free games, and unique user incentives.

Available to all partners and content.
Keep up to date with the latest campaigns. Documentation: Keep Up to Date With Sales and Promotional Opportunities.

Seasonal Campaigns

Join well-known seasonal sales campaigns that line up perfectly with worldwide holidays and events. Highly anticipated by our users, these include:

  • Lunar New Year
  • Spring Sale
  • Summer Sale
  • Autumn Sale
  • Halloween Sale

Ideal for the first discount on your product. Use seasonal campaigns to align your sales campaign with your product release beats, and popular holiday periods. Combine your seasonal campaigns with other campaigns like Evergreen Campaigns and Flex Campaigns to make sure you have a good sales cadence throughout the year.

Available to all partners and content.

Evergreen Campaigns

Ideal for the first discount on your product or for increasing engagement with your product. Promote your product as part of the Deals of the Week experience, positioned on our homepage. Use Evergreen Campaigns to align your sales campaign with your product release beats between other campaigns. Gain more exposure by raising the profile of products outside the Tentpole Campaigns.

Evergreen Campaigns (branded as Deals of the Week) are part of Epic Games Store evergreen campaign experience:

  • New products every Tuesday.
  • Runs all year, except during the Holiday Sale.
  • Submit your product with the Discount Management Tool.
  • Submit two months before the campaign or the day before.
  • See your product across multiple premium shelf spaces.
  • Test your catalog performance between campaigns.

Available to all partners and content.

Check eligibility and timelines for your product. Documentation: Discounts.

Flex Campaigns

Not all big campaigns are optimal for all types of content. Participate in smaller, more frequent Flex Campaigns to help users discover your content and optimize your catalog. Flex Campaigns (branded as Epic Savings) run between price cool-down windows of the Tentpole and Seasonal Sale Campaigns.

  • Test how your catalog performs in Flex Campaigns.
  • Compare the results with other campaigns.
  • Maximize your performance.

Available to all partners and content.
Keep up to date with the latest campaigns. Documentation: Keep Up to Date With Sales and Promotional Opportunities.
Check eligibility and timelines for your product. Documentation: Discounts.

Custom Discounts

Sales created by you. Use the Discount Management Tool to create your own custom sales beats in the Epic Games Store. Run full catalog sales, franchise sales, and discounts on launch of your product to engage with your Epic Games Store user base.

  • Manage discounts, sales windows, and the catalog yourself.
  • Attract users looking for a discount.
  • When you take part in certain preset campaigns, you might not be eligible to join later campaigns, in line with our cooldown period policy. Make sure you are familiar with these guidelines before you take part in a preset campaign. Documentation: Manage Discounts–Discount Guidelines.
  • Preset campaigns on the Epic Games Store usually start and end at 11.00 am ET / 8.00 am PT / 5.00 pm CET / 4.00 pm BST / 3.00 pm UTC.
