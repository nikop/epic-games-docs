Copy a Localized Rich Presence Template to Another Deployment

How to copy a localized rich presence template from one deployment to another.

1 min to read

Use localized rich presence templates to define localized text for custom status messages (rich presence) that your product displays to your users' friends. For more information, see Localize Your Game's Rich Presence Messages—Introduction.

Every template is specific to a deployment, but you can copy templates between deployments.

Note: If you want the template to be different in the target deployment, you can edit it in the target deployment. This does not affect the template in the original deployment because the original and the copy are completely separate.

To copy a template to another deployment:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

  2. Select the correct organization for your product.

    (To check which organization is selected, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the on-screen instructions.)

  3. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.

  4. In the left navigation, click Epic Account Services > Localized Presence.

  5. In the left navigation, click Epic Online Services.

  6. Under Accounts & Social, click Localized Presence.

  7. In Deployment, select the appropriate deployment.

  8. In the list of templates, locate the template you want to copy.

    • You can search the list. See documentation: Find a Specific Template.

  9. For that template , click the three dots ( … ).

  10. Click Copy to another deployment.

  11. In Deployment, select the deployment you want to copy to.

    • Note: If there is a template with the same name in the destination deployment, then the template you copy overwrites the template in the destination deployment.

  12. Click Copy.
