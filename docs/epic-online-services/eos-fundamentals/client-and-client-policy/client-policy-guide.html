Guide: Manage Your Clients and Client Policies

A guide on how to manage EOS clients and client policies.

7 mins to read

A client is software that accesses any Epic Online Services (EOS) service. Players download and run a client to play a game that uses EOS services. (Note that throughout this guide "game" also means "non-game software application" and "player" also means "user"). In addition, a trusted server can be a client.
For more information on types of clients, see the Client Types section of the Client and Client Policy Reference documentation.

Before you set up clients, you must first set up a client policy. A client policy defines what EOS services and other EOS features a client can access, such as Achievements, Stats, Leaderboards, and more. You set up both clients and client policies in the Developer Portal. You also assign a client policy to a client in the Developer Portal.

This guide describes how to:

  • Set up a client policy and avoid common pitfalls.
  • Set up a client (you can only do this after you have set up a client policy).
  • Edit clients and custom client policies.

Use this guide if you are integrating Epic Online Services (EOS) into your game and need to give your game's clients access to EOS services and other EOS features. For a detailed list of the EOS services and EOS features available for client policies, see the Client and Client Policy Reference documentation.

For a full list of the available guides, see the How-to Guides page.

Set Up a Client Policy

Client Policy Types

There are two client policy types. These are as follows:

  • Predefined Client Policy
    Gives your game's clients access to a ready-selected collection of EOS services and other features.
    Note: You cannot modify a predefined client policy.
  • Custom Client Policy
    You define the policy. Select which EOS services and other features your game's clients can access. We recommend this policy type for production (live) environments so you have fine-grained control over your game's client access to EOS services and other features.

This guide covers both of these client policy types.

Before You Start

Before you can set up a client policy, you must complete these steps:

  • Set up an Epic Games account, organization, and product in the Developer Portal.
    (Documentation: Developer Portal > Introduction > Sign In)
  • Have a role in your organization that can edit client policies.
    (Documentation: Developer Portal > Organizations > Roles)
  • Sign any required agreements for EOS services that you use in your game. (For example, Anti-Cheat or Ecom.)
    • You can check your agreements in the Developer Portal on the Agreements tab of the Organization page.
      To do this:
      1. Sign in to the Developer Portal at dev.epicgames.com/portal.
      2. In the left navigation, click Organization.
      3. The default tab is Members. Click the Agreements tab.

Select a Predefined Client Policy

Note that if you use a predefined client policy, you cannot make changes to it at any time. To create your own client policy that you can make changes to, see the Create a Custom Client Policy section on this page.

You select a predefined client policy in the Developer Portal on the Product Settings page. To select a predefined client policy, follow the steps below.

To go to the Product Settings page:

  1. Go to your product's Developer Portal page:
    • In the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal), in the left navigation, under Your Products, click the name of your product.
      (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)
  2. When you are in your product's Developer Portal page, in the left navigation, click Product Settings.

On the Product Settings page:

  1. The default tab is SDK Download & Credentials. Click Clients.

  2. Scroll down to Client policies, and click Add new client policy.
    (Note that the Clients tab might show new agreements to accept. To see the Client policies section, scroll down.)

  3. In the Client policy name field, enter a name for the client policy. You can use any combination of alphanumeric characters, spaces, and special characters.

  4. From the Client policy type dropdown, select one of the predefined client policy types.
    For details about the available policies, see the documentation: Client and Client Policy Reference > Predefined Client Policy Types.

  5. Select the User required checkbox, unless the client is a trusted server.

    Warning: Never use a client ID and client secret tied to a trusted server policy with a public game build.

  6. Click Add new client policy.

Create a Custom Client Policy

Use a custom client policy to restrict client access to only the EOS services or other features that your game uses.

You create a custom client policy in the Developer Portal on the Product Settings page. To create a custom client policy, follow the steps below.

To go to the Product Settings page:

  1. Go to your product's Developer Portal page:
    • In the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal), in the left navigation, under Your Products, click the name of your product.
      (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)
  2. When you are in your product's Developer Portal page, in the left navigation, click Product Settings.

On the Product Settings page:

  1. The default tab is SDK Download & Credentials. Click Clients.

  2. Scroll down to Client policies, and click Add new client policy.
    Note that the Clients tab might show new agreements to accept. To see the Client policies section, scroll down.)

  3. In the Client policy name field, enter a name for the client policy.
    You can use any combination of alphanumeric characters, spaces, and special characters.

  4. From the Client policy type dropdown, select Create custom policy.

  5. Select the User required checkbox, unless the client is a trusted server.

    Warning: Never use a client ID and client secret tied to a trusted server policy with a public game build.

  6. Under Features, select the features you want to enable in your custom client policy.
    For details of the available features, see the documentation: Client and Client Policy Reference > Client Policy Features.

  7. Click Add new client policy.

Note: The EOS Authentication service works with a client policy that has no features enabled. Other services (such as EOS Achievements and EOS Stats) require a client policy with specific features enabled.

Set Up a Client

You must set up a client policy before you can set up a client.

For a game in a production (live) environment, we recommend that you create a custom client policy to grant your game's clients access to only the EOS services and other features that you intend to use in your game. For guidance, see the Create a Custom Client Policy section on this page.

You set up a client in the Developer Portal on the Product Settings page. To set up a client, follow the steps below.

To go to the Product Settings page:

  1. Go to your product's Developer Portal page:
    • In the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal), in the left navigation, under Your Products, click the name of your product.
      (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)
  2. When you are in your product's Developer Portal page, in the left navigation, click Product Settings.

On the Product Settings page:

  1. The default tab is SDK Download & Credentials. Click Clients.
    Note that the Clients tab might show new agreements to accept. If applicable, accept these agreements before continuing.)
  2. Scroll down to Clients, and click Add new client.
  3. In the Client Name field, enter a name for the client. You can use any combination of alphanumeric characters, spaces, and special characters.
    Tip: Choose a name that makes it clear that this is either a user client or a trusted server.
    (For more information on the types of clients, see the Client Types section of the Client and Client Policy Reference documentation).
  4. From the Client policy dropdown, select one of your existing client policies.
    • If you have not set up a client policy, there is no dropdown. Click Add new client policy and follow the on-screen instructions.
    • If you have already set up a client policy but want to set up a new one, click Create new client policy and follow the on-screen instructions.
    • If you're setting up a new client policy, you can either:
      • Create a custom client policy. (See the section on this page: Create a Custom Client Policy.)
      • Choose a predefined client policy. (See the section on this page: Select a Predefined Client Policy.)
  5. With your client policy selected, update the settings in the rest of the form if needed.
    The settings are:
    • Add the IP allow list for the trusted server.
    • Add the Epic Account Service Redirect URL.

      For details on these settings, see the Add New Client Settings section of the Client and Client Policy Reference documentation.
  6. Click Add new client.

Edit Clients or Custom Client Policies

Predefined Client Policies

You cannot edit predefined client policies. If you want to change a predefined client policy, you must create a new custom policy and assign the clients to it. For guidance on this, follow the steps in the Create a Custom Client Policy section on this page.

Clients and Custom Client Policies

To edit existing clients or custom client policies:

  1. Go to your product's Developer Portal page:
    • In the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal), in the left navigation, under Your Products, click the name of your product. (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)
  2. In the left navigation, click Product Settings.
  3. The default tab is SDK Download & Credentials. Click Clients.
  4. Next to your client or client policy, click the ellipsis (...) icon > Edit.
