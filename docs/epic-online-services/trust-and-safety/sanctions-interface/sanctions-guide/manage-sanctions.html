Guide: Sanction Players — 4. Manage Sanctions and Sanction Appeals

Step-by-step guide on how to manage sanctions and sanction appeals in the Developer Portal.

5 mins to read

Previous section: 3. Allow Players to Appeal Sanctions

In the Developer Portal, you can perform the following actions for sanctions and sanction appeals:

  • Edit sanctions.
  • Review sanction appeals.
  • View and download sanction data.
  • Allow players to appeal sanctions through an online form that Epic provides.
    • Note: This feature is disabled by default. So, you must enable it if you want to provide this method to your players.
    • For more information, see the following section of this guide: 3. Allow Players to Appeal Sanctions > Notes.

For more information on creating sanctions and sanction appeals, see the following sections of this guide:

  • Create sanctions, see 1. Set Up and Apply Sanctions
  • Create sanction appeals, see 3. Allow Players to Appeal Sanctions

Before You Start

Before you start, you must complete these steps:

  • Set up sanctions in the Developer Portal. (Section of this guide: 1. Set Up and Apply Sanctions.)

  • Verify that you have one of the following roles to manage sanctions and sanction appeals in the Developer Portal:

    • Admin
    • Player Moderation Tools

    For more information on roles, see the Roles (Developer Portal Access) documentation.

Manage Sanctions and Sanction Appeals in the Developer Portal

You manage sanctions and player sanction appeals in the Developer Portal, on the Sanctions page. To manage sanctions and sanction appeals, follow the steps below. (Note that in the Developer Portal, your game is usually known as a "product".)

To go to the Sanctions page:

  1. Go to your game's product page in the Developer Portal. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal at (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. In the left navigation, under Your Products, click the name of your game.
      • Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.
  2. In the left navigation, click Epic Online Services.
  3. In the Trust and Safety section, click Sanctions.

Note: The steps below suggest an order, but you do not need to complete them in order. You can complete the steps that you require.

Complete the following steps to manage sanctions and sanction appeals in the Developer Portal:

  • Step A: Edit Sanctions
  • Step B: Review Sanction Appeals
  • Step C: Download Sanction Data

Step A: Edit Sanctions

On the Sanctions page, follow these steps to edit a sanction:

  1. From the Deployment dropdown, select a deployment that is attached to the same sandbox where you created your sanctions.
  2. Use the Sanctions toggle below the bar graph to view the sanctions for your game.
    • By default, the Sanctions view displays active and pending sanctions, listed by the Reference ID, status, & issued date column.
      • Tip: Click the Filter by status dropdown to change the status you want to display, or click Filter to adjust the filters. For example, you can click Filter and select tags from the Tags dropdown so you just see the sanctions with those tags.
  3. In the Player column, click the PUID of the player whose sanctions you want to manage.
    • Tip: You can manage multiple sanctions at once by clicking the checkbox next to each sanction that you want to manage. Use the buttons that appear to bulk process the selected sanctions.
      When you click a Product User ID, the page displays all sanctions and sanction appeals for the player, including those that are pending, expired, and closed.
      • Note: By default, in the right pane, Sanctions is selected. You can manage other items from this pane as well, but that is outside the scope of this guide. For more information on the other options, see the following documentation:
        • For information on linked accounts, see Identity Provider Management.
        • For information on Anti-Cheat and detections, see Anti-Cheat Interfaces.
        • For information on reports, see Reports Interface.
  4. Click the three dots (...) next to the sanction that you want to edit and select one of the following options:
    • Activate: Changes the status of a sanction from Pending to Active.
    • Appeal: Opens an appeal for the sanction. Someone on your team can then review the appeal and either approve or deny it.
    • Edit: Edit the justification and tags for the sanction.
    • Close: Removes the sanction from the player.
      • Note: When you close a sanction, all linked sanction appeals for the sanction close automatically.
  5. Click Save & Exit to save the changes.

Step B: Review Sanction Appeals

On the Sanctions page, follow these steps to review sanction appeals:

  1. From the Deployment dropdown, select a deployment that is attached to the same sandbox where you created your sanctions.

  2. Use the Sanction Appeals toggle below the bar graph to view the sanction appeals for your game.
    By default, the Sanction Appeals view displays all sanctions appeals, including closed sanction appeals.

    • Tip: Click Filter to adjust the filters. For example, you can click Filter and select a specific status to see only the sanction appeals with that status.

  3. In the Player column, click the PUID of the player whose sanction appeals you want to manage.

    • Tip: You can manage multiple sanction appeals at once by clicking the checkbox next to each sanction appeal that you want to manage. Use the buttons that appear to bulk process the selected sanction appeals.
      When you click a Product User ID, the page displays all sanctions and sanction appeals for the player, including those that are pending, expired, and closed.
      • Note: By default, in the right pane, Sanctions is selected. You can manage the other items from this pane as well, but that is outside the scope of this guide. For more information on the other options, see the following documentation:
        • For information on linked accounts, see Identity Provider Management.
        • For information on Anti-Cheat and detections, see Anti-Cheat Interfaces.
        • For information on reports, see Reports Interface.

  4. Click the three dots (...) next to the sanction appeal that you want to review and select Review. You can take the following actions:

    • Change the status of the appeal:
      • Open: The appeal is ready for review.
      • In progress: The appeal is under review.
      • Approve: Closes the sanction appeal and associated sanction, removing the sanction from the player.
      • Deny: Keeps the associated sanction active and closes the sanction appeal.
    • Check the Assign to me option to assign yourself to review the sanction later.

  5. Click Save & Exit to save the changes.

Step C: Download Sanction Data

On the Sanctions page, a bar graph tracks sanctions by action, per day or hour. Follow these steps to download the sanction data:

  1. From the Deployment dropdown, select a deployment that is attached to the same sandbox where you created your sanctions.
  2. Use the Daily or Hourly toggle above the bar graph to switch the bar graph to the time interval that you want to view the data in.
    • Note: The interval you choose determines the interval of the data that you download.
  3. Click .csv above the bar graph to download the bar graph data.
