3. Finalize and Test Configurations

Epic Games Store - Get Started Steps - 3. Finalize and Test Configuration.

11 mins to read

Mobile Early Adopter: Do not use this page. Go to the Mobile Early Adopter—3. Finalize and Test Configurations page for guidance.

Previous step: 2. Publish Your Product Home Page

Complete all of the configurations required to release your product. This includes the following tasks:

  • Create offers.
  • Upload your product's binary files (executable code file and any supporting files) to the Epic Games Store.
  • Test to ensure your product and store presence are ready for launch.
  • Submit your product for review by the Epic Games Store team.

Estimated time to complete: Varies per product depending on specific configurations needed.

Prerequisites:

  • Complete Step 1. Onboarding.
  • Review the release management documentation to find out about the sandbox environments where you configure artifacts and binaries, settings, offers, and pages relating to your product. Documentation: Release Management.

Before you begin:

To complete this step, prepare the following information:

  • A list of all offers (for example, editions or season passes) that you plan to apply to your product.
  • For all offers, prepare the following information:
    • Metadata (for example, genre and features), distribution regions, and pricing information.
    • Storefront media and text.
    • Localized storefront content. (Epic Games offers a free localization service for some text, details are below.)
      • Recommended: Localized text
      • Optional: Localized media
  • FAQ page content, if applicable.
    • Tip: FAQs are optional for most products, however you must provide them in certain situations. For example, FAQs are required for early access products, products using blockchain or NFT technology, or products featuring in-app purchases.
      For more information, see documentation: Epic Games Store distribution requirements.
  • Your product's binary files. (A file or set of files known as "a binary" or "binaries" interchangeably.)
    • Your product's binary files are the executable code and any supporting files that users need to run your product. For example, any of the following types of file can be a binary file: executable file, metadata file, configuration file.
    • In the Developer Portal and Epic Games Store, the terms "a binary" or "binaries" are interchangeable, regardless of how many binary files your product requires.
    • Note: Ensure your product's binary files meet the technical distribution requirements for the Epic Games Store and adheres to content guidelines.
      For information on this, see documentation: Requirements and Guidelines.

Process

Configure the settings and complete the tasks listed below.

Note: If you completed Step 2. Publish Your Product Home Page (Early Awareness), you might have already completed some of the tasks described in this section.
If this is the case, as you prepare for launch, make sure you do the following two tasks from the full list of tasks in this section:

  • B. Base Product Offer: Update your base product offer (especially the available date).
  • G: Binaries and Artifacts: Upload your product's binary files (executable code file and any supporting files, known as a "binary" or "binaries").

To do this:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal), and make sure you are in the correct organization for your product.
    (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation in the Developer Portal. To change organization, click Organization and follow the onscreen instructions.)
  2. On the left navigation, under Your products, select the name of your product.
  3. Follow the process steps below.

A. Store Settings: Product Configuration, Regions and Ratings

Documentation:

  • Store Settings.

To configure store settings, go to the Store Settings page. To do this:

  1. On the left navigation, click Store Settings.
  2. On the Store Settings page, locate the Product configuration tile and click Edit.
  3. Follow the onscreen instructions to add essential details about your product.
    This section collects details and specifications for your product as a whole. If individual offer details differ from your overall product information, you can provide those details separately when you configure the offers.
  4. Close the Product Configuration page. On the Store Settings page, locate the Regions and ratings tile and click Edit.
  5. Follow the onscreen instructions to obtain an IARC rating (or upload traditional ratings) and select the regions in which you plan to distribute your product. Epic Games Store does not require an IARC rating for your product, but we recommend it has one because it offers the best reach potential users.

B. Base Product Offer

Documentation:

  • Manage Offers.

An offer is an item you sell in the store. Your base product offer is the product you sell, such as the game Fall Guys without any additional items (for example: store.epicgames.com/~fall-guys).

Additional Offers To configure offers, go to the Offers page. To do this:

  1. On the left navigation, click Offers.
    The Offers page defaults to the Sandbox: Dev tab. Your product is listed in the Dev tab with an automatically-generated base product offer. Your product is assigned the default type, Game.
  2. Click your product's name.
  3. Follow the onscreen instructions.

Epic Games Store also provides additional offers, such as: edition, demo, add-on, season passes, consumable, digital extra, chapters, or whatever you might add to your product.

Create Your Base Product Offers (“Base Game Offers”)

You must create and configure your base product offers before adding any additional offers. To create your base product offers, go to the Offers page. To do this:

  1. On the left navigation, click Offers.
  2. On the Offers page, click Create Offer.
  3. In the Create Offer panel click Create to create the base product offer (written as Base Game Offer in the Create Offer panel) for your chosen platform. (Note: “PC” in the Developer Portal means “Windows”.)
    Note that platforms do not share a base offer: you must create an individual base product offer for PC (Windows, macOS). (You can have up to three base product offers.)
  4. In the Offer’s page, your new offer is listed as a Base game (this means the base product offer for the platform you chose) and it is in the Dev sandbox. You must configure every base product offer you have created.
    Click on your product's name.
  5. In the Offer Details panel, follow the onscreen instructions to add details. Note that you must complete the Required Information section of your base product offer in order to publish your product.
    There are two sections to complete: Click General Details to open the General Details panel and click Dates to open the Offer Dates panel.
  6. Set the launch Date Type to Specific and provide the date and time you plan to release your product.
    For guidance on specifying other types of dates, see the following documentation: Manage Offers.
  7. Make sure you configure every base product offer you have created.

A checkmark next to a section tile in the Offer Details panel indicates that you have added details.

When you have created your base product offers, you can create additional offers for your product as required.

Note: If you already published your product home page for early awareness (in Mobile Early Adopter—2: Publish Your Product Home Page (Early Awareness)), without attaching a binary to your base product offer, you must update its available date type to Specific and provide the date and time you plan to release your product. You must do this or you can't release your product.

C. Product Home Page

Documentation:

  • Manage Pages.

To configure your product home page, go to the Pages page. To do this:

  1. On the left navigation, click Pages.
    The Pages page defaults to the Sandbox: Dev tab. Your product home page is listed in the Dev tab.
  2. Click on the page name, Product home.
  3. Follow the onscreen instructions to upload media assets for your product’s store presence.
    • Required:
      • Carousel: Upload videos and images to the featured media section that displays front and center on your product home page. To best reach users, we recommend that you include a video trailer for your product in the first position of the carousel.
      • About: Provide additional text that further describes your product or highlights certain features.
    • Optional:
      • Additional Images: Add images to a collapsible media grid on your product home page.
      • Social Preview: Specify images to use for posts on social media about your product.

D. Pages for Additional Offers

Documentation:

  • Manage Pages - Publishing a Page for an Offer.

Pages are automatically generated for each offer associated with your product. You can configure the pages for any additional offers you have created for your product on the Pages page. To do this:

  1. On the left navigation, click Pages.
    The Pages page defaults to the Sandbox: Dev tab.
  2. From the Page name list, locate the page you want to edit and click the three dots (...) and Edit page.
  3. Follow the onscreen instructions to add text and media assets.

E. FAQ Page

Documentation:

  • Manage Pages - Create an FAQ Page.
  • Distribution Requirements.

FAQ pages are optional for most products; however, you must add an FAQ for the following product types:

  • Products launched as early access titles.
  • Products using blockchain or NFT technology.
  • Products containing in-app purchases.

Add an FAQ, if applicable, on the Pages page. To do this:

  1. On the left navigation, click Pages.
    The Pages page defaults to the Sandbox: Dev tab.
  2. Click Create page, and select FAQ page.
  3. Follow the onscreen instructions to upload questions and answers about your product.

F. Binaries and Artifacts

Documentation:

  • Manage Artifacts

You must upload your product's binaries and attach them to an artifact.

What are binaries and artifacts?

  • Binaries
    Your product's binary files are the executable code file and any supporting files that users need to run your product.
    In the Developer Portal and Epic Games Store, binary files are known as "a binary" or "binaries". The terms "a binary" and "binaries" are interchangeable, regardless of how many binary files your product requires.
    The following are examples of binary files:
    • Types of file examples: executable file, metadata file, configuration file.
    • Types of content examples: offers, editions, season passes, chapters, or any other content you want users to download.
  • Artifact
    An artifact represents a single downloadable piece of content in your product, such as a game client or mod editor. You can attach multiple binary files to an artifact.

You must upload binaries and attach them to artifacts for your base product offer in order to complete Step 4. Submit for Final Review.

There are three sub-steps:

  • Check binaries
  • Upload binaries and attach to an artifact
  • Test binaries
Check Binaries

Documentation:

  • Requirements and Guidelines

Before you upload your binaries, ensure your product's binary files meet the technical distribution requirements for the Epic Games Store and adhere to content guidelines using the Requirements and Guidelines documentation.

Upload Binaries and Attach to an Artifact

Documentation:

  • BuildPatch tool

To upload your binaries and attach them to artifacts, you use the BuildPatch tool. You download the BuildPatch tool from the Artifacts and Binaries page. To do this:

  1. On the left navigation, click Artifacts and Binaries.
  2. Click Download build patch tool.
  3. Use the BuildPatch Tool to prepare and upload your product's binaries.
    For guidance on using the tool, see documentation: BuildPatch tool.
  4. Associate the binaries you upload with the appropriate artifacts in the Artifacts and Binaries page.
  5. Test your binaries (see below).
Test Binaries

Documentation:

  • Epic Games Store Testing Guide
  • Access Keys (required to test the binaries).

To ensure your build functions properly in the Epic Games Launcher and meets the requirements for launch, we strongly recommend you test your binaries by downloading and installing them through the Epic Games Launcher. For guidance, see documentation: Epic Games Store Testing Guide.

Note: When you test, in order to redeem an offer in private sandboxes, anyone testing must be a part of your organization and use a testing access key to access the artifact.

G. Optional: Localize

Documentation:

  • Localize Store Presence
  • Localize Store Presence—Request Text Translations from the Epic Translation Service

To configure your product's localization, go to the Localization page. To do this:

  1. On the left navigation, click Localization.
    The Localization page defaults to the Sandbox: Dev tab.
  2. Click the Store Settings, Product home page, Offer, or FAQ page you want to localize.
  3. In the Manage Localization pages, follow the onscreen instructions to upload localized text and media.
    Notes:
    • Epic Games does not require localization for all products. However, we recommend it to best reach global users. The Epic Games Store currently supports 32 different locales.
    • If you are unable to provide your own translations, the Epic Games Store offers a translation service to localize your product home page text free of charge. (See documentation: Localize Store Presence—Request Text Translations from the Epic Translation Service.)

H. Push to Stage

After you have configured these settings and completed the tasks, you can push all artifacts and binaries, settings, offers, and pages that you want to include in your product launch to the Stage sandbox. This is the final sub-step before you submit your product for review by the Epic Games Store team.

To push to the Stage sandbox, go to the Release Management page. To do this:

  1. On the left navigation, click Release Management.
  2. On the Release Management page, in the Dev sandbox tab, click Push To Stage.
  3. Select all the artifacts and binaries, settings, offers, and pages that you want to include in your product launch. If you have additional offers or pages that are not yet ready for distribution, leave them unchecked.
  4. Click Push to Stage. This action may take a few minutes to complete.
    Note: Some artifacts and binaries, settings, offers, and pages might have dependencies that prevent you from pushing them to Stage. You cannot push these artifacts and binaries, settings, offers, and pages until all dependencies are in the Ready for Stage status. During this step, ensure you select all dependencies for the artifacts and binaries, settings, offers, and pages included in your product launch.
