Keep Up to Date With Sales and Promotional Opportunities

Find out about upcoming sales and promotional opportunities run by the Epic Games Store.

Under 1 min to read

There are three ways to make sure you're informed about upcoming sales and promotional opportunities:

  • Subscribe to the Epic Games Store Discounting News and Updates newsletter. This newsletter provides information about upcoming campaigns and discount submission timelines. This is the best way to stay up to date with news about promotions. For details, see Subscribe to the Epic Games Store Discounting News and Updates Newsletter on this page.
  • Check the Discount Management Tool to see upcoming promotional opportunities. We recommend that you do this at least once a month.
  • Visit the Epic Games community portal for news about upcoming promotions and quarterly promotional guides: dev.epicgames.com/community.

Subscribe to the Epic Games Store Discounting News and Updates Newsletter

To subscribe to the Epic Games Store Discounting News and Updates newsletter:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. Select the correct organization for your product.
  3. In the left navigation, scroll down to the bottom and select Notifications.
  4. At the top of the Notifications panel, click the three dots (...).
  5. Select Settings.
  6. In the panel on the right, open the Epic Games Store dropdown section.
  7. Select EGS discounting news and updates.
  8. At the bottom right, click Save.
