Test a Localized Rich Presence Template

How to test a localized rich presence template.

Under 1 min to read

Use localized rich presence templates to define localized text for custom status messages (rich presence) that your product displays to your users' friends. For more information, see Localize Your Game's Rich Presence Messages—Introduction.

To test a localized rich presence template:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

  2. Select the correct organization for your product, if prompted.

    (To check which organization is selected, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the on-screen instructions.)

  3. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.

  4. In the left navigation, click Epic Online Services.

  5. In the Accounts & Social section, click Localized Presence.

  6. In the list, find the template you want to test.

  7. Click the downwards arrow to expand the template details:

  8. Choose a locale, and click test:

    The left-hand pane shows a preview of the text in the following locales:

    • The player view locale: The locale for the player whose status is reported by the message.
    • The observer view locale: The locale for the player who receives the message.

  9. In the right-hand pane, you can switch the template, the player locale, and the observer locale. You can also choose a value for each variable that appears in the text. Click Test to refresh the preview with the details you selected.
