Guide: Add Leaderboards to Your Game — 1. Set Up Leaderboards

Overview of how to add leaderboards to your game.

4 mins to read

Previous section: Introduction

To rank your players on leaderboards, you must first create the leaderboards in the Developer Portal.

Before You Start

Before you start, you must complete these steps:

  • Integrate the EOS SDK into your code. (Documentation: Guide: Get Started With EOS.)
  • Create stats in the Developer Portal. (Documentation: Guide: Add Stats to Your Game — 1. Set Up Stats.)

Note: We recommend that you set up the Logging Interface to receive useful messages that can help you diagnose technical issues with the EOS SDK. (Documentation: Logging Interface.)

Configure Your Game in the Developer Portal

Complete the following steps in the Developer Portal to set up the available EOS leaderboards for your game:

  • Step 1: Configure Your Game's Client Policy.
  • Step 2: Configure Your Game's Leaderboards.

Step 1: Configure Your Game's Client Policy

Note: This guide uses a preset client policy type for simplicity. For a game in a production environment, we recommend that you create your own custom client policy to ensure your game is secure. For more information on client policies, see the following documentation: Guide: Manage Your Clients and Client Policies.

You configure your game's client policy in the Developer Portal, on the Product Settings page's Clients tab. To do this, follow the steps below. (Note that in the Developer Portal, your game is usually known as a "product".)

To go to the Clients tab:

A. Go to your game's product page in the Developer Portal.

  • To do this:
    a. Sign in to the Developer Portal at (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    b. In the left navigation, under Your Products, click the name of your game (or software application).
    • Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.

B. In the left navigation, click Product Settings.
C. From the default tab (SDK Download & Credentials), click Clients.

Note that the Clients tab might show new agreements to accept.

On the Clients tab:

A. Scroll down to the Client policies section. (This is below the Clients section.)
B. Either create a new client policy or enable the Stats feature for an existing client policy.

  • To create a new client policy:
    a. Click Add new client policy. (Do not click “Add new client”.)
    b. For Client policy name, enter a name of your choice.
    c. For Client policy type, select GameClient w/ Unlock Achievements if you plan to use EOS achievements for your game. For more information about achievements, see the Achievements Interface documentation.
    d. Leave the default options and click Add new client policy. The preset client policy enables the Leaderboards feature by default.

  • To enable the Leaderboards feature for an existing client policy:
    a. Locate the client policy and click the three dots (...) next to it, then select Edit.
    b. In the Edit client policy pane, scroll down to Features and use the Leaderboards toggle to enable the leaderboards feature.

Step 2: Configure Your Game’s Leaderboards

You configure your game's leaderboards in the Developer Portal on the Leaderboards page. To do this, follow the steps below.

To go to the Leaderboards page:

A. Go to your game's product page in the Developer Portal.

  • To do this:
    a. Sign in to the Developer Portal at (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    b. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your game.
    • Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.

B. In the left navigation, click Epic Online Services.
C. In the Player & Game Data section, click Leaderboards.

On the Leaderboards page:

A. From the Deployment dropdown, select a deployment that is attached to the same sandbox where you created your stats. Stats and leaderboards you create are shared for all deployments in a sandbox.
B. Click Create Leaderboard.
C. In the Leaderboard Name field, enter a name for the new leaderboard. Choose a name that is meaningful to you, because you will refer to this leaderboard name in your game code.

  • Note: The name can include only letters, numbers, or the underscore character (_) with no spaces.

D. From the Stat dropdown, select the stat to track for the leaderboard.
E. Under Timespan, set the Start Date and (optional) End Date for the leaderboard.

  • Notes:
    • Stat values ingested outside the start and end timespan that you specify for the leaderboard are not included in the leaderboard.
    • Use the Never expire this leaderboard toggle to create a leaderboard without an end date.

Repeat these steps to create as many leaderboards as your game requires.

Results

You can see the leaderboards on your game’s Leaderboards page in the Developer Portal.

Notes

The total number of leaderboards you can create depends on how many stat milestones you have left to use for your deployment. For more information on usage limitations, including stat milestones, see the documentation: Leaderboards Reference > Usage Limitations.
