5. Release Your Product

Epic Games Store - Get Started Steps - 5. Release Your Product.

3 mins to read

Mobile Early Adopter: Do not use this page. Go to the Mobile Early Adopter—5. Release Your Product page for guidance.

Previous step: 4. Submit for Final Review

After your product is approved by the Epic Games Store team, you are ready to release your product on the Epic Games Store. You do this by pushing your product to the Live sandbox in the Developer Portal; we call this "publishing" your product. Publishing your product makes it available for purchase in the Epic Games Store. When you push your product to the Live sandbox, you can choose to either publish your product immediately or at a scheduled time.

Estimated time to complete: 15 minutes

Prerequisites:

  • Complete Step 1. Onboarding.
  • Complete Step 3. Finalize and Test Configurations.
  • Complete Step 4. Submit for Final Review.

Note: To release your product on the Epic Games Store, the Epic Games Store team must have approved your product submission. If you received feedback that there are "issues found" in your submission, you must complete the required updates and resubmit your product for final review.

Process

After your product is approved by the Epic Games Store team, release your product by publishing it on the Epic Games Store using the Release Management page. To do this:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal), and make sure you are in the correct organization for your product.

  2. In the left navigation, under Your products, select the name of your product.

  3. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store and then Release Management.

  4. Make sure your product's artifacts and binaries, settings, offers, pages, and web shop items in the Stage sandbox are in the Ready for Live status.

  5. In the Stage sandbox, click Push to Live.

  6. In the list of entities (artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, pages, and web shop items), select the entities that you want to publish.

    • At a minimum, you must push the following entities, if they are not already in your Live sandbox:
      • A base product offer
      • A set of store settings
      • A product home page
      • An artifact for the base product offer (you do not need to do this for a "coming soon" offer).
      • Any other entities that are mandatory for your product (for example, an FAQ page if your product is a PC (Windows, macOS) product in Early Access).
        • For information on mandatory entities, see documentation: Epic Games Store Requirements.

  7. To do an immediate publish:

    • At the bottom of the page, click Push to Live.

  8. To do a scheduled publish:

    • a. At the top left, enable the Schedule Publish toggle.
    • b. Enter the date and time you want to publish.
    • c. At the bottom of the page, click Schedule Publish.
    • For more information on scheduled publishing, see Scheduled Publishing.

Congratulations — you have launched your product on the Epic Games Store!

Notes:

  • The visibility of your product's page or pages depend on the dates you configured for your offers. For example, if an offer has dates set in the future, that offer is not available to users until that time.
  • If you push your product to the Live sandbox with a publish date set to a future time, your product is not visible on the storefront until that time. However, once your product is in the Live sandbox, it is indexable on the public web. Ensure you are ready for your product to be public information before pushing it to Live.
  • You can cancel a scheduled publish before the scheduled publish date. To do this, click the Cancel button on the Release Management page.

Analytics

Documentation:

  • Product Marketing Tips - Optimize Your Marketing Strategy With the Analytics Dashboard

You can monitor your product’s performance post-launch in the Developer Portal's Analytics page. To do this:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal), and make sure you are in the correct organization for your product.
    (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation in the Developer Portal. To change organization, click Organization and follow the onscreen instructions.)
  2. On the left navigation, under Your products, select the name of your product.
  3. On the left navigation, click Analytics.
