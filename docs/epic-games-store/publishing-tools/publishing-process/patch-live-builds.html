Patch Your Live Builds

Publish updated artifacts without additional review.

3 mins to read

In certain scenarios, you might need to update artifacts and binaries, settings, offers, offer pages, and product home pages that have gone through the review process and are in the Live sandbox.

To streamline this process, you can complete some types of updates without needing to resubmit your product for review.

Note: If you remove, add, or change a platform associated with your artifact, you must resubmit that artifact for review.

Publish One or More Artifacts Without Review

When you publish an artifact and do not put it through review, the artifact moves directly from the Dev sandbox to the Live sandbox in the Developer Portal. The Epic Games Store also updates the corresponding artifact in the Stage sandbox. You can promote multiple artifacts at the same time through this process.

Criteria to Publish Without Review

If you want to publish an artifact without review, the artifact must meet the following criteria:

  • You must have previously pushed the artifact to your Live sandbox. To do this, upload a new binary and set it as the active binary. (Documentation: Manage Artifacts > How to Upload Binaries and Configure Artifacts and Binaries)
  • The artifact must have the Ready for Stage status in the Dev sandbox. (For guidance on statuses, see documentation: Release Management > Status Definitions)
  • The artifact is required to fix broken functionality in your product. You cannot publish an artifact without review to add new functionality or to make changes that affect the product's age rating.

If the artifact does not meet the above criteria, then you must use the standard artifact update process. See documentation: Manage Artifacts.

How to Publish Without Review

Subject to the criteria listed above, you can publish one or more artifacts without review in the Developer Portal. To do this, follow the steps below:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to your product's Epic Games Store dashboard:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.
    • c. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store, then click Release Management.
  2. At the top of the Release Management page, click Make live update.
  3. In the Live Update pane, Click Artifacts and binaries.
  4. Select the artifact that you want to update.
  5. Click Next.
  6. Upload a new binary. The new binary displays in the Inactive section of the Live Update pane.
    • See documentation: Manage Artifacts > How to Upload Binaries and Configure Artifacts and Binaries
  7. In the Inactive section of the Live Update pane, click Set as active binary to the right of the new binary.
  8. In Set update type, select Bugfix.
    • Note: If you select any other type, then you cannot publish the artifact without review.
  9. Click Next.
  10. In the preview, check that the artifact is set up as you want.
  • Note: After you make the live update, you cannot make changes to the artifact.
  1. To update the artifact in your Stage sandbox in addition to updating the artifact in your Live sandbox, select the Push this change to the Stage Sandbox checkbox. If you don't want to update the artifact in your Stage sandbox, clear the checkbox.
  • Note: We recommend that you update the artifact in the Stage sandbox whenever you update the Live sandbox. This ensures your artifacts are synchronized between sandboxes.
  1. Click Make Live update.
  2. To confirm the update, click Continue.

Review your artifacts in the Live sandbox to confirm they have been successfully updated. To do so:

  1. In the Dev Portal, go to Your Product > Epic Games Store > Artifacts and Binaries.
  2. From the row of sandbox options, select Live:
  3. Locate the modified artifact in the list, and click its menu (...) icon.
  4. Select Manage Artifact.

Update Live Offer Dates

In certain scenarios, you might need to update offer dates for products that have already been pushed live (for example, to accommodate release schedule changes). See documentation: Offer Details > Dates.
