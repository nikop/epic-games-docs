Accounts and Social

Bring your community together and reach nearly a billion players with the Epic Games account ecosystem, powering friend management, rich presence information, streamlined crossplay, and a unified UI and UX with the Social Overlay. Deliver a consistent, connected experience across every platform and storefront on the engine of your choice.

Manage player accounts and social interactions in your game with the following EOS interfaces:

  • Friends: Enable players to invite friends to join a gameplay session. Retrieve and display friend information to players.
  • Presence: Display a player’s status (online, offline, playing) in a game.
  • Auth (EOS Epic Account Services authentication): Authenticate players, get authorization credentials (access tokens), and handle other Epic Games account-related interactions with Epic Online Services (EOS).
  • Progression Snapshot: Merge a player’s Epic Games account and proxy account.
  • UI: Implement a product-independent overlay window that displays status updates, shows or hides the overlay, and adjusts display and hotkey preferences.
  • User Info: Obtain information about a player, such as their display name or country.

Accounts and Social also includes the following features:

  • Epic’s Account Ecosystem: EOS Epic Account Services provide player accounts: either an Epic Games account or a proxy account. This enables your game to tap into a player account ecosystem of nearly a billion players across multiple platforms and stores.
  • Crossplay: Enable players accessing your game from different storefronts to play together using a combined friends list.
  • Social Overlay: Display to a player their achievements and friends information, including if their friends are playing the same game.
  • Localized Rich Presence: Localize the dynamic messages you display to players.

Accounts and Social are EOS Epic Account Services — players can connect to these services in your game with an Epic Games account or proxy account. For details, see Epic Online Services (EOS) Overview > Type of Service.
