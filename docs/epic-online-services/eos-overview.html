Epic Online Services (EOS) Overview

Epic Online Services (EOS) are free, cross-platform services for multiplayer games.

10 mins to read

Epic Online Services (EOS) are free, games-platform-agnostic services to launch, operate, and scale your game, whatever games platform your game clients run on, and whatever game engine you use for development, or if you use no game engine at all.

You integrate EOS services into your game by referencing service interfaces (SDK API or Web API) in your game code. There are multiple services, each with their own interface and set of APIs. The following are groups of services:

  • Accounts and Social
    Bring your players together with the Epic Games account ecosystem, tapping into a cross-platform network of nearly a billion players.
    • See the section on this page for more information: Accounts and Social.
    • Accounts and Social are EOS Epic Account Services — players can connect to these services in your game with an Epic Games account or proxy account. For details, see the section on this page: Type of Service.
  • Multiplayer
    Make every match possible, however your players choose to connect. Enable multiplayer experiences across diverse platforms and services.
    • See the section on this page for more information: Multiplayer.
    • Multiplayer services are EOS Game Services — players can connect to your game with an account from any supported identity provider. For details, see the section on this page: Type of Service.
  • Player and Game Data
    Keep progress persistent and increase long-term engagement. Securely manage and sync data across platforms.
    • See the section on this page for more information: Player and Game Data.
    • Player and Game Data are EOS Game Services — players can connect to your game with an account from any supported identity provider. For details, see the section on this page: Type of Service.
  • Trust and Safety
    Protect players, build trust, safeguard your game’s integrity, and navigate the complexities of parental consent and verification.
    • See the section on this page for more information: Trust and Safety.
    • Trust and Safety are EOS Game Services — players can connect to your game with an account from any supported identity provider. For details, see the section on this page: Type of Service.

Type of Service

There are two types of service:

  • EOS Game Services
  • EOS Epic Account Services

The type affects what player authentication interface you use and what player accounts can connect to the service.

EOS Game Services

Game Services use the Connect Interface to authenticate players. You can integrate Games Services into your game without any requirement for your players to have an Epic Games account. Players can connect to your game with an account from any supported identity provider, such as a Steam account, Google account, or Amazon account, among many others, including an Epic Games account. (See the Identity Provider Management documentation for a full list of providers.)

The following groups of services and their interfaces are EOS Game Services:

  • EOS Fundamentals
  • Multiplayer
  • Player and Game Data
  • Trust and Safety

Documentation:

  • Connect Interface (Use this interface to authenticate players using EOS Game Services.)

EOS Epic Account Services

Epic Account Services use the Auth Interface to authenticate players. To use Epic Account Services, players of your game must have an Epic Games account (or a proxy account).

The following group of services and their interfaces are EOS Epic Account Services:

  • Accounts and Social

Documentation:

  • Auth Interface (Use this interface to authenticate players using EOS Epic Account Services.)

EOS Services

Accounts and Social

Bring your community together and reach nearly a billion players with the Epic Games account ecosystem, powering friend management, rich presence information, streamlined crossplay, and a unified UI and UX with the Social Overlay. Deliver a consistent, connected experience across every platform and storefront on the engine of your choice.

EOS Epic Account Services
Accounts and Social are EOS Epic Account Services: EOS Epic Account Services use the Auth Interface to authenticate players. To use EOS Epic Account Services, players of your game must have an Epic Games account (or a proxy account).

Documentation:

  • Auth Interface

Epic's Account Ecosystem

EOS Epic Account Services provide player accounts: either an Epic Games account or a proxy account. This enables your game to tap into a player account ecosystem of nearly a billion players across multiple platforms and stores.

Crossplay

Enable players accessing your game from different storefronts to play together using a combined friends list.

Documentation:

  • Crossplay Introduction

Friends

Enable players to invite friends to join a game-play session, as well as retrieve and display player-friend information. Your game can display to any signed-in player their friends and their invite status in relation to other players. You can enable players to add new friends and to keep their friends list up-to-date by subscribing to friends status updates.

Documentation:

  • Friends Interface

Social Overlay

Display to a player their achievements and friends information, including if their friends are playing the same game.

Documentation:

  • Social Overlay
  • Friends Interface

Presence

Display a player's status (online, offline, playing) in a game. You can update and delete a player’s presence data. In combination with the friends feature, players can also view their friends' presence information.

Documentation:

  • Presence Interface

Stats

Define the stats for your game in the Developer Portal. You can include any statistical data about a player that you want to track, for example the number of items the player has collected. You can use these stats to determine when to unlock achievements and how to rank players in leaderboards.

Achievements

Access data about player achievements, unlock them for a player, get data on a player's progression for an achievement, and send notifications to players when they unlock an achievement.

Note: Epic Games Store achievements are built on Epic Online Services achievements. You can offer XP to players in games distributed on the Epic Games Store by using Epic Games Store achievements.

Trust and Safety

Build player trust by safeguarding your community and promoting a fair and safe experience for everyone. Easy Anti-Cheat (the Anti-Cheat Interfaces), player reports, and sanctions protect game integrity and help curb problem behavior. Kids Web Services (KWS) make it easier to navigate the complex global landscape of parental consent and verification, building confidence with parents and players alike.
