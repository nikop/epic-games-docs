Indie Developers — Let Epic Games Know About Your Game

Let Epic Games know about your indie game.

1 min to read

Epic Games regularly gives extra help and support to the most promising upcoming indie titles that use Unreal Engine, or any of the Epic Games tools and services. To apply for this help and support, let Epic Games know about your game.

More information: Epic for Indies Help and Support (dev.epicgames.com/community/epic-for-indies/help-and-support).

Use the following procedure to let Epic Games know about your game. After you've done this, you can use the same procedure to update the details that you've provided to Epic Games.

To let Epic Games know about your game, or to update the details you've provided to Epic Games:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal), and make sure you are in the correct organization for your product.

    (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation in the Developer Portal. To change organization, click Organization and follow the onscreen instructions.)

  2. If you have already set up a product for your game in the Developer Portal:

    • In the left navigation, under Your products, select the name of your product.

  3. If you haven't set up a product for your game in the Developer Portal yet:

    • a. In the left navigation, under Your products click Create Product.
    • b. Follow the on-screen instructions to set up your product.
    • c. After you create your product, it appears in the left navigation. Click the product.

  4. In the left navigation, click For Indie Games > Tell us more about your game.

  5. Click Next.

  6. Complete as many details about your game as you can.

    • You must accept the terms and conditions, and complete any fields marked with an asterisk ( * ).

  7. Click Share.

At any time in the future, you can repeat the above process to update the details that you've provided to Epic Games.
