Multiplayer

Make every match possible, however your players choose to connect. Turn your playerbase into a community by enabling multiplayer experiences across platforms. Give players the tools to form groups, set up matches, and communicate in real time with peer-to-peer, sessions, lobbies, and voice chat.

Manage multiplayer services in your game with the following EOS interfaces:

  • Peer-to-Peer (P2P) (also known as “NAT P2P”): Enable players to connect with other players in a game.
  • Lobby: Enable players to create, remove, join, and leave lobbies.
  • Sessions: Allow players to host, find, and interact with online gaming sessions.
  • Voice Interface and Real-Time Communication (RTC) Interface: Help players talk, strategize, and socialize. Voice chat enables you to create and manage cross-platform voice chat rooms between your players during matches or in lobbies. Real-time-communication (RTC) text chat is also available. To integrate voice or text chat, either use EOS Lobbies (through the Lobby Interface) or your own trusted server.

Multiplayer services are EOS Game Services — players can connect to your game with an account from any supported identity provider. For details, see Epic Online Services (EOS) Overview > Type of Service.
