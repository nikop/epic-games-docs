You

Details on what a player can do on the You tab of the Social Overlay.

2 mins to read

Note: The Social Overlay does not support the following platforms: macOS, Linux, Linux ARM64, Android, and iOS.
Players must sign in with their Epic Games account or a proxy account to use the Social Overlay.

This documentation assumes that you install the Social Overlay with your game and initialize the EOS SDK when a player starts your game. For more information on how to install and initialize the Social Overlay, see the SDK Integration documentation.

The Social Overlay in the Epic Games Launcher when the player selects the You tab.

Players can navigate to the You tab on the Social Overlay to see player-specific information and adjust their communication settings.

The You tab displays the following information:

  • The player's linked accounts.
  • A link to the player’s Epic Games profile.
  • Communication settings.
  • Blocked users.

Communication settings

The Communications Settings section of the Social Overlay in the Epic Games Launcher.

Note: The Text Chat, Text Reporting, Filter Mature Language, and Filter Personal Information settings under Communication settings are only available in the Epic Games Launcher, Epic Games Store and Epic Games mobile apps, and Fortnite.

Text chat

Players can change this setting to manage who they can text chat with.

The available options are:

  • Everybody: Players can use text chat with anyone.
  • Friends, team members and connections: Players can only use text chat to communicate with their Epic Games friends, platform friends (for example, Xbox friends), people in invite-only chats and everyone on their in-game team.
  • Friends only: Players can only use text chat to communicate with their friends.
  • Off: Players cannot use text chat to communicate with anyone.

Text reporting

Players can change this setting to manage their text reporting settings.

The available options are:

  • Always On: Text reporting is on in every text chat conversation
  • Off when possible: Text reporting is off in direct messages and chats where all players have disabled text reporting.

Note: Text reporting is on, regardless of settings, in chats where at least one player has text reporting set to Always on, or when a player is added to a group through Looking For Party, as well as in all in-game text channels. Text reporting is always on for players under the age of 18.

For more information about text reporting, see https://www.fortnite.com/voice-text-reporting-faqs

Friend requests

Players can change this setting to manage who can send them a friend request.

The available options are:

  • Everybody: Anyone with an Epic Games account can send friend requests to the player.
  • Friends of Friends: Only the player’s friends of friends can send friend requests to the player. For more information on how a friend is defined, see What is a Friend?
  • Nobody: Nobody can send a friend request to the player.

For more information about players sending and receiving friend requests on the Social Overlay, see Friends.

Filter mature language

Players can toggle this setting on to filter mature or offensive languages in text chat.

Filter personal information

Players can toggle this setting on to filter information that might allow someone to guess their or someone else's identity. For example, Email addresses, phone numbers, and account IDs.

Do not disturb

Players can toggle this setting on to disable all social notifications.

Note: If the player is under the age of digital consent and their account requires a Guardian PIN, then they have to enter the Guardian PIN to send or accept invitations. See the Epic Games Cabined Accounts documentation for more information.
