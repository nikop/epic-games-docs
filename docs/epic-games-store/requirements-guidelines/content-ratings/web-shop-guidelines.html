Web Shop Guidelines

Guidelines for web shops on the Epic Games Store.

4 mins to read

On the Epic Games Store, a product can launch one web shop. (See the following glossary entry for a full definition: web shop). In addition to the Epic Games Store requirements (Documentation: Requirements Overview.), web shops must follow specific guidelines and requirements on the Epic Games Store.

  • Your product must fulfill web shop items accurately and immediately. For example, if a user buys digital currency as a web shop item, the exact amount of currency is added to the user's account immediately after purchase.

  • You must create a base product offer and fill out the Product Home page with your system requirements. If your product is only available on mobile (iOS, Android) devices, we recommend setting the system requirements as "N/A". (Documentation: Get Started > Base Product Offer.)

  • One of the options below must be true for your base product offer:

    • Your base product offer is released and available for purchase on the Epic Games Store or on another third-party PC or mobile storefront, or
    • Your base product offer is not available on the Epic Games Store, but you provide a Product Home page with early store discovery enabled. (Documentation: Manage Pages > Publishing Statuses of Offer Pages.)

  • Your product and web shop cannot support blockchain, NFT, or cryptocurrency technology (known as blockchain technology).

  • You must maintain a valid webhook for your web shop. (Documentation: Manage Webhook.)

  • Your linking player ID instructions must be comprehensible and complete. You must display these instructions in the web shop. The player ID is required to link the purchase of a web shop item to the user’s player ID. To edit player ID instructions, you must do so from the web shop details panel. (Documentation: Manage Your Web Shop > Edit Web Shop Details.)

  • Your web shop must follow the Epic Games Store content guidelines. (Documentation: Content Guidelines.)

  • Your product must follow the Epic Games Store Prohibited Content Guidelines on every storefront it is available on. (Documentation: Content Guidelines > Prohibited Content.)

  • Different storefronts have different restrictions regarding the use of web shops in different regions of the world. Consoles might have different requirements and guidelines than PC (Windows, macOS). See external documentation:

  • Google:

    • Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement
    • Google Play Payment Policy
    • Understanding Google Play's Payments Policy

  • Apple:

    • Apple App Store Connect Terms of Service (sign in required)
    • Alternative Terms Addendum for Apps in the EU
    • App Review Guidelines

Web Shop Ratings

If your base product offer is available for purchase on the Epic Games Store, you can use your existing IARC certificate for your web shop.

If your base product offer is not available on the Epic Games Store and you have obtained ratings for your product from any official rating authority, you must display that information on your web shop page. You must first create a product before you create a web shop. As part of the onboarding experience, during the new product questionnaire, select No when you are asked to complete the IARC questionnaire.

The Epic Games Store does not require all products on the storefront to be rated by an official rating authority. Unrated products are treated as having the highest-level maturity rating available in regions where they are distributed. Some regions have legal requirements for ratings. You cannot distribute products in such regions without ratings from the appropriate authorities.

For more information, see the following documentation:

  • Ratings on the Epic Games Store
  • IARC Ratings

FAQ Page

If your base product offer is not available on the Epic Games Store, we strongly recommend that you create an FAQ page. An FAQ page provides additional information about your web shop. You can only create one FAQ page per web shop, but the page can contain multiple questions and answers. You are welcome to add additional questions or information to your FAQ page, as needed.

Required questions on your FAQ page:

  • How can I purchase the base product offer?
    • Note: The answer you provide must include a direct URL link and follow our Linked-to content guidelines. (Documentation: Content Guidelines > Linked-to content.)
  • Is the base product offer available everywhere, or are there regional restrictions?
  • What platform is the product available on: PC (Windows, macOS) or Mobile (iOS, Android)?

Consequences

Content that does not conform to the guidelines above might be subject to re-review or removal from the Epic Games Store by the Epic Games Store team. When the team reviews your content, we look at a variety of factors, including the severity of the issue and whether you’re a repeat offender. The Epic Games Store team sanction can be, for example, asking you to remove the content, a warning, or a permanent account ban.
