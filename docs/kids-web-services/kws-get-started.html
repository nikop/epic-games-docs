Get Started with Kids Web Services

How to use the Kids Web Services platform for the first time.

3 mins to read

To get started with Kids Web Services (KWS), there are four steps:

  • Create an account or sign in.
  • Set up your organization.
  • Choose your level and set up your product (optional step).
  • Set up Kids Web Services.

Step 1 — Sign In

To access the Developer Portal, go to the Developer Portal website at dev.epicgames.com/portal and follow the instructions to sign in. There are different ways to sign in:

  • Create a new Epic Games account.
  • Sign in with your existing Epic Games account.
  • Sign in with one of the third-party accounts listed.

Note: However you sign into the Developer Portal, this documentation refers to the account you signed in with as your “Epic Games account”.

Step 2 — Set Up Your Organization

An organization represents the group of people (usually a company or other business) responsible for your product.

After creating and signing into an Epic Games account, follow the on-screen instructions to create your first organization.

To create an additional organization, follow the steps below:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal at dev.epicgames.com/portal.

  2. In the left navigation, click the name of your organization (shown as "Your..." in the following image):

  1. In the Choose your organization panel, scroll to the bottom, click Create organization and follow the on-screen instructions.

For more information about organizations in the Developer Portal, see the following Epic Online Services (EOS) documentation: Your Organization and Products.

Step 3 — Choose to Set Up KWS for Your Organization or Product

KWS can be set up in the Developer Portal at the organization or product level. In the Developer Portal, a product is a game or other software application that uses one or more Epic tools or services, including KWS. An organization owns a product, which might have different versions that run on different platforms. You can create multiple products for your organization.

You can set up KWS on two levels:

  • Set up at the organization level if you want a consistent verification flow across all your products (games or software applications). For example, if all verification flows use your company’s branding assets.
  • Set up at the product level for verification flows tailored to a specific product (game or software application). For example, each verification flow can have its own branding and different verification methods.

It is not necessary to set up both unless you require multiple KWS implementations. If you choose to set up KWS at the organization level, you can always set up additional implementations at the product level at any time.

If you choose not to set up KWS at the product level, proceed to Step 4.

Set Up Your Product (Optional)

To create a new product in the Developer Portal dev.epicgames.com/portal, follow the steps below:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal at dev.epicgames.com/portal.

  2. Check that you are in the correct organization.

  3. In the left navigation, click Create Product and follow the on-screen instructions.

For more information about products in the Developer Portal, see the following EOS documentation: Your Organization and Products.

Step 4 — Set Up Kids Web Services

Before you use KWS, you must accept the service terms. The Developer Portal prompts you to accept the terms when you begin using KWS. For your reference, the terms are viewable on the Developer Terms Hub website at kidswebservices.com/en-US/developer-terms-hub.

The steps to set up KWS are as follows:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal at dev.epicgames.com/portal.

  2. In the left navigation, click Kids Web Services.

  3. On the Kids Web Services page, click Get started now in the Parent Verification panel.

  4. Read and follow the on-screen prompts to review and accept the service terms.
