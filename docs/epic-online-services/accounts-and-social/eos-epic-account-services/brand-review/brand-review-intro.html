Brand Review Introduction

Introduction to how to verify the brand associated with your game.

6 mins to read

Brand review enables your game (or software application) to register as an EOS Epic Account Services application. EOS Epic Account Services provides player accounts that enable your game's players to tap into the Epic Games account ecosystem of nearly a billion players across multiple platforms and stores and gain access to the Accounts and Social group of EOS services and the Epic Games Store Ecommerce (Ecom) service. The brand review process verifies your game's brand with Epic Games. Your brand is the company or real-world organization that is associated with your product.

Brand review protects you, all your players (users), and Epic Games from malicious activities, such as brand spoofing, phishing, malware, stolen identity, and copyright infringement.

The Accounts and Social group of EOS services includes functionality such as the Presence Interface, the Auth Interface for authentication, and the Friends Interface.

For more information on the Accounts and Social group of services and the Epic Games Store services that EOS provides, see the following documentation:

  • Accounts and Social
  • Epic Games Store Ecommerce (Ecom) service

About Brand Review

Until Epic Games has verified your brand, players can only use your game's integration of the Accounts and Social group of EOS services if they meet the following criteria:

  • They have an Epic Games account.
  • Their Epic Games account is a member of your organization.

When your brand is verified, Epic Games removes this restriction and any player with an Epic Games account (known as "an Epic Games user") can use your game's integration of the Accounts and Social group of EOS services.

Note on the terms “product” and “application”
In the Developer Portal during the brand review process, note the following:

  • “product” represents your game or software application. (For example, the game “Fall Guys”.)
  • “application” represents an implementation of your product for Epic Account Services. An application associated with your product has settings for brand, user permissions (consent), and linked clients. (For example, the settings for Fall Guys' brand, user permissions and linked clients in Epic Account Services is an application.)

This set of brand review pages use “product” to mean game or software application and "application" to mean a product's Epic Account Services settings.

What Brand Review Is Not

  • Epic Games does not review or certify your product. Epic Games only reviews the brand that you are associating with your product.
  • Approval of Brand Review does not provide you access to player data for any other product.
    • Documentation: About EOS Epic Account Services.

Your Brand's Visibility to Users

  • When you register your product as an Epic Account Services application, Epic Games users encounter your brand in association with your product. They encounter this before Epic Games asks them to give explicit permission regarding their access to your product.

Before Your Brand is Verified

Until Epic Games verifies a brand through brand review, a product registered as an Epic Account Services application has the following restrictions:

  • The product displays a warning to players as part of the consent dialog.
  • The product has audience restrictions: only members of your organization can access your product.

Audience Restrictions

A product with an unverified brand has specific audience restrictions. These restrictions are in place to protect Epic Games users, you (the developer), other product developers, and Epic Games from brand spoofing, phishing, and more.

Until Epic Games verifies your product's brand, only Epic Games users who are members of your product's organization can authenticate with their Epic Account Services associated player account and access your product. No other Epic Games users can access your product. They instead see the access restriction message shown below. After Epic Games reviews and verifies your brand, this restriction is removed.

Evaluation Criteria

When reviewing your brand details, the Epic Games moderation team applies a set of guidelines that might evolve over time to reflect changes to any relevant regulations, the features that Epic Games provides, or the terms of service.

The criteria the team focuses on include, but are not limited to, the following:

Domain Verification

You must provide a website address (known as a "domain" or "website domain") for your organization and verify your ownership of it. The website domain you provide must be where you host information about your product.

Documentation: Domain Verification

Your website domain must:

  • Be publicly accessible.
  • Include your organization name.
  • Include the name and an overview of your product. Note: If you are using Epic-managed distribution (Documentation: Epic-Managed Distribution > Is This For Me?) for your product and have a codename prior to launch, you must:
    • Notify the team by creating a private discussion on the support website (eoshelp.epicgames.com), or by contacting your Business Development manager.
    • Add your product name to your website domain when you launch the product.
  • Adhere to the Epic Games Store content guidelines and be suitable for general audiences. Your website domain must merit a rating of PEGI 12, ESRB Everyone 10+, or lower.
    • Documentation: Content Guidelines.

Privacy Policy

You must have a publicly accessible privacy policy that demonstrates intent to handle user-account information with respect, and in a manner that complies with applicable law and the Epic Games requirements.

Your privacy policy must:

  • Be available through a direct URL link.
  • Be publicly accessible.
  • Provide contact information for data protection inquiries.
  • Be descriptive in how user data is handled, including how and what personal information is collected and handled, and with whom the information is shared.
  • Be descriptive regarding user data retention, including how users can access and delete their information.
  • Not make claims about what Epic Games does and does not do: Epic Games has a privacy relationship with Epic Games users, communicated through the Epic Games privacy policy. (Documentation: www.epicgames.com/~/privacypolicy.)
  • Adhere to the Epic Games Store content guidelines. (Documentation: Content Guidelines.)

Brand Content

The content you use to brand your product must:

  • Not contain third-party intellectual property that you do not own or has not been adequately licensed to you.
  • Not attempt to misrepresent or deceive users about the product owner's identity.
  • Not contain or represent illicit or illegal material.
  • Be appropriate for all audiences.
  • Adhere to the Epic Games Store content guidelines:
    • It must be suitable for general audiences.
      This means that the content must merit a rating of PEGI 12, ESRB Everyone 10+, or lower.
    • Documentation: Content Guidelines.
