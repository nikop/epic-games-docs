Localize Your Storefront Assets (Images and Video)

Localize the images and video in your storefront content to best reach global Epic Games Store users.

4 mins to read

You can localize the images and videos that appear on your product home page and offer pages. This helps you reach a larger audience, because your store presence will present Epic Games Store users with images and videos in their preferred language.

For a list of the languages supported by the Epic Games Store, see documentation: Supported Languages.

Create Asset Localizations

To create your asset localizations, complete the following process:

  • Step 1: Set up your Assets in English
  • Step 2: Upload Your Localized Assets
  • Step 3: Publish Your Localized Assets

Step 1: Set up your Assets in English

Before you upload your assets, do the following:

  1. In the Dev sandbox, configure in English the pages, offers, and store settings that you want to localize.
    • For information, see the documentation on Manage Pages, Manage Offers, and Configure Store Settings.
  2. Make sure that these pages, offers and store settings have the status Ready for Stage.
    • To learn more about statuses, see documentation: Release Management.

Step 2: Upload Your Localized Assets

Follow the steps below to upload assets to a page, offer, or store settings for a specific locale. Repeat this process for each additional locale.

To upload assets for a specific locale:

  1. In the Dev Portal, navigate to Your Product > Epic Games Store > Localization.
  2. Ensure the Dev sandbox is selected. Then, click the name of a page, offer, or set of store settings to edit it.
  3. Choose the language you want to configure from the options on the right. If the desired language is not available, click Add New Language and select the appropriate option to add it to the list.
  4. In the Media Localization section, select a component to configure (e.g., Offer Images). Then, upload your localized assets as needed.
  • Tip: When you update your assets, click Preview in Store to open a preview of your storefront in a new tab. Use this preview to ensure that your localized assets display as desired on your product home page and offer pages. Note that this option might not be available for all pages, offers, or sets of store settings.
  1. Click Save Changes to finalize the upload.

After you upload and check your localized assets, continue to Step 3: Publish Your Localized Assets.

Notes:

  • Assets associated with a specific language have the Localized label, while assets copied from the original English configuration display the Default label. If you make any unintended edits, click Use Original Media to revert to the default assets for that section.
  • If you update an asset in English that has previously been localized, you are given the option to either Update All or Update Non-Localized Only. If you select Update Non-Localized Only, then all assets associated with a page, offer, or set of store settings for a given locale are no longer counted as Default and instead display as Localized.

Step 3: Publish Your Localized Assets

To publish your localized assets, you first must push the localized pages, offers, and sets of store settings to the Stage sandbox and submit them for review. After the Epic Games Store team approves them, you can publish your changes to the Live sandbox.

  1. Navigate to Your Product > Epic Games Store > Release Management.
  2. In the Dev sandbox, click Push to Stage.
  3. In the list, select the pages, offers, and sets of store settings that contain localized assets.
  4. Click Push to Stage. The pages, offers, and sets of store settings then display in the Stage sandbox section of the screen.
  5. In the Stage sandbox, click Submit for Review to submit your media to the Epic Games Store team.
  6. After your pages, offers, web shop items, and sets of store settings are approved, push your content to the Live sandbox. To do so:
    • a. In the Stage sandbox, click Push to Live.
    • b. In the list of entities (artifacts and binaries, store settings, offers, pages, and web shop items), select the entities that you want to publish.
      • At a minimum, you must push the following entities, if they are not already in your Live sandbox:
        • A base product offer
        • A set of store settings
        • A product home page
        • An artifact for the base product offer (you do not need to do this for a "coming soon" offer).
        • Any other entities that are mandatory for your product (for example, an FAQ page if your product is a PC (Windows, macOS) product in Early Access).
          • For information on mandatory entities, see documentation: Epic Games Store Requirements.
    • c. At the top right, click Push to Live.

After you have created one or more localizations, you can manage this information through the Dev Portal. For more information, see documentation: View Your Localized Content.

Note: During the review process, the Epic Games Store team might provide feedback that requires you to make changes to your English assets. In this scenario, you must also update any associated localized assets before you resubmit your pages, offers, and sets of store settings for review.

After you create localizations for your product, you can view them, remove specific locale support, and view a history of your template uploads.
