Discount Management Guide

Create and manage discount campaigns for your offers using promo campaigns.

26 mins to read

In the Developer Portal, you can create and manage discounts for your offers. This page describes how to create and manage those discounts. For guidance on offer types and how to configure them, see: Manage Offers.

Discount Overview

When you apply a discount to an offer, the Epic Games Store displays both the list price and the discount price using strikethrough text. This means that the store displays the discounted price alongside the regular price to highlight the comparison to users.

When you set up discounts, you must define them with two primary elements:

  • Duration: The length of time the discount is available on the storefront, as determined by its start and end dates/times.
  • Discount value: The percentage decrease applied to the offer's base price, such as 25% off.

In the Developer Portal, you can create three types of discount for your offers:

  • Custom discount: A discount with a customized campaign name and start and end dates provided by your organization.
  • Preset campaign: A discount that is associated with an Epic Games Store sale or an event that includes preset start and end dates (for example, the Epic Games MEGA Sale).
  • Limited-time free promotion: A 100% discount with a customized campaign name and start and end dates provided by your organization.

For custom discounts and preset campaign discounts, you can specify the percentage decrease between 5 and 95. For limited-time free promotions, you cannot customize the percentage decrease. The discount for limited-time free promotion offers is always 100.

Discount Guidelines

When you create a discount, the Discount Management Tool automatically reviews it. This ensures that your discount follows the discount guidelines before it appears on the store.

The following discount guidelines apply on a per-product basis for custom discounts, Epic Games preset campaigns, and limited-time free promotions.

  • You can apply only one discount to an offer in a specific timeframe. In other words, multiple discounts cannot be active for a single offer at the same time.
  • You cannot edit the list price or the discount rate if the offer currently has a discount.
    • You can change the list price after the discount ends. However, if less than 30 days have elapsed since the previous discount ended, you cannot set a new list price that is lower than the previous discounted price.
  • For custom discounts and preset campaigns, the discount rate must be a value between 5% and 95%.
  • For limited-time free promotion discounts, the discount rate is set to 100% and cannot be changed.
  • Post-release discounts cannot exceed 30 consecutive days.
  • You cannot discount an offer for more than 45 days total during any 90-day window.
  • You must allow a minimum 30-day cooldown period between discounts that you apply to a specific offer.
  • You cannot schedule discounts within the following time windows:
    • Within 30 days of a product's available date.
    • Within 30 days of the end of an introductory offer.
  • You can change the price after the discount ends. However, you cannot set a new price that is lower than the previous discounted price if less than 30 days have elapsed since the previous discount ended.
  • Products in a pre-purchase state can have discounts, but the discounts start together with pre-purchase and end when the product releases. You can schedule a continuation of your pre-purchase discount to convert it to an introductory offer. However, the following guidelines apply:
    • The discount must be the same discount value as the introductory offer.
    • You must configure the discount at the same time as you configure the pre-purchase discount. Notify the Epic Games Store team before the pre-purchase discount goes live. To do so, create a private discussion using this support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com.
  • You cannot schedule a discount for a date within 30 days of a price change. This means that you must schedule discounts for a date at least 31 days after a price change.

The following guidelines apply to any changes you make to region availability for offers.

  • You cannot remove an offer from availability in a region (block a region) while it has an active discount.
  • You can only remove an offer from availability in a region (block a region) or add an offer in a region (unblock a region) when the offer does not have an active discount.
    • Exception: You can unblock an offer during an active discount if you treat the active discount as an Introductory Offer. (See the section below on Pre-Purchase, Introductory, and Special Offers.)
  • You must wait 30 days before you can start a discount on your offer if you change the region availability of that offer.
    • Note: You must update any existing scheduled discount to start 30 days after you change the region availability of an offer.
    • Exception: An Introductory Offer does not have to wait 30 days to start. (See the section below on Pre-Purchase, Introductory, and Special Offers.)

Pre-Purchase, Introductory, and Special Offers

You can use the following offer types to apply a temporary discount to your product:

  • Pre-Purchase Offer
  • Introductory Offer
  • Special Offer

Note: The temporary discounts include “Offer” in their name, but they are a temporary discount and not an Epic Games Store offer. An offer is an item that Epic Games Store users can buy.

You can apply these temporary discounts based on the criteria below.

Pre-Purchase Offer

The following criteria apply to this offer:

  • It is only for products that are not yet released.
  • It must start when a product’s pre-purchase period starts (it cannot start any earlier than the start date and time of the pre-purchase period) and it must end when the product releases.
  • Its reduction amount must be between 5% and 40%.

Notes:

  • When you have set up your Pre-Purchase Offer, you must change its start and end date if you change your product’s available date.
  • An offer start date is known as an offer "available date on the Epic Games Store" (or "available date") in the Developer Portal.

Introductory Offer

The following criteria apply to this offer:

  • It is only for newly released products.
  • It must start at the exact same date and time as the product available date and time.
  • Its duration cannot exceed 14 consecutive days.
  • Its reduction amount must be between 5% and 40%.
  • You must submit an Introductory offer before your product's available date or before the start date of any Pre-Purchase Offer you have applied to your product.

For more information, see the following section on this page: Create Introductory Offer.

Notes:

  • When you have set up your Introductory Offer, you must change its start and end date if you change your product’s available date.
  • An offer start date is known as an offer "available date on the Epic Games Store" (or "available date") in the Developer Portal.

Special Offer

You can run a Pre-Purchase Offer into an Introductory Offer. This type of temporary discount is called a Special Offer. To do this, you must follow these requirements:

  • The Introductory Offer must have the same reduction amount as the Pre-Purchase Offer.
  • You must submit the Introductory Offer before the Pre-Purchase Offer starts.

Create a Custom Discount or Preset Campaign Discount

In the Developer Portal, you can create both custom discounts and preset campaign discounts for your offers.

  • Custom discounts allow you to create your own discount campaigns with customized start and end dates. These dates determine when a discount is active on the storefront.
  • Preset campaigns are associated with an Epic Games Store sale or event (for example, the Epic Games MEGA Sale) and include preset start and end dates. These dates cannot be modified.

To be eligible for a discount, an offer must follow these guidelines:

  • It must be in the Live sandbox.
  • It must not be a demo offer.
  • It must not have a base price of $0.
  • It must have a specific available date assigned. Discounts cannot be created for offers with an estimated or unknown available date.
  • It must have an artifact uploaded. You cannot create a discount for an offer without an uploaded artifact.

Note: Discounts that you apply to offers at the time of their release must meet specific criteria. To learn more, see the Create Introductory Offer section on this page.

To create a custom discount or a preset campaign discount, follow the steps below:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Discount Management Tool page. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. Select the correct organization for your product.
    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.
    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Promo Campaigns.
    • e. On the Promo Campaigns page, click Discount Management Tool.
  2. At the top right, click Create discount.
  3. Select Custom campaign or Pre-set campaigns by EGS.
  4. If you selected Pre-set campaigns by EGS:
    • a. Select the campaign you want to enroll in.
      • Campaigns that are currently running have a green dot next to them. If you cannot enroll in a running campaign (because the campaign ends in less than seven days), the campaign is disabled in the list, and you cannot select it.
      • To find campaigns with specific text in the campaign name, enter that text in the Search campaigns box.
    • b. Click Next.
  5. In the Discount info section, provide the appropriate details for your discount. Ensure the information adheres to our discount guidelines.
    • Custom Campaigns: Provide the discount name, start date/time, end date/time, and discount value.
      • Note: To easily identify discounts, we recommend you use a naming convention that clearly conveys the timeframe and content of the discount campaign.
        Examples: "[Publisher Sale] Epic Games - Spring ‘25" or "[Franchise Sale] Rocket League - Summer ‘25".
    • Preset campaigns by EGS: Provide the discount value. All other details are provided by Epic Games and cannot be modified.
  6. In the Offers section, select every offer you want to include in the discount. You can then set the discount value individually per offer or apply the same discount to all selected offers.
    • Individual discounts: In the Discount % column, enter the desired decrease for each selected offer.
    • Bulk discount: You can apply a discount amount to the selected offers. At the bottom of the table, in Discount %, enter the discount amount, then click Apply.
    • Note: The Offers section only contains offers in your Live sandbox. It does not include any demo-type offers or offers with a base price of $0.
  7. Review the details of your discount to ensure accuracy.
  8. Click Submit.
    • Your discount automatically undergoes validation to ensure that it adheres to our guidelines (see section on this page: Discount Guidelines). If the discount review process is successful, it is approved and scheduled by the Discount Management Tool. For more information, see Submit Discounts for Validation on this page.
    • If the discount does not adhere to our discount guidelines, a validation error displays at the top of the screen. The Offers section shows error details for each affected offer. Update your discount as needed to resolve the issues, then click Submit again.

When the discount is created, it displays in the table on the Discounts page. Its status is as follows:

  • If the discount review process is successful, its status is Published.
  • If the discount review process is unsuccessful, its status is Issues Found.

On the discount start date, the discount goes live on the storefront, and its status is updated to Active.

You can edit or delete a discount as needed before the discount's start date.

For more information, see the Manage Discounts section on this page.

Create an Introductory Offer

An introductory offer is a discount applied to an offer at the time of the product's release, such as a release day sale. The offer must revert to full price no later than 14 days after release.

The discount created for an introductory offer must meet the following criteria:

  • The start date and time of the discount must exactly match the offer's available date and time.
  • The discount rate must be between 5% and 40%.
  • The duration cannot be more than 14 days.

The offer receiving the discount must have a specific available date assigned that matches its effective date (for example, the date the offer becomes purchasable on the storefront). Make a note of this date and time because you need this information to create your introductory offer.

To create an introductory offer, you need to access three different pages in the Developer Portal:

  • The Offers page
  • The Release Management page
  • The Discount Management Tool page (part of the Promo Campaigns page)

To create an introductory offer, follow these steps:

Step 1: The Offers Page

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Discount Management Tool page. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. Select the correct organization for your product.
    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.
    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Offers.
  2. Select the appropriate sandbox.
  3. Create a new offer or update an existing offer.
  4. Set the offer's available date to the date and time of your product release. Take note of this date and time because you need this information to create your discount.

Step 2: The Release Management Page

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Release Management page. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. Select the correct organization for your product.
    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.
    • b. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Release Management.
  2. Promote your offer to the Live sandbox. You must complete this step before you create your discount.
    • Note: The offer will not be visible to users on the storefront until its viewable date or available date occurs.

Step 3: The Discount Management Tool Page

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Discount Management Tool page. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. Select the correct organization for your product.
    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.
    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Promo Campaigns.
    • e. On the Promo Campaigns page, click Discount Management Tool.
  2. Create a new custom discount as follows:
    • Set the Start Date and Time to the available date and time of your offer.
    • Set the End Date and Time to no later than 14 days after the start date and time.
    • Set the Discount Rate to a value between 5% and 40%.
    • In the Offers section, select the offer from the steps above in Step 1: The Offers Page.
  3. Carefully review the details of your discount.
  4. Click Submit.
    • If any validation errors occur, you must resolve those issues before you can submit the discount again. To learn more, see the Discount Guidelines section on this page.

Create a Limited-Time Free Promotion

A limited-time free promotion is a 100% discount applied to an offer for a set time period. Limited-time free promotional offers follow the same requirements as custom discounts or preset campaign discounts. However, you cannot adjust the discount rate for a limited-time free promotion.

For more information on discount requirements, schedule limitations, and guidelines, see the Discount Guidelines section on this page.

To be eligible for a limited-time free promotion, an offer must follow these guidelines:

  • It must be in the Live sandbox.
  • It must not be a demo offer.
  • It must not have a base price of $0.
  • It must have a specific available date assigned. Discounts cannot be created for offers with an estimated or unknown available date.
  • It must have an artifact uploaded. You cannot create a discount for an offer without an uploaded artifact.

To create a limited-time free promotion, follow the steps below:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Discount Management Tool page. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. Select the correct organization for your product.
    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.
    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Promo Campaigns.
    • e. On the Promo Campaigns page, click Discount Management Tool.
  2. At the top right, click Create discount.
  3. In the Select Discount Type menu, choose Limited-time free promotion.
  4. In the Limited-time free promotion menu, provide the appropriate details in the Discount info section. You must provide the following details:
    • Discount name.
    • Start date and time.
    • End date and time.
  5. In the Offers section, select each offer to include in the discount. Note: You cannot set the discount rate for limited-time free promotions. Note: The Offers section only contains offers in your Live sandbox. It does not include any demo-type offers or offers with a base price of $0.
  6. The table shows the following fields:
    • Available date: the start date of the offer.
    • Offer label: the current offer state. The states can be any of the following:
    • Released: An offer (that is not a Pre-Purchase Offer) is live on the Epic Games Store.
    • Coming soon: An offer (that is not a Pre-Purchase Offer) is set up with a specified start date.
    • Pre-purchase: A Pre-Purchase Offer is live on the Epic Games Store. Users can pre-purchase this product before it releases.
    • Coming soon & Pre-purchase: A Pre-Purchase Offer is set up with a specified start date.
  7. Review the details of your discount to ensure accuracy.
  8. Click Submit. Your discount automatically undergoes validation to ensure that it adheres to our guidelines (see the Discount Guidelines section on this page.).
    • If the discount review process is successful, it is approved and scheduled by the Discount Management Tool. For more information, see the Submit Discounts for Validation section on this page.
    • If the discount does not adhere to our discount guidelines, a validation error displays at the top of the screen. The Offers section shows error details for each affected offer. Update your discount as needed to resolve the issues, then click Submit again.

When the discount is created, it displays in the table on the Discounts page. Its status is as follows:

  • If the discount review process is successful, its status is Published.
  • If the discount review process is unsuccessful, its status is Issues Found.

On the discount start date, the discount goes live on the storefront, and its status is updated to Active.

You can edit or delete a discount as needed before the discount's start date.

For more information, see the Manage Discounts section on this page.

Discount Validation

When you submit a discount, it automatically goes through validation to ensure that it adheres to the Epic Games Store discount guidelines. For more information, see the Discount Guidelines section on this page.

Discounts must pass validation and review before they can become active on the storefront.

You can monitor the progress of validation and approval on the Discount Management Tool page of the Developer Portal. The Status column indicates its current position in the workflow, as follows:

  • Published: The discount passed validation.
  • Validation failed: The discount did not pass validation. You must take additional action before the discount is sent for review. For more information, see the Validation Failed section on this page.

For more information on statuses, see the Discount Statuses section on this page.

Validation Failed

If your discount has the Validation failed status, follow the steps below to review the validation feedback:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Discount Management Tool page. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. Select the correct organization for your product.
    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.
    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Promo Campaigns.
    • e. On the Promo Campaigns page, click Discount Management Tool.
  2. Click the discount with the Validation failed status.
    • Note: You can filter the list of offers to make it easier to find offers that have failed validation. To do this, at the top left click Show: All, then select Offers with issues.
  3. View the details of the validation failures and update your discount in accordance with the feedback provided.
  4. For preset campaigns, check your offers' date eligibility as follows:
    • For each offer in the list, the Date eligibility column shows whether the offer is allowed to enter the campaign based on the preset campaign's dates, as follows:
      • If an offer is eligible to enter the preset campaign, the date it will enter the campaign is shown next to a green check. (For more information, see Date Eligibility Statuses on this page.) You do not need to adjust this offer to enroll it in the campaign.
      • If an offer is eligible to enter the campaign, but it cannot join the campaign at the start, then you need to adjust the date. The earliest possible date it can enter the campaign is shown in yellow. (For more information, see Date Eligibility Statuses on this page.)
        • This situation happens if date restrictions prevent the offer joining the campaign at the start. For example, the offer might have been discounted within 30 days of the start of the campaign.
        • In this situation, you can enter the offer into the campaign on the date shown in yellow. To do this:
          • a. To set the offer's enrollment date to the date shown in yellow, in the Apply date dropdown, select Apply date to this offer .
            • You can also apply the date to all offers in the list. This is useful if you want all your offers to enter the campaign at the same time as each other. To do this, in the Apply Date dropdown, select Apply date to all offers.
          • b. To change a date back to its initial value when you opened this page, select Apply Date > Revert to default date.
            • You can also change all of the dates in the list back to their initial values when you opened the page. To do this, select Apply Date > Revert all dates.
      • If you cannot enter an offer into the campaign because of date restrictions, the Date eligibility column shows Not eligible. (For more information, see Date Eligibility Statuses on this page.)
        • This situation happens if date restrictions prevent entry into the campaign. For example, a 30-day timeout between discounts might apply.
        • To view details, hold your mouse pointer over the ? icon.
        • If you want to enter the campaign without this ineligible offer, then you can de-select this ineligible offer and select the other offers.
  5. When you have resolved all validation failures, you can resubmit the discount. To do this, at the bottom right, click Resubmit.

After you click Resubmit, the discount is validated again. If successful, the discount is published to use on the storefront.

Upload Bulk Discounts

You can create discounts in bulk by filling out and uploading a .csv template provided by Epic Games. With this template, you can create multiple discounts across multiple products with a single file upload.

Note: You cannot create custom and preset campaign discounts in a single upload. You must complete and upload a separate template for each of these discount types.

To upload bulk discounts, you must do the following:

  • Step 1. Download the template.
  • Step 2. Complete the template.
  • Step 3. Upload your completed template.

Step 1. Download the Template

To complete the bulk discount template, follow the steps below:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Discount Management Tool page. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. Select the correct organization for your product.
    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.
    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Promo Campaigns.
    • e. On the Promo Campaigns page, click Discount Management Tool.
  2. Click Upload Bulk Discount.
  3. On the Upload Bulk Discount page, select the type of discounts you want to create (for example, custom discounts or an available preset campaign).
  4. Click Download .CSV Template.
    • Note: To ensure a successful file upload, do not change the template’s structure (for example, do not rename or add columns.)

Step 2. Complete the Template

Open the template. Some of the columns are pre-filled with your data as follows:

  • Product ID: A unique identifier for each product.
  • Offer ID: A unique identifier for each offer.
  • Offer name: (Optional). Use this field to label your offers so that you can identify them easily.
  • Current price: The current base price applied to the offer.

In the empty columns, enter the details as follows:

  • For all discount types, provide the following information:
    • Percentage: Integer value in the range of 1-100. Do not include the “%” symbol.
  • For custom discounts only, provide the following details:
    • Discount Start Date: Enter a date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
    • Discount Start Time: Enter a time in the format HH:MM. Use a 24h format, UTC time zone.
    • Discount End Date: Enter a date in the YYYY-MM-DD date format.
    • Discount End Time: Enter a time in the format HH:MM. Use a 24h format, UTC time zone.
    • Discount Name: You can enter any text, except commas.

Step 3. Upload Your Completed Template

When you have completed the bulk discount template, upload the file in the Developer Portal. After confirming the information is correct, submit your discounts to the Epic Games Store.

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Discount Management Tool page. To do this:

    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. Select the correct organization for your product.
    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.
    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Promo Campaigns.
    • e. On the Promo Campaigns page, click Discount Management Tool.

  2. Click Upload Bulk Discount.

  3. In the Upload Bulk Discount panel, select the type of discounts you want to create (for example, custom discounts or an available preset campaign).

  4. Drag your completed .csv into the Drag File(s) here or Browse box or click Browse and select the .csv file from your local file system.

  5. Click Next.

  6. The Confirm Bulk Discount page displays an overview of your uploaded discounts. Review these discounts to ensure the information provided is accurate.

    • Note: To update any discounts before submission, complete the appropriate changes in the .csv file. Then, click Clear & Re-Upload File in the Discount Request(s) section and upload the new template.

  7. Click Submit.

    • The submission process might take some time, depending on the number of discounts you create. Do not refresh or close the page until this process is complete.

After you click Submit, you are taken to the Discount Management Tool page while your discounts undergo validation. Validation helps expedite the approval process by ensuring discounts adhere to our guidelines. See Discount Guidelines on this page for details.

The table displays the discounts you created with bulk upload. Each discount has one of the following statuses:

  • Published: Your discount was successfully published on the storefront with the discount start date. You do not need to take action at this time.

  • Validation failed: The validation process found one or more issues with the discount. You must resolve these issues and resubmit the discount. For more information, see the Submit Discounts for Validation and Review section on this page.

    • Note: You cannot resolve issues with the bulk upload process. You must update and resubmit each discount with issues separately. If you created discounts across multiple products with bulk upload, you must access the Discount Management Tool page for each product separately to review the feedback.

  • Error: An error occurred during the submission process. Access the View Discount page to review the error details.

Troubleshoot Bulk Discounts

If your Bulk Upload .CSV export does not look as expected, or you get errors when you try to import the .CSV file into the Discount Management Tool, you might have a problem with regional comma-separator characters.

To fix this issue:

  1. Change your device’s regional setting from using semicolon (;) to using comma (,).
    • For information, see the documentation for your device's operating system.
  2. Download the .CSV again.
  3. Open the .CSV file. It should look like an organized table. If you open it in a spreadsheet application, such as Microsoft Excel, then you should see the content laid out in separate columns.
  4. Update the .CSV file according to your discounting plan.
    • For guidance, see documentation Manage Discounts–Upload Bulk Discounts on this page.
  5. Save the .CSV file.
  6. Upload the .CSV into the Discount Management Tool.
  7. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete your submission.

Note: For guidelines on how to format your .CSV file, see .CSV file recommendations on this page.

.CSV file recommendations

  • We recommend that you use Excel to edit bulk discount .CSV files.
  • In the .CSV, use the following date and time formats:
    • For start and end times, use the 24-hour clock format (00:00 - 24:00).
    • For dates, use the structure YYYY-MM-DD.
      • If you don't see this format when you open the .CSV, change the format of the date columns to YYYY-MM-DD.

Manage Discounts

Use the Discount Management Tool page in the Developer Portal to review your upcoming discounts, monitor their statuses, and edit and delete discounts.

To go to the Discount Management Tool page:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. Select the correct organization for your product.
  3. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.
  4. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Promo Campaigns.
  5. Select Discount Management Tool.

Discount Statuses

The statuses given to discounts show their current position in the process. Possible statuses are as follows:

  • Draft: The discount has been created but not yet submitted. For example, you selected Save as Draft when you created the discount.

  • Validation failed: When you submitted the discount, the validation process found one or more issues. You can review specific issues on the View Discount page.

    • For more information, see the Discount Guidelines section on this page.

  • Published: The discount is ready to use on the storefront as scheduled. When the discount is live, its status is Active.

  • Active: The discount is currently live on the storefront.

  • Canceled: The discount was published but canceled before its start date. Canceled discounts do not become active on the storefront.

  • Expired: The end date of the discount has passed, and it is no longer active on the storefront.

  • Error: A failure occurred during the discount submission or cancellation process. You can review specific issues on the View Discount page.

  • Created by Epic: This discount was created by the Epic Games Store team. You cannot perform any actions on this discount.

    • If you want to make any changes, contact the Epic Games Store team. To do so, create a private discussion using this support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com.

  • Created in SPT This is a discount that you created by you in the previous version of the SPT Discounts interface. You cannot perform any actions on this discount.

Discount Management Options

Several actions are available to help you manage your discounts.

You can click a discount to open the Edit Discount page, where you can view and edit the discount. This page displays the following:

  • Specific details about the discount (such as its included offers).
  • Any validation issues or feedback from the Epic Games Store team (if the discount review process applies to your organization).
  • Note: You cannot edit discounts with the statuses In review, Active, Canceled, Expired, Error, Created by Epic, or Created in SPT.

You can also click the three dots (...) for a discount, then, select one of the options below:

  • Delete: Removes the discount from your product. You cannot delete a discount with the status published, canceled, or active.
  • Cancel: Prevents the discount from becoming active on the storefront on its start date.You can select this action only on discounts with the published status.
  • Clone: Use this action when you want to create a new discount based on this one. The Developer Portal copies the details from the discount into a new discount creation form. You can then provide additional information and submit the new discount.
    • Note: Start and end dates are not copied when you take this action.

Review Your Product's Discount Schedule

There is a discount calendar in your product's Epic Games Store page in the Developer Portal. The calendar is near the bottom of the page in the Promo Campaigns section. The calendar provides an overview of your upcoming discounts so you can quickly review your product’s discount schedule.

To access your product's discount calendar:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. Select the correct organization for your product.
  3. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.
  4. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store.
  5. Scroll down the page to the Promo Campaigns section.

Download Image Templates for Current Sales (PSD Templates)

Epic Games provides image templates you can use for current Epic Games Store sales. The templates are in .psd file format.

See the Image Templates for Current Sales (PSD Templates) page to download the image templates available for current sales.
