Mobile Early Adopter — Get Started Steps

Follow these steps to set up and publish your product on the Epic Games Store.

MOBILE EARLY ADOPTER
Before You Begin

Familiarize yourself with the requirements and guidelines for distributing products on the storefront.
1. Onboarding

Get set up on the Epic Games Store.
2. Publish Your Product Home Page

Set up your product and publish a page about it on the Epic Games Store.
3. Finalize and Test Configurations

Make sure your product is ready for launch on the Epic Games Store.
4. Submit for Final Review and Launch

Submit your product for review by the Epic Games Store team and launch your mobile (iOS, Android) product.
5. Release Your PC Product

Release your PC product on the Epic Games Store.
