Manage Regions and Ratings

Upload product ratings and determine your regions for distribution.

13 mins to read

To publish your product on the Epic Games Store, you must:

  • Obtain the required age ratings for all of the regions you want your product to be distributed in.
  • Declare all of the regions you want your product to be distributed in.

To do this, use the Regions and Ratings section of the Store Settings page in the Developer Portal.

Regions and Ratings is one of two sets of configuration you must complete to publish your product on the Epic Games Store. The other is product configuration.

For more information on product configuration and the Store Settings page, see the documentation: Configure Store Settings.

You can perform the following actions in Regions and Ratings:

  • Set up age ratings for a new product.
  • Opt out of IARC ratings.
  • Add an existing IARC rating to a product.
  • Obtain a new IARC rating.
  • Retake the IARC questionnaire.
  • Access your IARC certificate ID.
  • Manage traditional ratings for your product (for example, ESRB or PEGI).
  • Configure distribution regions for your product.

Page Navigation

To configure regions and ratings, go to the Regions and Ratings page in the Developer Portal. To do this:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. Select the correct organization for your product.
  3. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.
  4. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Store Settings.
  5. On the Store Settings page, click Regions and ratings.

Configure Age Ratings

You must configure your product's age ratings before you can select regions for distribution. Different regions have different rating requirements. For example, some regions require ratings from region-specific rating authorities (IARC, GRAC, or ESRB), while other regions do not require age ratings at all for distribution.

For more information on age ratings and region-specific requirements, see Ratings on the Epic Games Store.

If you haven't configured age ratings for your product yet, take the product ratings setup guide to obtain the proper ratings for your product. See the Set Up Age Ratings for a New Product section of this page.

Set Up Age Ratings for a New Product

To manage regions and ratings for a new product, complete the product ratings setup guide. The setup guide is a series of questions that direct you to the appropriate section of the Developer Portal based on your current IARC rating status and whether you would like to add, opt out of, or obtain an IARC rating.

To complete the setup guide:

  1. Go to the Regions and ratings page for your product. Follow the steps under the Page Navigation section.
  2. Click Ratings. If you have not yet added ratings or declared regions for your product, the setup guide displays.
  3. Answer the first question: “Do you already have an IARC Certificate ID or Global Rating ID (GRID) for this product?”
    • If you select Yes, you are prompted to add an existing IARC rating to your product. See the Add an Existing IARC Rating section of this page.
    • If you select No, proceed to the next question.
  4. Answer the second question: “Would you like to get a set of IARC ratings for this product by completing the questionnaire?”
    • If you select Yes, you are prompted to complete the IARC questionnaire. See the Obtain a New IARC Rating section of this page.
      We strongly recommend that you take the IARC questionnaire and receive an IARC rating. IARC ratings allow you to distribute your product in most regions. See IARC Ratings for more information.
    • If you select No, you choose to opt out of receiving an IARC rating for your product and your product is classified as “unrated”. If you opt out of receiving an IARC rating, you can opt in at any time. See the Opt In to IARC Ratings section of this page for more information.
      Leaving your product unrated limits the regions you can distribute your product in. See the Unrated Products section of this page.

After you add an existing IARC rating, obtain a new IARC rating, or opt out of receiving an IARC rating, you have completed the product ratings setup guide. You can now take the following actions using the Regions and Ratings page:

  • Take or retake the IARC questionnaire to receive a new IARC rating.
  • Access your IARC certificate ID.
  • Manage traditional ratings.
  • Configure distribution regions.
  • Opt in to IARC ratings (if you originally opted out).

Unrated Products

If you answer “No” to both questions of the product ratings setup guide, you choose to opt out of receiving an IARC rating for your product and your product is classified as “unrated”. Unrated products are more difficult for users to access in the Epic Games Store.

For more information on unrated products and their restrictions on the Epic Games Store, see the documentation: Ratings on the Epic Games Store > Unrated Products.

To remove the unrated status from your product, take one of the following actions:

  • Opt in to IARC ratings. Once you opt in to IARC ratings, you can either upload an existing IARC rating or take the IARC questionnaire to receive a new rating.
    • See the Opt In to IARC Ratings section of this page for more information.
  • Upload ratings from traditional rating authorities, such as ESRB or PEGI.
    • See the Manage Traditional Ratings section of this page for more information.

Opt In to IARC Ratings

If you chose to opt out of receiving an IARC rating in the product ratings setup guide, you can opt in to IARC ratings at any time. To do this:

  1. Go to the Regions and Ratings section of the Store Settings page. Follow the steps under the Page Navigation section.
  2. In the Regions and Ratings section, click Edit.
  3. In the Regions and Ratings pane on the right side of the page, click Ratings. The Product Ratings page displays.
  4. Click Opt in using IARC in the upper-right corner of the Product Ratings page.
  5. Select one of the following options:
    • Click Add IARC certificate ID / global rating ID to upload an existing IARC rating. See the Add an Existing IARC Rating section of this page for more information.
    • Click Start IARC Questionnaire to obtain a new rating using the IARC questionnaire. See the Obtain a New IARC Rating section of this page for more information.

Add an Existing IARC Rating

If you have an existing IARC rating for your product, you must upload your IARC rating to the Developer Portal in order to distribute your product on the Epic Games Store. To ensure a successful release, we recommend uploading your existing IARC rating before submitting your product for review.

Complete the following steps to add an existing IARC rating to your product:

  1. Go to the Regions and Ratings page. Follow the steps under the Page Navigation section.
  2. Go to the IARC certification upload form.
    • New products: The form displays if you select Yes to the first question of the product ratings setup guide. See the Set Up Age Ratings for a New Product section of this page.
    • Unrated products: Opt in to IARC ratings, then click Add IARC certificate ID / global rating ID. See the Opt In to IARC Ratings section of this page.
    • Replace a rating received from the IARC questionnaire: If you already completed the IARC questionnaire but want to upload an existing certificate, click Ratings, then click Manage IARC Certificate.
  3. Enter your IARC certificate ID or global rating ID in IARC Certificate ID / Global Rating ID.
    • Note: If you already have an IARC rating and want to replace your certificate ID with a different one, click the Edit icon, then enter your replacement ID.
  4. Enter your email in Contact email.
  5. Click Next.
  6. The regional ratings assigned to your product according to IARC guidelines display. Review this information for accuracy, then click Confirm to upload your IARC ratings to your product.

Obtain a New IARC Rating

If you do not currently have an IARC rating for your product, you can receive one by completing the IARC questionnaire. This step is recommended to promote an optimal user experience.

When you take the IARC questionnaire, the following conditions apply:


  • If you start the IARC questionnaire, you must complete it.
  • You have three hours to complete the IARC questionnaire from the time you start it. If you take longer than three hours, then you must start the questionnaire again.
  • You cannot opt out of IARC ratings after you start the questionnaire.
  • If your product is already live on the storefront, then you have 24 hours to complete the questionnaire after you start it. If you do not complete the questionnaire in 24 hours or less, then your product is suspended from being purchasable on the Epic Games Store.
  • You cannot push your product to the Stage sandbox until you complete the IARC questionnaire and receive your rating.

To take the IARC questionnaire and obtain an IARC rating for your product, complete the following steps:

  1. Go to the Regions and Ratings page: Follow the steps under the Page Navigation section.
  2. Open the IARC questionnaire pop-up.
    • New products: The pop-up displays if you select Yes to the “Would you like to get a set of IARC ratings for this product by completing the questionnaire?” question in the product ratings setup guide. See the Set Up Age Ratings for a New Product section of this page.
    • Unrated products: Opt in to IARC ratings, then click Start IARC questionnaire. See the Opt In to IARC Ratings section of this page.
  3. In the pop-up, enter your email in Contact email.
  4. Click Yes, Continue. The IARC questionnaire opens.
    • Note: The IARC questionnaire is hosted on the official IARC site and is external to the Developer Portal.
  5. Fill out the IARC questionnaire on the external IARC site. The questionnaire asks about the content of your product (such as the presence of violence or profanity) to help determine the appropriate ratings.
  6. After you complete the questionnaire, the regional ratings assigned to your product display. Review the assigned ratings and confirm they are accurate.
    • Note: If you believe an assigned rating does not accurately represent your product, click Back to return to the questionnaire and update your answers.
  7. Click the confirmation checkbox to agree to the IARC terms of use.
  8. Click Finished to complete the IARC questionnaire and return to the Developer Portal.

When you return to the Developer Portal, your IARC rating is automatically uploaded, and the Product Ratings page displays with your new rating. You can now take the following actions from the Product Ratings page:

  • Access your IARC certificate ID.
    • See the Access IARC Certificate ID for Ratings Portability section of this page.
  • Retake the IARC questionnaire.
    • See the Retake the IARC Questionnaire section of this page.
  • Go to the Regions page to configure distribution regions for your product.
    • See the Configure Regions section of this page.

Automatic IARC Rating Updates

When you obtain an IARC rating for your product, your product is assigned a set of regional ratings from a variety of rating authorities as per IARC guidelines. At times, you may notice ratings from a specific authority change automatically without any action from you.

Such updates can occur due to the following:

  • The rating authority amended the rating determination made by the automated IARC system.
  • A traditional rating was provided for your product that supersedes the determination made by the automated IARC system.

If one of your assigned ratings has changed, you will receive an email notification directly from IARC. No action is required from you to facilitate these updates.

Retake the IARC Questionnaire

You must retake the IARC questionnaire if any of the following apply to you:

  • If you feel you've made a mistake. For example, if you entered incorrect product information, you should retake the questionnaire.
  • If you've made changes to your build that would require a ratings change.
  • If you've created two products for the same game, you must take the IARC questionnaire once for each product. Each product with a unique product ID needs its own IARC certification.

To retake the IARC questionnaire, follow the steps below:

  1. Go to the Regions and Ratings section of the Store Settings page. Follow the steps under the Page Navigation section.
  2. In the Regions and Ratings section, click Edit.
  3. In the Regions and Ratings pane on the right side of the page, click Ratings. The Product Ratings page displays.
  4. In the Product Ratings page, click Manage IARC Certificate in the upper-right corner.
  5. Click Retake IARC questionnaire.
    • Warning: After you click Retake IARC questionnaire, you cannot push any changes to your product until you complete the IARC questionnaire.
  6. Complete the IARC questionnaire. See the Obtain a New IARC Rating section of this page for more details.

Access IARC Certificate ID for Ratings Portability

If you already have an IARC rating for your product, you can reuse your IARC rating when adding your product to other storefronts. To do this, you need your IARC Certificate ID or Global Ratings ID.

To retrieve your ID, follow the steps below:

  1. Go to the Regions and Ratings section of the Store Settings page. Follow the steps under the Page Navigation section.
  2. In the Regions and Ratings section, click Edit, then select Ratings.
  3. In the Product Ratings page, click Manage IARC certificate. On the Manage IARC Certificate page, your ID displays in IARC Certificate ID / Global Rating ID.
  4. Click the Copy icon to copy your ID to your clipboard.
  5. In the other storefront or location where you want to enter your rating, paste your ID into the applicable field.

Manage Traditional Ratings

In the Regions and Ratings section of the Store Settings page, you can upload product ratings obtained from traditional rating authorities, such as ESRB or PEGI. If your product has received any traditional ratings, you must upload this information regardless of whether you choose to leverage IARC ratings.

If your product has a traditional rating pending, you cannot distribute your product on the Epic Games Store until you receive and upload that rating.

To upload traditional ratings, follow the steps below:

  1. Go to the Regions and Ratings section of the Store Settings page. Follow the steps under the Page Navigation section.
  2. In the Regions and Ratings section, click Edit. Then, select Ratings.
  3. Select the rating you want to upload from the list of supported authorities.
    • Note: If needed, you can replace a specific rating you received through the IARC system with a traditional rating. To do this, select the authority from the list and click Replace with Traditional Rating.
  4. Complete the form to provide details about your product's rating. Depending on the authority you selected, you might need to take the following actions to complete the form:
    • Upload the rating license or certificate issued by the rating agency in PDF format.
    • Select the appropriate rating category.
    • Specify the content descriptors and interactive elements that influence the rating category (for example, violence, language, or in-game purchases).
      • Documentation: Ratings on the Epic Games Store > Content Descriptors and Interactive Elements
  5. Review the information provided for the rating authority, then click Save Changes.
  6. Repeat steps 3-5 as needed for each traditional rating available for your product.

Configure Regions

In addition to uploading rating information, you can also declare the regions in which you plan to distribute your product through the Store Settings section of the Dev Portal.

See the Configure Age Ratings section of this page to add ratings to your product.

Certain regions (such as South Korea, Russia, Brazil, Germany, or Australia) require products to have IARC or other region-specific ratings.

In order to distribute your product to these regions, you must first upload the appropriate ratings to your product. If you have not yet added ratings for a region with specific rating requirements, you will not be able to select those regions in the steps below.

Documentation: Ratings on the Epic Games Store > Ratings Requirements

To declare your product's distribution regions, follow the steps below:

  1. Go to the Regions and Ratings section of the Store Settings page. Follow the steps under the Page Navigation section.
  2. In the Regions and Ratings section, click Edit. Then, click Regions.
  3. The Regions selection list displays to the right. By default, all regions without rating requirements are selected. Update the selections in this list as appropriate for your product.
    • Note: If you have uploaded any ratings for this product, you can click Select all applicable regions to select or deselect all the regions that require the ratings you have provided.
  4. After you select all the distribution regions for your product, click Save Changes.

For the full list of distribution regions, see the following documentation: Create Custom Price Tiers > Epic Games Store Region List.
