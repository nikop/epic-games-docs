Retire a Product

Notify Epic Games when you no longer distribute an Unreal Engine product.

1 min to read

This page applies if your product (game or other software application) uses Unreal Engine.

If you no longer distribute your product, you can retire the product. This means that you no longer need to report revenue for the product after the end of the current calendar quarter. To do so, see Retire a Product.

If you no longer distribute your product, you can retire the product in the Developer Portal. This means that if you normally report revenue for the product to Epic Games, you will no longer need to do so after the current calendar quarter. Note: You still need to report any revenue for sales up to the last day of the current calendar quarter.

After you retire a product, if you subsequently start to receive revenue for that product, you must unretire the product. To do so, see Unretire a Product on this page.

To retire a product:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. Select your organization.
  3. In the left navigation, in the Epic Tools section click Unreal Engine > Royalties.
    • Note: You can only access the Royalties page if you have one of the following user roles for your Developer Portal organization: Owner, Admin, or Financial, or any custom role with the UE Royalties permission. For information on how to manage user roles, see documentation: Organizations.
  4. At the top right, click Retire product.
  5. In the dropdown list, select the product you want to retire.
  6. Click Retire product.

Unretire a Product

After you retire a product, if you subsequently receive revenue for that product, you must unretire the product and start reporting revenue again. To unretire a product, open a private case with Epic Online Services support on eoshelp.epicgames.com/~/eos-case-page.
