Localize Your Store Presence

Localize your storefront content to best reach global Epic Games Store users.

Under 1 min to read

Use the Epic Games Store publishing tools to localize your store presence. When you do this, you make your product more attractive to Epic Games Store users around the world. Epic Games Store users can view and interact with your product in their preferred language.

For a list of the languages supported by the Epic Games Store, see documentation: Supported Languages.

You can use the following tools and services to localize your store presence:

  • Text localization: Localize your product's text with a bulk upload template.
    • Documentation: Localize Your Storefront Text
  • Epic Translation Service: A complimentary localization service provided by Epic Games. If you are unable to provide your own translations, you can request localization support from the Epic Games Store team.
    • Documentation: Localize Your Storefront Text > Request Text Translations from the Epic Translation Service
  • Asset Localization (Image and Video): Upload localized images and videos to ensure that Epic Games Store users see the right assets for their region.
    • Documentation: Localize Your Storefront Assets (Images and Video)

After you create localizations for your product, you can view them, remove specific locale support, and view a history of your template uploads.
