Player Search

View and manage the data that EOS stores about your players on your behalf.

6 mins to read

Use the Player Search page to view and manage the data that Epic Online Services (EOS) stores about your players on your behalf.

Note: For information on the ticketing system (known as "player ticketing") to manage support for players of your game, see the following documentation: Ticketing System.

The Player Search Page

To access the Player Search page:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Dashboard page for your organization. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. Select the correct organization, if prompted.
    • c. In the left navigation, click Dashboard.
  2. On the Dashboard page, click Player Search.

On the Player Search page, you can search players through different user ID types. These are:

  • Product User ID - PUID (Glossary: ProductUserId)
  • Player Account ID - a unique ID from an external platform, for example, a player's Epic Games account ID or Steam ID.
  • Epic Account for development - an Epic account linked with a console development account.

The search results provide the following data about a player:

  • Player Profile
  • Linked Accounts
  • Data Rights

You can export or delete all stored data about a player.

Player Profile

In the Player Profile tab, you can see player-related EOS features that are enabled for your game and the player data for each feature. The features are:

  • Achievements
  • Stats
  • Player Data Storage
  • Anti-cheat
  • Sanctions

Linked Accounts

The Linked Accounts tab displays any external accounts a player has linked to their Product User ID using the EOS Connect Interface (documentation: Connect Interface). This information is useful for your organization's player support. Use it to resolve player issues, such as unlinking an accidentally-linked external account. Here you can also review the full account linking history for a player.

Note: The Linked Accounts tab can also give options for console-development accounts. For more information, see the Console Development Accounts section below.

Unlink a Player's Account

Players might want to change which external accounts they have linked through the EOS Connect Interface. The following are examples of why players might want to do this:

  • The player no longer wants to maintain an account for a particular identity provider.
  • The player wants to link a different identity provider account with a different username.
  • You have to unlink a player's account with a specific identity provider for security reasons or fraud-prevention measures.

To remove a linked account for an identity provider, click Unlink Account.

Notes:

  • Once you unlink an account, your organization no longer tracks it, and the player can no longer sign in to your games using that identity provider unless they re-link.
  • When you unlink an account, it is instantaneous but unlinking does not necessarily remove other linked accounts or remove the player from the EOS service.
  • If you unlink an external account from an Epic Games account, and you try to unlink either the last-linked account for a player, or the only linked account for a player, you see a warning. The warning alerts you that you cannot unlink the player's only sign-in method.

Console Development Accounts

If your players use Epic Account Services to sign in, when you sign up your game for console development with EOS, your console-development account is associated with a new console-Epic-Games account. When you search for an Epic Games account using Player Search, the results return the new console-Epic-Games account as well as the console-development accounts associated with it.

In the Linked Accounts tab, you can delete the console-Epic-Games account. You can use this to test the Epic Games accounts sign-in flow again, re-using the same console-development account.

Note: When you have a console-Epic-Games account, the following applies:

  • You cannot unlink its console-development account.
  • Access to your console-Epic-Games account has restrictions:
    • By default, you cannot access the console-Epic-Games account outside of the console device.
      This is because you cannot use the linked console-development account to sign-in outside the console development environment.
    • You can amend this as follows:
      • When you sign into Epic Games on a web browser using a console-development account, the sign-in prompts you to assign an email and a password to the new console-Epic-Games account.
        After you do this, you can access the console-Epic-Games account outside of the console development environment. This enables you to connect additional external accounts to the console-Epic-Games account.
        Tip: Connect accounts in Epic Games's account management website (go to your account icon, top right of any page in the Developer Portal > Manage Account > Account Settings > APPS AND ACCOUNTS).
      • After you have assigned a username and password to the console-Epic-Games account, you can only unlink the console-Epic-Games account from your console-development account. You cannot delete the console-Epic-Games account itself.

For information on how to become a console developer with EOS, see the following documentation: EOS Get Started Reference > Access the EOS SDK for Consoles.

Data Rights

Use the Data Rights tab to export or delete data stored for a player by EOS on your behalf. You can also export and delete player data for a specific game if a player has played multiple game created by your organization.

Note: You cannot export data while a delete is in progress and you cannot delete data while an export is in progress.

Export Player Data

There are two export requests. These are as follows:

  • Export Product Data

    • To request a player's progression and data for a specific game, click Export Product Data for any game. (Note that this does not include organization data associated with the player's user ID.)
    • This request is linked with the player's Product User ID (PUID) which is unique to each of your games (Glossary: ProductUserId).

  • Export All Data

    • To request a player's player progression and data for all games your organization manages, including all data associated with the player's user ID, click Export All Data.

After you have made either request, EOS emails you, the requester at your organization, a link to a bundle containing the data. This bundle is accessible for 30 days, after which you must make a new request to obtain up-to-date data. You can issue requests to export data for a player once every 10 days. The maximum expected time to process a request is 14 days, though requests are usually processed more quickly.

The bundle might contain encrypted information. See the following documentation for guidance:

  • Player Data Storage
  • Title Storage
  • File Decryption Tool - for information on how to decrypt data.

Delete Player Data

Some circumstances might require deleting a player's data completely. For example:

  • You want to permanently remove a player due to a violation.
  • A player's account data might become corrupted or compromised.
  • A player might specifically request removal of their account data.

There are three deletion options:

  • Delete Product Data

    • To permanently delete a player's player progression and player data for that game, click Delete Product Data.

  • Delete User

    • To delete a player's data across all games managed by your organization, click Delete User at the top of the page.
    • Delete User also unlinks all their accounts for all identity providers and removes any organization data about the player not explicitly associated with any single game. This request has a 10-day grace period in case the deletion request is canceled. During the grace period, the account remains searchable, but the player won't be able to sign in through EOS Connect.

  • Delete Product Data

    • To delete a player's player progression data on a single game, click Delete Product Data.
    • Be aware that linked accounts and organization-level data associated with that player are not affected by this delete and remain stored.
