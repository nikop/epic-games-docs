Publishing Tools Workflow

Introduction to the publishing process.

Under 1 min to read

Epic Games Store publishing tools provide you with everything you need to distribute your product on the Epic Games Store. With these tools, you can maintain your product’s store presence, create offers, create web shop items, manage price tiers, and more.

To ensure a successful launch of your product on the storefront, follow the steps in the Epic Games Store Get Started:

  • Step 1. Onboarding
  • Step 2. (Optional) Publish Your Product Home Page (Early Awareness)
  • Step 3. Finalize and Test Configurations
  • Step 4. Submit for Final Review
  • Step 5. Release Your Product

Each step includes an estimated time to complete. Note that these are general guidelines only and are subject to change. Times can vary based on unique business needs, volume of requests, or the level of configuration required.
