Manage Trader, Tax, and Payout Details

Provide required financial information for your organization.

3 mins to read

To distribute your products on the Epic Games Store, you must go through trader verification, create a tax profile, and activate your payout account. The information you provide must undergo verification by the Epic Games Store team and might take more than two weeks to complete. You cannot submit your product for review, which is a necessary part of the publishing workflow, until you complete this process.

Ensure the financial information you provide throughout this process reflects the entity type you select. For example, if you select "company" or "business" as your entity type, provide the requested information (such as IDs) for your organization rather than personal information.

Complete Trader Verification

Before you can sell products on the Epic Games Store, you must put your Epic Games Store organization through trader verification. This process involves the following:

  • You commit to only offer products or services on the Epic Games Store that comply with the applicable rules of the European Union law.
  • You provide trader information that the Epic Games Store verifies. The Epic Games Store shares some of this information with consumers as part of the Epic Games Store Publisher Index.
    • The Publisher Index is a list of publishers on the Epic Games Store, and the products they sell. For more information, see the Epic Games Store Publisher Index.

Note:

  • If your Epic Games Store organization is new to the Epic Games Store, then you must put the organization through trader verification before you can publish products on the Epic Games Store.
  • As part of the contact details you provide in step 3 below, you must provide an email address that is linked to either an Epic Games account or to your organization.

To complete trader verification in the Developer Portal, see the documentation: Complete Trader Verification.

If Your Trader Verification Succeeds

The Trader Details tab shows a preview of your Verified Trader card. The Epic Games Store displays this information in the Epic Games Store Publisher Index.

If Your Trader Verification Fails

  • The Trader Details tab displays a message that your trader verification failed. To retry, you must go through the verification process again. To do so, contact Epic Games Store support with a private discussion.
  • If you do not receive a trader verification email, make sure the email address you provide is linked to either an Epic Games account or to your organization in the Developer Portal.

Edit Your Trader Information

If you have gone through trader verification and want to edit the information shown on the Trader Details tab, do the following:

  • You can change the options Display my address publicly and Display my phone number publicly on the tab. Click Save Changes to apply your changes.
    • Note: You cannot change the answers that you gave during verification about your home or personal address and home or personal phone number. To change these answers, contact your release manager or create a private discussion on the support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com.
  • To change any other trader information, you must go through verification again. To do so, contact your release manager or create a private discussion on the support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com.

Create Tax Profile

After you complete your trader verification, you must create your tax profile by completing a tax interview in the Developer Portal. You must complete this step before you can activate your payout account.

To create a tax profile, see the documentation: Complete the Tax Interview.

Activate Payout Account

After your tax profile has been created, you must activate your payout account through Hyperwallet. For step-by-step instructions to activate your Hyperwallet account, see the documentation: Activate Your Hyperwallet Account for Payout.

To update your Hyperwallet account after creation, see the documentation: Update Your Hyperwallet Account Information.
