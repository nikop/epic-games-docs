Domain Verification

How to verify your website domain as part of brand review.

5 mins to read

As part of the brand review process (Documentation: Brand Review Process), you must provide a website address (known as a "domain" or "website domain") for your organization and verify your ownership of it.

As part of the domain verification process, Epic Games confirms that the following items are related:

  • Your product (your game or software application)
  • Your brand
  • Your Epic Games Store storefront presence
  • Any other associated web links

Domain verification assists with brand cohesion and mitigates against malicious activities, such as brand spoofing, copyright infringement, malware, and stolen identities.

Domain Verification Evaluation Criteria

To successfully complete the domain verification process, your website domain must:

  • Be publicly accessible.
  • Include your organization name.
  • Include the name and an overview of your product. Note: If you are using Epic-managed distribution (Documentation: Epic-Managed Distribution > Is This For Me?) for your product and have a codename prior to launch, you must:
    • Notify the team by creating a private discussion on the support website (eoshelp.epicgames.com), or by contacting your Business Development manager.
    • Add your product name to your website domain when you launch the product.
  • Adhere to the Epic Games Store content guidelines and be suitable for general audiences. Your website domain must merit a rating of PEGI 12, ESRB Everyone 10+, or lower.
    • Documentation: Content Guidelines.

In addition, you should keep the following criteria in mind when submitting your website for domain verification:

  • Choose one format for your URL and use the same format for your domain verification and brand review applications. For example, foo.com and www.foo.com are treated as different domain names. If you enter foo.com in your domain verification application, you should also enter foo.com in your brand review application.
  • If your apex domain (your top-level or root domain without any subdomains) is approved, all of its subdomains are automatically approved. This includes any subdomains you add to the apex domain in the future. For example, if foo.com is approved, then app.foo.com and account.foo.com are automatically approved on submission.
    • Likewise, if a subdomain is approved, the apex domain and all other subdomains are also approved.
  • If you want to use a subdomain to send or receive email notifications, you must complete verification for the subdomain separately. Documentation: Organizations > Notifications.

Verify Your Domain

Before proceeding with the brand review process, Epic Games must verify ownership of your website domain.

Complete the following steps to verify your domain:

  1. Add a domain and get automated verification: You add a website domain to your organization in the Developer Portal and begin the automated verification process.
  2. Ownership verification: You verify ownership of your website domain.
  3. Manual verification: An Epic Games moderator manually verifies the website domain.

Step 1: Add a Domain and Get Automated Verification

First, you must add a website address ("domain") to your organization and verify your ownership of the domain.

To do this:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to your organization page. To do this:
    1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    2. Select the correct organization for your product.
    3. In the left navigation, click Organization.
  2. Select the Settings tab.
  3. Scroll down the page to Domains.
  4. Click Add Domain.

Successful Automatic Verification: If the domain you add passes the automatic verification process, the Developer Portal moves onto the next step and displays the Verify ownership pane.

Failed Automatic Verification: If the domain fails automatic verification, the Developer Portal does not move on to the next step and the domain is not added to your organization.

This automated verification process ensures that your domain resolves and is not blocked by any deny list.

Note: To delete a domain, in the Domains section of the Settings tab, click the three dots ( ΓÇª ) for the domain you want to delete. Then follow the onscreen instructions.

Step 2: Ownership Verification

After your domain passes the automated verification, you must verify ownership of the domain.

To verify ownership, Epic Online Services (EOS) provides a unique TXT Domain Secret that you manually add to your domain host DNS records for the exact domain name you submitted for verification. EOS confirms your domain ownership when the TXT Domain Secret becomes visible.

Follow the steps below to add the TXT Domain Secret to your domain host DNS records:

  1. In the Developer Portal's Verify ownership pane, copy the starred-out text in the Domain Secret field
  2. Go to your domain host account administration access. Note: The management of your domain host account depends on your domain host provider and is beyond the scope of this page. Contact your website domain host provider for assistance.
  3. In your domain host account, do the following:
    1. Find your domain's DNS records and select the option to add a new record (it might be slightly different text to "add a new record").
    2. Select the TXT record type. Note: If your domain host account doesn't support TXT record (for example, it uses CNAME), contact Epic Games support. See the Support page for details.
    3. In the appropriate field, paste your TXT Domain Secret. This field might be known as Value, Answer, Destination, or some other name.
    4. Save the record on the domain host account. A TXT Domain Secret verification record does not affect your website or DNS settings.
  4. In the Developer Portal's Verify ownership pane, click Check txt record.

Successful Ownership Verification: If your domain's ownership verification is successfully completed, the Developer Portal Organization page's Settings tab displays Verified below the domain name list.

Failed Ownership Verification: If your domain's ownership verification fails, contact Epic Games support. See the Support page for details.

When you have successfully completed both the automated verification step and the ownership verification step, you can use the verified domain in your brand review application.

Step 3: Manual Verification

Epic Games performs the manual verification step during your product's brand review. An Epic Games moderator manually checks the domain name as well as the hosted content to validate that it does not contain anything offensive, explicit, or violate any intellectual property rights.

If Epic Games approves your brand review, the Developer Portal Organization page's Settings tab displays "Approved" below the domain name list.

Moderator Feedback

The Epic Games moderator can provide feedback at any step of verification. If your domain fails verification, Epic Games provides you with a list of issues to address. You must fix the issues before you resubmit your domain for verification.
