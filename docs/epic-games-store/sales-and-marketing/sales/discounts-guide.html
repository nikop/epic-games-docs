Discounts Quick Guide

Use discount campaigns to promote your product.

8 mins to read

In sales and marketing, publishers use the term "SKU" to mean an item that you sell or make available for free. On this page, we use the term "SKU" in the same way for items on the Epic Games Store. In the Developer Portal, a SKU is called an offer.

Use discount campaigns to promote your product with discounted prices. For an overview of promotions, see Quick Guide - Promotional Campaigns.

Create a Discount

Use the Discount Management Tool in the Developer Portal to manage your discounts. You can use it to do the following:

  • Create a custom discount.
  • Create a discount to enter your SKUs in one of the Epic Games Store's preset campaigns. For more information on the available campaign types, see the documentation: Promotions Guide > Epic Games Store Campaign Types.

When you set up the discount, you select which eligible SKUs you want to include in the discount. For more information, see the Eligibility for Discounts section on this page.

For information on how to set up discounts, see the documentation: Manage Discounts.

Note: If you cannot access the Discount Management Tool, create a private discussion on this support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com.

Time Zones in the Discount Management Tool

The time zone that you see in the Discount Management Tool is your time zone. This might not be the same as the time zone associated with Epic-led campaigns.

Note: When you set up a discount, you must select the start and end times in the 24-hour clock format.

Eligibility for Discounts

You can add a SKU to a discount when all of the following apply:

  • The SKU must be in the Live sandbox.
  • The SKU must not be a demo offer.
  • The SKU must not have a base price of $0.
  • The SKU must have a specific available date assigned. You cannot create a discount for a SKU that has an estimated or unknown available date. For more information on SKU (offer) dates, see documentation: Offer Details–Dates.
  • There are special criteria for participation in some promotion types. For more information about the requirements for offer discounts, see documentation: Manage Discounts > Create a Custom Discount or Preset Campaign Discount.

Note: If a SKU is not eligible for discounts, it does not appear in the Discount Management Tool.

Timelines for Discounts

  • Preset campaigns:
    • You can set up a discount for a preset campaign as soon as the preset campaign appears in the Discount Management Tool. Preset campaigns appear in the Discount Management Tool 1.5 to 2 months before the campaign start date.
    • You do not have to enter an SKU into a preset campaign at the start. The SKU can join the campaign part-way through. However, the SKU must join the campaign at least seven days before the campaign end date. After you submit the SKU, allow approximately one hour for validation before the offer joins the campaign.
    • For information on how to stay informed about upcoming preset campaigns, see Keep Up to Date With Sales and Promotional Opportunities.
  • Custom discounts and limited-time free promotions:
    • Custom discounts and limited-time free promotions are applied immediately after passing validation. However, your discount may require up to 24 hours of processing and validation before being published. We recommend you take this processing period into account when planning your custom discounts and limited-time free promotions.

Guidelines for Discounts

Epic Games has a set of guidelines that applies to discounts. Make sure you are familiar with them before you set up a discount.

  • Documentation: Manage Discounts–Discount Guidelines.

Discounts for Multiple Products (Bulk Discounts)

To set up discounts for multiple products at the same time, use the Upload Bulk Discount option in the Discount Management Tool. For more information, see documentation: Manage Discounts–Upload Bulk Discounts.

Edit or Cancel a Scheduled Discount

You can use the Discount Management Tool to edit or change a scheduled discount. A scheduled discount is a discount that has a start date in the future.

For information on how to edit or cancel a discount, see documentation: Discount Management Guide > Discount Management Options.

Note:

  • You cannot cancel a live discount, because the Epic Games Store Pricing Restriction Policy forbids this action. Make sure you are familiar with the Pricing Restriction Policy before you make changes to your discounts. Documentation: Pricing Overview–Pricing Restrictions.

Restrictions on Discounts

Preset Campaigns: Mid-Campaign Discounts

You can enroll a SKU into an in-progress preset campaign, but the SKU must join the campaign at least seven days before the campaign's end date. This seven-day restriction ensures that your product is promoted for a meaningful length of time. It also prevents a short discount period from triggering the standard 30-day cooldown restriction between discounts.

Note:

  • When you submit an SKU for enrollment in a preset campaign, it takes approximately one hour for the SKU to be enrolled into the campaign.
  • If you want your product to be considered for marketing support from Epic Games, you must follow the official submission deadlines for the preset campaign. Epic Games communicates these deadlines in the Promotions News & Updates emails and Community Portal threads. If you submit a discount after these deadlines, your product will still be included and tagged in the preset campaign's pages, but it might not be eligible for additional marketing support. For more information on marketing support from Epic Games, see Marketing Overview.

Discount Amounts

  • For preset campaigns and custom discounts, you can discount an offer between 5% and 95%.
  • For limited-time free promotions, the discount rate is set to 100% and cannot be changed.

Note: If you offer a product for free and then change it to a charged-for price tier, then you must leave the product at the same price for at least 30 days before you can discount the product again.

More information:

  • For information on SKU prices, see the documentation: Pricing Overview > Pricing Restrictions.

Introductory Offers and Pre-Purchase Offers

You must set up introductory offers and pre-purchase offers on a SKU before the available date of the SKU. In other words, you must set them up before the SKU is available for Epic Games Store users to buy.

You can only apply a discount to a SKU after the SKU has been at its standard price for at least 30 days. Note: Pre-purchase periods do not count towards this 30-day period. For more information on guidelines for discounts, see documentation: Manage Discounts–Discount Guidelines.

Discounts Following a Price Change

If you change the price on a SKU in any currency, you must leave the price at the new level for at least 30 days before you can set a discount on the SKU. This applies regardless of the currency. For example, it still applies if you change the price of the SKU in only one currency.

Note:

  • Make sure you read the following guidelines that relate to price changes:
    • Documentation: Manage Discounts–Discount Guidelines
    • Documentation: Pricing Overview–Pricing Restrictions
  • Before you make any price changes, we strongly recommend that you check all of your scheduled or live discounts to ensure they comply with the guidelines above, and edit the discounts as necessary. For information on how to edit discounts, see documentation: Manage Discounts.

Price Changes Following a Discount

When a discount on a SKU ends, you can change the SKU price in any or all currencies immediately.

Note: If you change a price on a SKU, you must leave the price at the new level for at least 30 days in all currencies before you can apply a discount to the SKU.

Note:

  • Before you make any price changes, make sure you read the following guidelines:
    • Documentation: Manage Discounts–Discount Guidelines
    • Documentation: Pricing Overview–Pricing Restrictions

Regional Availability

You can change the regional availability of your SKUs and still run discounts on those SKUs.

Before you make any regional changes, consider the following guidelines:

  • You cannot make a product unavailable in a region if that product has a live discount, introductory offer, pre-purchase, or special offer. You must wait until after the discount, introductory offer, pre-purchase, or special offer is over before you make the product unavailable in the region. For more information on how to manage regions on a SKU (offer), see documentation: Offer Details-Regions.
  • When you make a SKU available in a region, the following guidelines apply:
    • The SKU must stay at the same price for at least 30 days before you can discount it.
    • If you have any scheduled discounts (scheduled discounts are discounts with start dates in the future):
      • You must leave the SKU's price at the same level for at least 30 days before you can discount it. This means that you must change any scheduled discount start dates to happen 30 days after the date of the region setting change.
    • Note: Region changes impact discounts globally: if you change one region, this impacts any scheduled discounts in all other regions.
    • For more information on how to manage regions on a SKU (offer), see documentation: Offer Details-Regions.
  • You can make a product available in a region if that product has a live special offer (an introductory offer, a pre-purchase offer, or a combination of both). If you do this, the special offer applies for the remaining days of the original duration in the new region. Example: if you have an introductory offer that runs for 30 days, and you make the product available in a region after 10 days, then the introductory offer will run for the remaining 20 days in all regions, including the region where you just made it available.
  • When you change your product's regional availability, the following conditions apply:
    • If the product has been unavailable in the region for 30 days or less:
      • You can make the product available in the region and run discounts on the same day.
      • You must leave the price the same for at least 30 days before you can make the product unavailable again in this region.
    • If the product has been unavailable in the region for greater than 30 days:
      • You must leave the price the same for at least 30 days in the region before you can make the product available in the region.
      • You can make the product unavailable in the region again as soon as the discount ends.
  • For more information on region availability and pricing, see the following:
    • Documentation: Manage Discounts–Discount Guidelines
    • Documentation: Pricing Overview–Pricing Restrictions

Troubleshoot Discounts

When you create a discount, you must submit it for review. Most issues with discounts are caused at the review stage, by discounts that don’t meet the discount guidelines. Make sure you are familiar with them before you submit a discount for review. Documentation: Manage Discounts–Discount Guidelines.

To find out about how to resolve pricing issues in the Discount Management Tool, see documentation: Manage Discounts–Submit Discounts for Validation and Review.
