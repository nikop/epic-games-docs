Delete a Variable Definition (Localized Rich Presence)

How to delete a localized rich presence variable definition.

Under 1 min to read

Use localized rich presence variables to display dynamic content in your rich presence messages, such as the map that a player is currently on. Create variable definitions to ensure that only valid content can appear in your presence messages. For more information, see Variable Definitions for Localized Rich Presence Messages — Introduction.

Note:

  • When you delete a variable definition, you permanently remove the variable definition. You cannot undo this.
  • You cannot delete a variable definition if there are variables based on it in one or more templates.

To delete a variable definition:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Localized Presence page. To do this:
    a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    b. Select the correct organization for your product.
    c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.
    d. In the left navigation, click Epic Online Services.
    e. In the left navigation, under Accounts and Social, click Localized Presence.
  2. Click the Variable Definitions tab:

  1. In Deployment, select the appropriate deployment.

  2. In the list of variable definitions, locate the variable definition you want to delete.

    • You can search the list. See documentation: Find a Specific Variable Definition.

  3. For that variable definition, click the three dots ( … ).

  4. Click Delete.
