Create Custom Price Tiers

Create custom regional pricing for your offers.

12 mins to read

When you create an offer for your product, or a web shop item for your web shop, you set a base USD price for that offer or web shop item. By default, the Developer Portal calculates and applies prices for all other regions, based on the USD value you set.

As an alternative, you can create your own custom price tier to determine your own regional pricing in place of the automatically-calculated price tiers. For more information on how to set price tiers, see documentation: Pricing Overview > Pricing Restrictions.

After you create a custom price tier, you must send it to the Epic Games Store team for review. After the price tier passes review, you can assign it to your offers and web shop items. The Developer Portal never automatically assigns your custom price tiers to offers or web shop items.

Create a Custom Price Tier

To create a custom price tier:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Custom Price Tiers page. To do this:

    • (a) Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

    • (b) Select the correct organization for your product.

      (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the onscreen instructions.)

    • (c) In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.

    • (d) In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Custom Price Tiers.

  2. At the top right, click Create price tier.
  3. Enter details of the price tier as follows:
    • Enter tier name: Type a name that describes your custom price tier.
      • This name appears in the list of available price tiers when you set the prices on an offer and web shop items.
        • TIP: Enter a clear and descriptive name. Choose a name that makes it easy to identify the price tier when you set prices on your offers and web shop items.
      • Select base price: Choose a base USD price for the tier.
        • The Developer Portal automatically populates the price tier with prices for the various regions based on the price you select here. You can leave these prices as the default values, or edit them.
      • For each region, in the Regional price column you can keep the default price or enter a custom price.
        • The Standard price column lists the original price that the Developer Portal automatically generated for each region.
        • For more information on regions, see the Epic Games Store Region List section on this page.
  4. Click Submit.
    • The Developer Portal sends your custom price tier to the Epic Games Store for review. For more information about the review process, see the Check the Status of a Price Tier's Review section on this page.

Check the Status of a Price Tier's Review

When your price tier is in review, you can check on its progress in the Status column on the Custom Price Tiers page.

To go to the Custom Price Tiers page:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

  2. Select the correct organization for your product.

    (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the onscreen instructions.)

  3. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.

  4. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Custom Price Tiers.

Each price tier has one of the following statuses:

  • Draft: You have not completely set up the price tier. Before you send it for review, you must edit it to add more detail. For information on how to edit a price tier, see the Edit a Custom Price Tier section on this page.
  • Ready for Review: You can send the price tier for review. For information on how to create and submit a price tier for review, see the Create a Custom Price Tier section on this page.
  • Queued for Review: You submitted the price tier for review, but the review is not complete yet.
    • Price tier reviews usually take 1-2 business days.
    • Note: When the review is complete, the price tier has the status Live (if it passes review) or Confirmation required (if the Epic Games Store team found any issues with the price tier during the review).
  • Confirmation Required: The review is complete. However, during the review, the Epic Games Store team found issues with the price tier (for example, they found a misplaced decimal point).
    • To view the details of the review, select the price tier from the table and read the feedback at the top of the page.
    • You can do one of the following:
      • Update the price tier based on the feedback. Then, at the top right, click Submit to resubmit the price tier for review. For information on how to update a price tier, see the Edit a Custom Price Tier section of this page.
      • Proceed with the price tier without making any changes. To do so, at the top right, click Submit.
  • Live: The price tier passed review. You can assign the price tier to your offers and web shop items.
    • Note: the Epic Games Store does not automatically assign custom price tiers to offers or web shop items–you must assign them manually. For information, see the Add a Custom Price Tier to an Offer section on this page.

Add a Custom Price Tier to a New Offer or Web Shop Item

To add a custom price tier to a new offer or web shop item, follow the standard process to create the offer or web shop item. When you set the price, select the custom price tier.

For more information, see the following documentation:

  • Manage Offers > Create Additional Offers
  • Manage Web Shop Items

Add a Custom Price Tier to an Existing Offer

To assign a custom price tier to an existing offer:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Offers page. To do this:

    • (a) Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

    • (b) Select the correct organization for your product.

      (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the onscreen instructions.)

    • (c) In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.

    • (d) In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Offers.

  2. Click the offer that you want to assign the custom price to.
  3. On the Offer Details screen, click Price.
  4. In Price tier, select your custom price tier.
  5. Click Save Changes.

Note: restrictions on price changes:

When you want to change an existing price on an offer, there are some restrictions. For details, see documentation: Pricing Overview > Pricing Restrictions.

To learn more about offers, see documentation: Manage Offers.

Add a Custom Price Tier to an Existing Web Shop Item

To assign a custom price tier to an existing web shop item:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Web Shop page. To do this:

    • (a) Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

    • (b) Select the correct organization for your product.

      (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the onscreen instructions.)

    • (c) In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.

    • (d) In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Web Shop.

  2. Click the web shop item that you want to assign the custom price to.
  3. On the Web Shop Item Details screen, click Price.
  4. In Price tier, select your custom price tier.
  5. Click Save Changes.

Note: When you want to change an existing price on a web shop item, there are some restrictions. For details, see documentation: Pricing Overview > Pricing Restrictions.

For information about web shops, see documentation: Manage Your Web Shop.

View the Details of a Custom Price Tier

On the Custom Price Details page, you can view the details of a custom price tier, including pricing information and a list of the offers and web shop items that currently use that price tier.

To view the details of a custom price tier:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Custom Price Tiers page. To do this:

    • (a) Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

    • (b) Select the correct organization for your product.

      (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the onscreen instructions.)

    • (c) In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.

    • (d) In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Custom Price Tiers.

  2. Click the price tier you want to view.

Edit a Custom Price Tier

Note:

  • You cannot edit a price tier that has the status Queued for Review. If you want to edit it, wait until the review is complete and the status of the price tier changes. For more information on statuses, see the Check the Status of a Price Tier's Review section of this page.
  • You can edit price tiers that are currently assigned to offers and web shop items. After the review is complete, your edits automatically apply to any offers and web shop items that the price tier is assigned to. This includes offers and web shop items that are live on the storefront.

To edit a custom price tier:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Custom Price Tiers page. To do this:

    • (a) Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

    • (b) Select the correct organization for your product.

      (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the onscreen instructions.)

    • (c) In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.

    • (d) In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Custom Price Tiers.

  2. Click the price tier you want to edit.
  3. Edit the pricing information for the price tier.
  4. To submit your changes for review, click Submit.

Delete a Custom Price Tier

You can delete a custom price tier. Note that you cannot undo this action.

Note: If a price tier is currently assigned to one or more offers or web shop items, then you must remove it from these offers and web shop items before you can delete it. To do so, select a different price tier on these offers and web shop items.

See the section above for information on how to select a price tier:

  • Add a Custom Price Tier to an Existing Offer
  • Add a Custom Price Tier to an Existing Web Shop Item

To delete a custom price tier:

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Custom Price Tiers page. To do this:

    • (a) Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

    • (b) Select the correct organization for your product.

      (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the onscreen instructions.)

    • (c) In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.

    • (d) In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Custom Price Tiers.

  2. In the list of price tiers, find the price tier you want to delete.
  3. Click the menu ( … ) icon and select Delete.
