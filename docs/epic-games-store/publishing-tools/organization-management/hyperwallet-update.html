Update Your Hyperwallet Account Information

Update the information on your Hyperwallet account.

1 min to read

After activating a Hyperwallet account for your organization, you might need to change your account details or add new transfer methods. Depending on the information you want to update, you can make changes to your Hyperwallet account by using the Hyperwallet account portal (epicgames.hyperwallet.com/) or by contacting Epic Games Support at eoshelp.epicgames.com.

For any updates to your Hyperwallet account that are not listed below, contact Hyperwallet Support at pay.hyperwallet.com to update your account information.

Access the Hyperwallet account portal at epicgames.hyperwallet.com/ and follow the on-screen instructions to update any of the following pieces of information:

  • Transfer methods (such as a bank account or PayPal).
  • Phone number
    • Note: You must activate your account before you can change your phone number. Documentation: Activate Your Hyperwallet Account for Payout.
  • Payout currency
  • Hyperwallet account password.
    • Note: If you cannot access your Hyperwallet account portal, go to the Hyperwallet Reset Password page at pay.hyperwallet.com to reset your password.

If you want to update any of the following pieces of information, contact Epic Games Support at eoshelp.epicgames.com. To make these changes, Epic Games Support closes your current Hyperwallet account and creates a new Hyperwallet account for you with the updated information.

  • Legal name
  • Email address
  • Tax profile information (for example, if you want to change your profile from an Individual profile to a Business profile)
  • Date of birth
  • Address
  • Tax Identification Number (TIN)

Epic Games Support cannot complete update requests on behalf of any third parties representing you. Only contact Epic Games Support if you are the owner of the Hyperwallet account.

Close Your Hyperwallet Account

If you want to close your Hyperwallet account, contact Epic Games Support at eoshelp.epicgames.com.
