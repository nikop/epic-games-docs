Pricing Your Product

This page describes the Epic Games Store's restrictions and best practices around pricing. It also includes how to work with your Release Manager to set up your regional prices. Pricing is one of the most important decisions to make as you set up your product. Consider the following factors:

  • Your production costs
  • Your target audience
  • Perceived value of the product
  • Gameplay duration and replayability
  • Similar products on the market today
  • Upcoming discounts and sales you may offer

In addition, consider regularly evaluating and adjusting prices based on user feedback and changes in the market. Your pricing strategy helps you build a sustainable business while delivering value to your audience.

Pricing Restrictions

The Epic Games Store is committed to facilitating fair and competitive pricing for your products. However, ultimately the prices you set are up to you. Note that some regions have laws in place to protect users against price gouging and price manipulation. To maintain this commitment, rules apply to all prices.

Manage your price tiers

You can suggest pricing for your offers with the Epic Games Store - Regional Price Tiers form. Your Release Manager can provide you with this form. It contains details of all Epic Games Store pricing regions and their associated currencies.

To complete the form:

  1. Select Base Price Tier for USA / ROW to populate default regional equivalents across all regions.
  2. Manually enter the Suggested Retail Price (SRP) for any regions that do not have the desired suggested price in the SRP column. The Delta column shows the % difference between our suggested prices and what's entered under SRP.

Note: If you submit suggested pricing for multiple offers, you can copy Columns D/E in the sheet. You do not need to submit multiple forms.

Discounts

In the Developer Portal (Dev Portal), you can create and manage discounts for your offers. For information, see the page Manage Discounts.
