Organizations

Overview of the organization management tools in the Developer Portal.

10 mins to read

For the Epic Games Store, Epic Online Services (EOS), Fortnite Creative, and Epic Tools, data relating to your account in the Developer Portal is organized in a hierarchy, with "organization" at the top.

What is an Organization?

An organization is a group that creates and owns products, or that uses Twinmotion or RealityScan. It is usually a company or other business, such as a game studio or publisher, but it can also be a single developer.

In the Developer Portal, you associate your Epic Games account with an organization. Each Epic Games account associated with an organization is a "member" of the organization. An organization can have multiple members. Typically, these members are people who work for the group that the organization represents.

Members of an organization manage products that they can add to the Developer Portal on behalf of the organization.

Members of your organization can use Fortnite Creative and Epic Tools to create digital products. Epic Tools include Unreal Engine, Twinmotion, and RealityScan.

Members of your organization can use Epic Developer Resources to do the following:

  • Distribute games on the Epic Games Store.
  • Report royalties due for products that use Unreal Engine.
  • Use Epic Online Services to create cross-play games.
  • Use Kids Web Services to help you create safe and compliant digital experiences for young audiences.

The people responsible for the product must associate their Epic Games accounts with the organization. You can use the Developer Portal to configure and manage the group of people in your organization.

Further information:

  • To find out more about licensing for Epic Tools, see Unreal Subscription Licensing at www.unrealengine.com/en-US/license.
  • To learn more about the free services and tools that Epic Games provides, see Epic Developer Resources at dev.epicgames.com/docs.

For an overview of the Developer Portal data hierarchy, see the following documentation:

  • Developer Portal > Introduction > Your Organization and Products

Create or Join an Organization

You can create a new organization in the Developer Portal, or join an organization that someone else has already created.

Create an Organization

When You First Sign In to the Developer Portal

When you first sign in, the Developer Portal prompts you to create a new organization associated with your account.

(Note that if the Epic Games Business Development team set up your Epic Games account for you, when they added your account to the Developer Portal, they set up your organization. In this situation, the Developer Portal does not prompt you to create a new organization.)

After You Have Signed In to the Developer Portal

You can create a new organization at any time.

To create a new organization:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

  2. In the left navigation, click the name of your organization at the top (shown as "Your…" in the following image):

  1. In the Choose your organization pop-up window, click Create organization.

  2. Follow the on-screen instructions to set up your organization.

Join an Existing Organization

To associate an Epic Games account with an existing organization, a member with appropriate permissions (Admin role, Owner role, or the Team Management permission) can invite that person to join the organization. For more information, see the Members section of this page.

If you are a member of more than one organization, at sign-in a prompt asks which organization you want to access. After you have signed in, you can select a different organization at any time.

Organization Page

Use the Organization page in the Developer Portal to manage your organization members, their roles, and to view agreements.

Page Navigation

To access the Organization page:

  1. Go to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. In the left navigation, click Organization. (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)

There are multiple tabs on the Organization page. Click a tab's title to see the contents of the tab. Note: You might not see all the tabs. Some tabs are only visible to organization admins or owners.

Organization Page Tabs

Members

Each member has a role in the organization. The role gives them permission to access different tools in the Developer Portal.

Add New Members
You must have the correct permissions (Admin role, Owner role, or the Team Management permission) to add a new member to your organization. If you have the correct permissions, click Invite new. (If you do not have the correct permissions, you can’t see this button.)

The Developer Portal adds the new member to your organization when they accept the invitation.

Remove Members or Change Access Rights
A member with the correct permissions (Admin role, Owner role, or the Team Management permission) can remove or change the access rights of a member. To do this:

  1. Click the checkbox against the member name on the member list.
  2. Choose the action you want from the pop-up menu at the bottom of the page.

Notifications

This page is only visible to an organization's admins or owners. Here, you can set up email notifications regarding updates to Epic Games Store and Epic Online Services, as well as notifications about your products.

Note: You can only add email addresses for domains that are already verified. (To verify a domain, see the Domains section of this page.)

Roles

Each member has a role. A role grants a member permission to access different tools in the Developer Portal. For information on how to assign roles to members of your organization, see Manage Roles.

For detailed information on the available roles, see Available Roles in the Developer Portal.

Custom Roles
A member with the appropriate permissions (Admin role, Owner role, or the Team Management permission) can create custom roles for their organization. You can allocate your organization members bespoke permissions to access Epic Games Store and EOS features in the Developer Portal.

For information on how to define custom roles, see Manage Roles > Create a Custom Role.

Publishing Tools Permissions
To publish your product on the Epic Games Store, a member of your organization must have the correct publishing tools permissions. For details, see documentation: Epic Games Store > Organization Management > Publishing Tools Permissions.

Tax Info

If you use the Epic Games Store to distribute a product:
You must complete a tax profile on the Tax Info tab. For information on how to complete or edit your tax profile, see the Epic Games Store documentation: Manage Trader, Tax, and Payout Details.

If you use a different Epic Games service:
You do not need to set up a tax profile in the Developer Portal. However, you might need to set up a tax profile in the service that you use. For more information, see the documentation for that service.

Payout Info

The Payout Info tab shows money paid to your organization. You can only see the contents of this tab if you have set up your tax profile and payout account.

If you use the Epic Games Store to distribute a product:
You must set up your payout account so that Epic Games can send any due payments to you. For information on how to set up or edit your payout account, see the Epic Games Store documentation: Manage Trader, Tax, and Payout Details.

If you use a different Epic Games service:
You do not need to set up payout info in the Developer Portal. However, you might need to set up payout info in the service that you use. For more information, see the documentation for that service.

Agreements

Use the Agreements tab to review the active agreements with Epic Games for your organization.

The Epic Games Store documentation has details of Epic Games Store agreements. See: Agreements and Disclosures.

Profile

This is the representation of your organization as follows:

  • In the Developer Portal, to members of your organization.
  • In the Epic Developer Community (EDC) (dev.epicgames.com/community), if you make your organization's profile public.

Your organization profile includes a profile image (for example a company logo), a cover image (a background image), text to introduce your organization, and social media links (for example a link to your organization’s LinkedIn profile).

Your Organization’s Profile as a Public Profile
You can choose to make your organization profile public on the Epic Developer Community (EDC) (dev.epicgames.com/community).

If you do this, members of your organization are associated with your organization publicly on the EDC. This means that any activity by members of your organization on the EDC is publicly associated with your organization’s profile.

For example, when a member of your organization creates guides (known as “tutorials”) in the EDC or answers questions in the EDC forums, all users of the EDC can see this activity on the profile of your organization, as well as the member’s personal profile. This helps users of the EDC discover your organization and any tutorials your organization's members create.

Make your Organization’s Profile Public
To make your organization's profile public:

  1. Go to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. In the left navigation, click Organization.
  3. On the Organization page, click Profile.
  4. Add the following to your organization’s profile content:
    • a. Enter text for the organization biography.
    • b. Upload a profile image
    • c. Upload a background image.
  5. Select Make this profile public.

Settings

In the Settings tab, you can configure your organization type, domains, and console developer access.

General Settings

Use this section to configure information about your organization such as changing its name, country, or adding an address or support email.

Claim VAT/Tax

Use this section to add information about your organization’s tax (or VAT) status. If your organization is registered to pay tax (or VAT), Epic Games might not have to charge you tax (or VAT) on any products or services it provides to your organization.

Organization Type

An admin or owner can configure your organization type. Your organization type can either be an individual or a company:

  • Individual: The default type for an organization. This type indicates that all of the members of the organization are acting on their personal behalf.
  • Enterprise: This type is available for incorporated entities. It indicates that your organization represents a legal entity.

Note: If you change your organization type to Enterprise, you cannot change it back to Individual.

Domains

Use this tab to add and verify website and email addresses (“domains”) associated with your organization.

Verifying your domain protects you, all your players or users, and Epic Games from malicious activities, such as brand spoofing, phishing, malware, stolen identity, and copyright infringement.

For guidance on the domain verification process, see the documentation: EOS > Domain Verification.

You must add and verify your domain in order to complete the following processes:

  • Email notifications.
    (Documentation: See the Notifications section of this page.)
  • Brand review.
    This is essential to using Epic Account Services in your product.
    (Documentation: EOS > Brand Review.)

Console Developer Access

You can only access the EOS SDK for console downloads, and their associated documentation, if you have the appropriate permissions. This section provides information on how to get the permissions.

Configure Console Developer Access

To find out how to get access to the EOS SDK for console downloads, and their associated documentation, follow the steps below.

  1. In the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal), click Organization from the left navigation.
  2. On the Organization page, click Settings.
  3. In the Settings tab, scroll down to Console Developer Access.
  4. Locate the console you want to develop games, and click Configure. This displays the console’s access-request page.
  5. Follow the on-screen instructions.

Seats

If all of the following apply to your organization, then you must purchase seats:

  • You use Unreal Engine for commercial purposes.
  • You have generated more than $1 million in revenue in the past 12 months.
  • You are not creating a game or other software application that relies on engine code at runtime that will be licensed to third-party end-users.

There are two licensing models for use with Epic Tools. To find out more about licensing for Epic Tools, see Unreal Subscription Licensing at www.unrealengine.com/en-US/license.

Purchase Seats

You can purchase seats directly from Epic Games sales, from resellers, or through self-service purchasing tools in the Developer Portal.

To purchase seats in the Developer Portal:

  1. Go to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. In the left navigation, click Organization. (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)
  3. In the left navigation, find the Epic tools section and open the tool you want to buy seats for (such as Unreal Engine).
  4. In the left navigation, click Seats.
  5. Click Buy seats.
  6. Follow the on-screen instructions.

View and Manage Seats

If you have appropriate permissions for your organization (Admin role, Owner role, Epic Tools Manager role, or the Seat Management permission), you can use the Seats page to do the following:

  • Keep track of how many seats you have purchased and how many you have used.
  • Assign each seat to a member of your organization.

When you assign a seat to a member, the member can then see the appropriate Epic Tools section in the Developer Portal (for example, Epic tools > Unreal Engine).
