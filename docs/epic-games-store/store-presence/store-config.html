Configure Store Settings

Manage general store settings and product-level details.

7 mins to read

Manage your product's configuration and regions and ratings settings in the Store Settings page of the Developer Portal.

  • Product Configuration

    Collects key details about your product, such as metadata, specifications, and other high-level information used to populate your store presence.

    This page contains guidance on product configuration.

  • Regions and Ratings

    Contains your product's age rating certificates and classifications as well as your intended regions for distribution.
    For information on this, see the documentation: Manage Regions and Ratings.

    You must complete both of these sections to distribute your product on the Epic Games Store.

Note:

  • On the Store Settings page, the status of both the Product Configuration and Regions and Ratings sections display for each sandbox to help you monitor the progress of your product through the publishing workflow. (For guidance on these statuses, see documentation: Release Management > Status Definitions.)
  • The Product Configuration and Regions and Ratings sections both contain multiple subsections that collect specific categories of information. When you complete a subsection, a checkmark displays next to its name.
  • When you edit a specific subsection, preview windows appear to the left of the settings panel. These previews show where the information and assets appear in the storefront.
  • For asset specifications and best practices, see the following documentation:
    • For PC (Windows, macOS): Storefront Media Guide.
    • For mobile (iOS, Android): Mobile Early Adopter—Best Practices For Your Store Presence .

Manage Product Configuration

  1. In the Developer Portal, go to the Store Settings page. To do this:

    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).

    • b. Select the correct organization for your product.

      (To check, see Organization at the top of the left navigation. To change organization, click Organization and follow the on-screen instructions.)

    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.

    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Store Settings.

  2. In the Product Configuration section, click Edit.
  3. Provide the product details as described below.

Product details

In the Product details section, set the information that appears on the storefront as part of your product's store presence. Unless otherwise noted below, this information is mandatory.

  • Product login: Specify which logins are required to play the game.
  • Product logo: The primary logo for your product featured in the storefront. For asset specifications and best practices, see documentation:
    • For PC (Windows, macOS): Storefront Media Guide.
    • For mobile (iOS, Android): Mobile Early Adopter—Best Practices For Your Store Presence .
  • Product display name: The title of your product displayed to Epic Games Store users in the storefront.
  • Short description: A simple and straightforward description of your product, this information may be used throughout the storefront as well as with SEO and sharing settings.
    • Note: Because the maximum length of this field is 280 characters across all languages, we recommend limiting English text to roughly 200 characters.
  • Genres: Select up to three genres that describe your product.
  • Features: Select all features supported by your product at the time of release. Do not include features that are not supported by the initial launch of your product. Additional features can be added in tandem with future releases as needed.
  • Product icon: An icon used to represent your product when it appears on the Epic Games Mobile Store. This field applies only for products on mobile platforms (iOS, Android). It is mandatory for products on these platforms.
  • Supported languages: Specify the Text and Audio languages supported for your product. Only the Text field is required.
  • Legal Info: The legal footer and privacy policy for your product. Only the footer is required.
    • Tip: If you do not have a privacy policy, we recommend that you create a page on your site stating that this information is not currently available, to promote transparency with users.
  • Terms of Use: Specify the link to any additional terms a user is required to sign, such as an End User License Agreement (EULA). This field is mandatory for products that require users to agree to additional terms before using the product.
    • Documentation: Store Requirements Overview > Additional Distribution Requirements > Consent to Additional Terms.
  • User Support: Provide either a support page URL or email address for users to access if they require assistance or have questions about your product. Completing at least one of these fields is required.
  • Developer and Publisher: The name of the developer and publisher of the product.

Specifications

Use the Specifications section to set the platforms supported by your product (for example, Windows or iOS) and the recommended technical requirements for an optimal user experience. This information might change when you develop your product, so before you submit your product for final review by the Epic Games Store team, check that these details are up to date.

To set the specifications for a platform:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. Select the correct organization for your product.
  3. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Store Settings.
  4. Under Product configuration, click Edit.
  5. Click Specifications.
  6. Click the name of the platform (PC, Mac, iOS, or Android) you want to set.
  7. If you want your product to support this platform, select My product supports this OS.
    • Only enable the platforms that are supported by your product at the time of its initial release on the Epic Games Store. Do not include platforms that you only plan to support in the future. You can add additional platforms for your app's future releases as needed.
  8. Enter the specifications for the platform.
    • For mobile platforms, see Specifications for Mobile Platforms on this page.
  9. Click Save changes.

Note: You cannot set platform information for offer types that do not have an associated artifact, such as editions, season passes, or consumables. (Documentation: Manage Offers > Offer Types.) These offers use the platforms assigned to your base product. To ensure your offers that do not have artifacts are assigned the appropriate platforms, you must promote these offers to the Stage sandbox any time you update the platforms assigned to your base product and push your product to Stage.

Specifications for Mobile Platforms

iOS
  • Installation size: The total size of all files that Epic Games Store users need to download and install before they can use the app.
    • You must enter a value for this specification.
  • Download size: The initial download size that Epic Games Store users need to download the app without running it. This size does not include any additional files that the app must download after the initial app download. This is a useful metric for Epic Games Store users who want to download the app but do not want to run it right away.
    • You must enter a value for this specification.
  • Application version: Enter the build version of your app.
    • You must enter a value for this specification.
  • Minimum iOS version: Enter the minimum version of iOS that users will need to run your app.
    • You must enter a value for this specification.
Android
  • Installation size: The total size of all files that Epic Games Store users need to download and install before they can use the app.
    • You must enter a value for this specification.
  • Download size: The initial download size that Epic Games Store users need to download the app without running it.This size does not include any additional files that the app must download after the initial app download. This is a useful metric for Epic Games Store users who want to download the app but do not want to run it right away.
    • You must enter a value for this specification.
    • When you upload and activate your Android build (.APK file), the Developer Portal automatically collects this information from your build and replaces the information you entered manually. For information on how to upload and activate Android builds, see documentation: Upload and Test Your Android Builds.
  • Application version: The build version of your app.
    • You must enter a value for this specification.
    • When you upload and activate your Android build (.APK file), the Developer Portal automatically collects this information from your build and replaces the information you entered manually. For information on how to upload and activate Android builds, see documentation: Upload and Test Your Android Builds.
  • Minimum Android version: The minimum version of Android that users will need to run your app.
    • When you upload and activate your Android build (.APK file), the Developer Portal automatically collects this information from your build and replaces the information you entered manually. For information on how to upload and activate Android builds, see documentation: Upload and Test Your Android Builds.

Social media

In the Social Media section, provide links to any social media pages available for your product. This information displays in the Follow Us section of your Product Details Page in the storefront.

Banner

The Banner displays on your Product Details Page and can be used to emphasize key phases of your go-to-market planning, such as beta or early access programs or in-game events.

To add a banner to your product’s storefront presence:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. Select the correct organization for your product.
  3. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Store Settings.
  4. Under Product configuration, click Edit.
  5. Click Banner.
  6. Enable the toggle (Enable for my product).
  7. Enter the details for your banner content.
  8. Click Save changes.
