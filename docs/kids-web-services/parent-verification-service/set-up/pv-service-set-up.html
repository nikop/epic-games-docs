Set Up the Parent Verification Service

How to get started with the Parent Verification Service.

3 mins to read

Accept the Parent Verification Service Terms

Before you can start setting up your Parent Verification (PV) Service, the Developer Portal prompts you to accept the terms of service.

For your reference, the terms are viewable on the Developer Terms Hub website at kidswebservices.com/en-US/developer-terms-hub. They are as follows:

  • KWS General Terms: (kidswebservices.com/general-terms) (including any terms incorporated therein by reference).
  • KWS Service Specific Terms for Parent Verification: (kidswebservices.com/parent-verification-terms)

The steps are as follows:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal at dev.epicgames.com/portal.

  2. In the left navigation, click the name of your organization (shown as "Your..." in the following image):

  1. In the Choose your organization panel, click the name of your organization and click Continue.

  2. To customize a specific product, select the name of the product in the left navigation under Your Products. Otherwise, proceed to the next step.

  3. In the left navigation, click Kids Web Services.

  4. On the Kids Web Services page, click Get started now in the Parent Verification panel.

  5. Read and follow the on-screen prompts to accept the service terms.

In the Developer Portal, each organization has a test environment and a production environment, each with its own configuration and integration details. The test environment enables you to thoroughly test the PV Service before setting it up in the production environment.

Choose Verification Methods

In your Parent Verification Service configuration, you must choose which verification methods to make available to parents or guardians (see documentation: Parent Verification Service Verification Methods).

The methods KWS presents to the parent or guardian on the verification Web UI screen also depend on the child’s location, which you pass to the PV Service API.

Note:

  • You must choose enough verification methods to provide global coverage.
  • Enabling more verification methods increases the options parents or guardians have to verify their adult status, increasing the chances of successful verification.

You can choose verification methods in two ways. Either:

  • Option A — For all the products in your organization.
  • Option B — For an individual product.

For more information about these options, see the documentation: Choose to Set Up KWS for Your Organization or Product.

For both options, you choose the verification methods in the Developer Portal, on the Parent Verification page’s Verification Methods tab, but the way you access the tab depends on which option you use.

To configure the verification methods, follow the steps below:

Step 1 — Go to the Parent Verification Page

Option A: For all the products in your organization

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal at dev.epicgames.com/portal.

  2. In the left navigation, click Kids Web Services.

  3. In the left navigation, click Parent Verification.

Option B: For an individual product

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal at dev.epicgames.com/portal.

  2. In the left navigation under Your Products, select the name of the product you want to configure.

    • Note: If you can’t see the product you want, make sure you are working in the correct organization.

  3. In the left navigation, click Kids Web Services.

  4. In the left navigation, click Parent Verification.

Step 2 — Choose Verification Methods

On the Parent Verification page, click the Verification Methods tab.

Note: All verification methods are enabled by default, but you must click Publish to Test to complete this step even if you make no changes.
