IARC Ratings

Overview of IARC ratings on the Epic Games Store.

10 mins to read

The International Age Rating Coalition (IARC) provides a globally streamlined age classification process for digital products. This process helps ensure users are given consistent access to established and trusted ratings across digital storefronts and platforms.

IARC simplifies the process by which you can obtain age ratings for your product from different regions around the world. To obtain an IARC rating, you take the IARC questionnaire, which asks a single set of questions about the content and interactive elements of your product. IARC uses your answers to these questions to automatically determine the appropriate ratings for your product at no cost to you. IARC provides regional ratings from a number of participating rating authorities along with a generic rating for the rest of the world.

Regions Where IARC Ratings are Required

If you distribute products in the following regions, you must get IARC or region-specific ratings:

  • Russia
  • Brazil
  • Germany
  • Australia

If you distribute products in South Korea, you must get a GRAC (Game Rating and Administration Committee) rating. Documentation: Distribution in Korea.

If your product leverages blockchain or NFT technology, your product must have an IARC or region-specific rating, regardless of the product's distribution region. Documentation: Blockchain Technology Guidelines.

Configure IARC Ratings on the Epic Games Store

When configuring your product for the Epic Games Store, you must either:

  • Obtain a new IARC rating for your product.
  • Upload an existing IARC rating obtained for your product on another storefront.
  • Opt out of using IARC ratings.

We highly recommend you obtain ratings for your product to best reach potential users on the storefront. If you do not have ratings for your product, you can obtain an IARC rating through the Epic Games Store publishing tools. If you choose to opt out of IARC ratings during the initial setup, you can opt in at any time.

To add an existing IARC rating, opt in or out of IARC ratings, or obtain a new IARC rating, see the documentation: Manage Regions and Ratings > Configure Age Ratings.

IARC Ratings Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is IARC?

The International Age Rating Coalition (IARC) is a rating system administered by many of the world's game rating authorities that globally streamlines the age classification process for digitally-delivered games and software applications. IARC enables the consistent accessibility of trusted and locally relevant guidance to help today's digital consumers make informed decisions.

The participating rating authorities maintain a single, comprehensive IARC questionnaire that incorporates each rating system's unique criteria and generates localized ratings for their respective regions. All participating rating authorities monitor rating assignments to ensure accurate ratings, and the system enables the prompt correction of ratings when necessary. Use of IARC is limited to games and software applications that are exclusively available via digital distribution.

For more information on IARC and IARC ratings, go to the IARC homepage at globalratings.com.

Are ratings mandatory for products distributed on the Epic Games Store?

No, ratings are optional on the Epic Games Store in most scenarios. However, some regions have legal requirements for ratings, and products cannot be distributed in these regions without ratings from the appropriate authorities.

In addition, the Epic Games Store requires ratings for products that leverage blockchain or NFT technology in any capacity. Documentation: Blockchain Technology Guidelines.

To promote an optimal user experience, we strongly recommend you obtain ratings for your products. The Epic Games Store treats unrated products as having the highest-level maturity rating available in regions where they are distributed. In addition, access to your product's product home page might be restricted, even in regions that do not require ratings.

Documentation:

  • Ratings on the Epic Games Store > Unrated Products
  • Manage Regions and Ratings > Unrated Products

How much time does it take to obtain a rating from IARC?

If your product has minimal content pertinent to ratings, the IARC submission process can take less than 5 minutes. If your product has more content pertinent to ratings, the IARC questionnaire can take a little longer to complete, about 10 to 15 minutes.

In both cases, no additional materials are required from participating rating authorities, and you receive confirmation of your product's rating assignments as soon as you complete the questionnaire.

If I receive an IARC rating for the Epic Games Store, can I use it on other storefronts?

Yes, with some exceptions. In most cases, you can use your IARC certificate ID to transfer ratings you obtain through the IARC rating system to other storefronts that participate in IARC, assuming no pertinent content or features have been added or removed from the game.

To get your IARC certificate ID from the Epic Games Store, see the documentation: Manage Regions and Ratings > Access IARC Certificate ID for Ratings Portability.

For the current list of storefronts that participate in IARC, see the IARC storefronts page: globalratings.com/storefronts.

If you distribute your product in South Korea, you can only use the GRAC rating you received through the Epic Games Store under certain conditions. See the Ratings Portability in South Korea section below for more information.

Ratings Portability in South Korea

If you distribute your product in South Korea and you want to use a GRAC rating from the Epic Games Store on another storefront, confirm whether or not the other storefront participates in IARC. See the IARC storefronts page at globalratings.com/storefronts for a list of IARC-participating storefronts.

  • If the other storefront participates in IARC, you cannot use the GRAC rating you received on the Epic Games Store on that storefront. You must obtain a GRAC rating from the other storefront, or contact GRAC directly to receive a rating for your product.
  • If the other storefront does not participate in IARC, contact GRAC to reuse the GRAC rating you received on the Epic Games Store on the other storefront.

Documentation: Distribution in Korea.

What if my product is not a game?

When you take the IARC questionnaire, the questionnaire asks you to state your product's application type. If you select any application type other than “Game”, then the Epic Games Store displays an IARC generic rating instead of agency-specific ratings.

The following regions display IARC generic ratings for non-game applications.

  • Australia
  • South Korea
  • Taiwan

For more information on IARC generic ratings and ratings definitions, see the IARC homepage at globalratings.com/ratings-definitions/.

My product displays a TRAD Rating Pending rating in the Developer Portal. What should I do?

If you see a Trad Rating Pending rating for your product in the Regions and Ratings > Ratings section of the Developer Portal, your product has been refused classification by a regional ratings authority. This is also known as an RC (Refused Classification) rating.

RC Rating for South Korea

The Game Rating and Administration Committee (GRAC) of South Korea can refuse classification for a product distributed in South Korea. To distribute in South Korea after you receive an RC rating, you must follow the GRAC application process for mature products. Documentation: Distribution in Korea > Mature Products (Age 19+).

RC Rating for the Rest of the World

For IARC distribution regions other than South Korea, an RC rating affects your product's distribution in the following ways:

  • If your product is already released, you can no longer distribute your product in the regions where you received the RC rating.
  • If your product has not yet been released, your product is automatically blocked from distribution in the regions where you received the RC rating.

To resolve an RC rating in regions other than South Korea, reach out to the region's rating authority for more information. You might be asked to provide an IARC certification ID. To access your IARC certification ID, see the documentation: Manage Regions and Ratings > Access IARC Certification for Ratings Portability.

How much does it cost to use IARC?

You can obtain IARC ratings in the Epic Games Store at no cost. This is possible due to the Epic Games Store's license from IARC for this service.

What happens if questions are answered incorrectly, whether inadvertently or deliberately, and my product is given a rating that does not accurately represent its content?

The IARC rating system includes a process in which participating rating authorities monitor ratings to ensure accuracy. When necessary, this system also facilitates the prompt correction of ratings on the Epic Games Store. If an IARC-assigned rating is corrected for your product, you receive an email notification about the update, and your new rating information is automatically updated on the storefront.

Documentation: Manage Regions and Ratings > Automatic IARC Rating Updates.

How do I know if an IARC-assigned rating has been changed?

IARC notifies you of any rating changes through the contact email you provide when you take the IARC questionnaire. Additionally, the rating displayed on your product home page in the storefront is automatically updated when your IARC rating changes.

Documentation: Manage Regions and Ratings > Automatic IARC Rating Updates

What can I do if I disagree with my product's assigned rating?

Before finalizing your IARC rating, you have an opportunity to review the ratings assigned to your product after you complete the IARC questionnaire. If you have concerns with a pending rating, you can click Back on the rating overview page to review your answers and correct any potential errors on the questionnaire.

You can also submit a Rating Check Request if you have concerns about your finalized rating. To do this:

  1. Locate the Rating Check Request link in the official rating certificate email you received when you released your product.
  2. Open the link to access the Rating Check Request form.
  3. Select the contested rating.
  4. Provide a brief explanation of why you believe the rating should be changed.
  5. Submit the form.

In response to the Rating Check Request, IARC might contact you to ask for supporting evidence, such as video clips or a build of your product. For more information on adjusting your assigned rating, see the FAQ at the IARC homepage: globalratings.com/faq.

What if my product has already been rated by one of the participating rating authorities through traditional (non-IARC) means?

If you obtained a qualifying traditional rating for your product (for example, an ESRB or PEGI rating), you can upload your traditional rating using the Developer Portal. If your product has received any traditional ratings, you must upload this information regardless of whether you choose to leverage IARC ratings.

If your product has a traditional rating pending, you cannot distribute your product on the Epic Games Store until you receive and upload that rating.

Documentation: Manage Regions and Ratings > Manage Traditional Ratings

If you have additional questions, contact IARC for assistance at globalratings.com/developer-contactus.

What if my product has Epic-managed distribution?

If your product has Epic-managed distribution, you cannot take the IARC questionnaire to obtain a rating. Instead, you must use rating information obtained through a traditional, long-form rating process. Contact your Release Manager to get help with this process.

More information:

  • Epic-managed and self-managed distribution

What is the impact and benefit that IARC can have for partners distributing products in multiple regions?

The IARC rating system streamlines the process of obtaining age and content ratings for your product through one questionnaire. The questionnaire is based on the factors that each participating rating authority considers when assigning ratings. When you complete the questionnaire, you instantly receive appropriate age ratings for each region.

This enables you to have your product simultaneously rated for several regions throughout the world and eliminates the cost of rating fees. This is a key reason why the Epic Games Store supports this initiative. IARC makes developing and releasing products more efficient and more affordable worldwide.

I've started or completed the IARC questionnaire but later realized I already have an existing certificate. How can I input my certificate?

If you have already started the IARC questionnaire but haven't completed it, first complete the questionnaire. Documentation: Manage Regions and Ratings > Obtain a New IARC Rating.

If you have already completed the IARC questionnaire, you can upload an existing IARC certificate in the Developer Portal at any time. Documentation: Manage Regions and Ratings > Add an Existing IARC Rating.

Can I use a combination of IARC ratings and traditional ratings?

Yes, you can combine IARC ratings and traditional ratings. After you take the IARC questionnaire, you can modify a specific traditional (also known as regional or long-form) rating from the Regions and Ratings page in the Developer Portal.

Documentation: Manage Regions and Ratings > Manage Traditional Ratings.

When should I retake the IARC questionnaire?

You must retake the IARC questionnaire if any of the following apply to you:

  • If you feel you've made a mistake. For example, if you entered incorrect product information, you should retake the questionnaire.
  • If you've made changes to your build that would require a ratings change.
  • If you've created two different products that use the same IARC certificate ID, you must take the IARC questionnaire once for each product. Each product with a unique product ID needs its own IARC certification.

To retake the questionnaire, see the documentation: Manage Regions and Ratings > Retake the IARC Questionnaire.
