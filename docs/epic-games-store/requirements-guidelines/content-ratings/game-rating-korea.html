Distribution in Korea

Requirements for distributing products in South Korea.

7 mins to read

This page outlines the following information:

  • Game Industry Promotion Act: Certain products containing randomization have requirements mandated by the Korean government.
  • Games-as-a-Service (GaaS): There are additional requirements required by GRAC (Game Rating and Administration Committee) for GaaS products.
  • GRAC Ratings: GRAC (Game Rating and Administration Committee) ratings are legally required to distribute products in South Korea for any playable builds that are available to the public, including demos and early access builds. To assist with this process, the Epic Games Store has a dedicated team available to help you obtain the necessary ratings for distribution.

Game Industry Promotion Act

South Korea's Game Industry Promotion Act requires any product that includes an element of randomization purchased during gameplay to make disclosures related to these elements. For example, this includes a random loot box or random item enhancement mechanic.

You can find more specific information on how and what information needs to be disclosed on the government website: www.mcst.go.kr.

Note: Regulators might contact Epic Games Store to seek assistance in removing your product from the store if it is not in compliance.

Games-as-a-Service

As a part of anti-addiction measures for PC and console games in Korea, the Korean government enforces a set of requirements for games-as-a-service (GaaS). A GaaS product discloses game content and messages through the internet.

The requirements are as follows:

  • Implement Real ID verification
  • Display the anti-addiction message in-game
  • Display the GRAC rating in-game
  • Implement a parent controls system
  • Implement a spending limit

If you need assistance or more detailed information on these requirements, please contact GRAC for support. You can also contact the Epic Games Store team by creating a private discussion using this support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com/~/private-question-page.

GRAC Ratings Requirements

To distribute products on the Epic Games Store in South Korea, you must meet the following requirements:

  • The Korea GRAC and Juvenile Protection Act strongly regulates game content to present legitimate rating marks. Game ratings in Korea are mandatory by law.
  • GRAC ratings are required for all playable builds available to the public, including demo, beta, and test builds. However, a GRAC rating is not required during a pre-purchase period that does not include a playable build.
  • For products distributed on the Epic Games Store, GRAC supports ratings granted by the International Age Rating Coalition (IARC) (www.globalratings.com). However, for mature products (age 19+ years), you must acquire GRAC ratings from GRAC directly. For details of how to obtain your rating, see the Mature Products (Age 19+) section of this page.
    • Tip: To learn more about the distinction between non-mature and mature products, see the Game Industry Promotion Act (GRAC) guidelines: grac.or.kr/~/ageratingsymbol.aspx.
  • If you use Epic-managed distribution for your product, the Epic Games Store team might be able to help you get a self-rated GRAC rating. For support to do this, create a private discussion on the support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com/~/private-question-page.

Refused Classification (RC) Ratings

The Game Rating and Administration Committee (GRAC) of South Korea can refuse classification for a product distributed in South Korea. If this happens to your product, your product gets a “Refused Classification” (RC) rating. In the Developer Portal under Regions and Ratings > Ratings, this displays as TRAD Rating Pending.

To distribute in South Korea after receiving an RC rating, you must follow the GRAC application process for mature products. See the Mature Products (Age 19+) section of this page for the mature product application process.

Add South Korea to Your Distribution Regions

Use the Regions and Ratings page of the Developer Portal to add South Korea to your product's distribution regions. Documentation: Manage Regions and Ratings > Configure Regions.

Before you can distribute your product in South Korea, you must take or retake the IARC questionnaire to receive a GRAC rating, then submit your product for review. To do this:

  1. Take or retake the IARC questionnaire.
    • If you have never taken the IARC questionnaire for this product, you must complete the questionnaire to receive a GRAC rating. Documentation: Manage Regions and Ratings > Obtain a New IARC Rating.
    • If you have already taken the IARC questionnaire for this product but want to add South Korea to your product's distribution regions, you must retake the IARC questionnaire to receive a GRAC rating. Documentation: Manage Regions and Ratings > Retake the IARC Questionnaire.
  2. Go to the Regions and Ratings page of the Developer Portal. To do this:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. Select the correct organization for your product.
    • c. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product.
    • d. In the left navigation, click Epic Games Store > Store Settings.
    • e. On the Store Settings page, click Regions and ratings.
  3. Complete any missing information in the Required Information section.
  4. Click Regions.
  5. Select South Korea from the list of regions.
  6. Click Save Changes.
  7. When you are ready, submit your product for review. Documentation: Submit for Final Review.

Mature Products (Age 19+)

For mature products, you must apply directly through the GRAC website to obtain your rating. The application process for mature products is outlined below.

As you prepare for launch, ensure you allocate enough lead time to acquire a GRAC rating prior to your product's available date. As a general guideline, mature (age 19+) products require at least six weeks.

Rating Application Process for Mature Products

To apply for a mature product classification, follow the steps below. If you need assistance at any time, contact GRAC support. Support is available in English.

  1. Learn more about the GRAC rating process.
  2. Create an account on the GRAC website.
  3. Sign in and apply for your mature product GRAC rating.
  4. Once you receive your classification, upload the GRAC rating to your product in the Dev Portal. Once uploaded, you must submit your product for review for final approval from the Epic Games Store team.
    • Tip: As ratings are required to distribute products in South Korea, the region is unchecked by default in the Dev Portal. Before submitting your product for review, ensure you select South Korea as a distribution region after uploading your rating.

Timing

Mature products must have a rating in order to launch on the Epic Games Store. The Epic Games Korea team recommends you submit your application to the GRAC system at least six weeks in advance of your target available date to account for delays from GRAC.

Ratings for mature products are typically provided about four weeks after your application is submitted. However, this timing may vary and could exceed four weeks. The Epic Games Korea team recommends regularly monitoring the status of your application by periodically visiting the GRAC website.

Temporary GRAC Status

If you have not received a GRAC rating in time for your product’s viewable date, you can add a temporary status and upload the official rating once available.

A product's viewable date determines when it becomes visible to users on the storefront; it does not control when the product becomes available for purchase. To learn more, review the Dates section of Manage Offers.

If you have not yet configured any ratings for your product, you must complete the initial setup process before you can follow the steps below.

To set a temporary GRAC status for your product in the Developer Portal, follow the steps below:

  1. Go to your product's Developer Portal page:
    • a. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    • b. In the left navigation, under Your products, click the name of your product. (Note: You might have to click Back to organization before you can do this.)
  2. When you are on your product's Developer Portal page, in the left navigation, click Epic Games Store.
  3. In the left navigation, click Store Settings.
  4. Click Regions and Ratings. Then choose Ratings.
  5. From the list on the right, select GRAC - Game Rating and Administration Committee.
  6. In the Rating Category section, select Rating Pending. This selection allows you to save the rating without uploading a certificate.
  7. Click Save Changes.

Traditional GRAC Certificates

If you want to use traditional GRAC certificates for your products, the following considerations apply:

  • If your product has a GRAC certificate for any platform supported by the Epic Games Store, you must display that information on your product home page, including the content descriptors. For more information on GRAC content descriptors, see GRAC Education Portal > Content Descriptors.
  • If you have a GRAC certificate for a platform not supported by the Epic Games Store, you can use that certificate on the Epic Games Store. However, you can only do this, if both of the following conditions apply:
    • Condition 1: The product content must be the same on the platform for which you have the certificate and the platforms you have on the Epic Games Store.
      • For example, if you have a GRAC certificate for a Nintendo Switch product, you can use this certificate for a Windows product on the Epic Games Store if the content is the same for the Switch and Windows versions. Note: You must also follow condition 2, described below.
    • Condition 2: You must submit a patch update to GRAC. In this patch update, you must inform GRAC that the product supports the platform or platforms that you have on the Epic Games Store. (Documentation: Patch Notes.)
      • For example, if you have a GRAC certificate for a Nintendo Switch product, you can use this certificate for a Windows product on the Epic Games Store if you inform GRAC that your product also supports Windows. Note: You must also follow condition 1, described above.

Note: Be sure you have submitted your patch notes to GRAC. Regulators might instruct the Epic Games Store to remove your product from the store if it is not in compliance.
