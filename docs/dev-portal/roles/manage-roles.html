Manage Roles (Developer Portal Access Permissions)

How to assign and define roles in the Developer Portal.

2 mins to read

Use roles to control access to Developer Portal functionality for members of your organization. Every member of your organization has an assigned role. Every role has a set of access rights that give permission to the members with that role.

For information on how to manage the members of your organization, see Organization Management > Members.

If you have the Owner or Admin role, you can assign the following roles to any member of your organization:

  • One of the predefined roles in the Developer Portal.
    • For more information, see Available Roles in the Developer Portal.
  • A custom role. You can define custom roles in the Developer Portal.
    • For more information, see Create a Custom Role on this page.

For information on how to assign a role, see Assign a Role to a Member of Your Organization on this page.

Roles give permissions for all your products, or for a specific set of products
When you assign a role, you can apply it for all your products, or for a specific selection of your products. Example: You assign the Publishing Tools role to a member of your organization for two of your products. This member can use the functionality granted by the Publishing Tools role, but only on these two products. The member cannot carry out actions on your other products.

Assign a Role to a Member of Your Organization

To assign a role to a member of your organization:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. Select your organization.
  3. Further down the left navigation (underneath Dashboard), click Organization.
  4. In the list of members, find the member whose role you want to assign.
  5. On the same row as the member, click the three dots (...).
  6. Select Change access rights.
  7. In the Role list, select the role you want to assign to this member.
    • For information on the roles you can assign, see Available Roles in the Developer Portal.
  8. In Products, select the products you want the role to apply to for this member.
    • To apply the role to all your products for this member, select All products.
    • For the Admin role, this is not available, because the Admin role always applies to all products.
  9. Click Save changes.

Create a Custom Role

You can define custom roles for your organization, with specific sets of access rights. This is useful if you need a set of access rights that are not covered by the Developer Portal's predefined roles (see Available Roles in the Developer Portal). You can then assign these custom roles to members of your organization.

To create a custom role:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. Select your organization.
  3. Further down the left navigation (underneath Dashboard), click Organization.
  4. Click the Roles tab.
  5. Click Create custom role.
  6. In Role name, enter a short name to describe the role.
  7. In Role description, enter a description to help your organization understand the purpose of the role.
  8. Select the access rights that you want to give this role.
    • For detailed information on access rights, see Available Roles in the Developer Portal > Access Rights.
  9. Click Create.
    • You can now assign this role to members of your organization. For more information, see Assign a Role to a Member of Your Organization on this page.
