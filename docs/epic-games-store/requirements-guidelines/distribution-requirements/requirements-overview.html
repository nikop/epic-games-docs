Epic Games Store Requirements

Requirements for distributing products on the Epic Games Store.

8 mins to read

Mobile Early Adopter: If you are in the Mobile Early Adopter program, go to the Mobile Early Adopter—Requirements and Guidelines page for guidance before you use this page.

To promote a transparent, consistent, and trustworthy store experience for our users, the Epic Games Store maintains a set of requirements that products must adhere to in order to be distributed on the storefront.

Review the provided requirements and guidelines to help ensure a successful launch of your product on the Epic Games Store. When you submit your product for review, the Epic Games Store team will confirm you have complied with these requirements.

In addition to the distribution requirements listed below, the Epic Games Store also maintains separate content and rating guidelines. To learn more, review the following resources:

  • Ratings on the Epic Games Store
  • Content Guidelines

Access Requirements

In order to use the Epic Games Store publishing tools in the Dev Portal, you must be at least 18 years of age. Additionally, you must be at least 18 to complete the required agreements. When attempting to access the publishing tools, you are prompted to confirm that you meet this requirement. If you do not provide confirmation, or otherwise do not meet this requirement, you cannot access the publishing tools.

Patch Notes

  • When you publish your product for the first time, you can add patch notes if you choose.
  • Patch notes are optional for products that have not yet been published.
  • Patch notes are required for major updates. Minor updates and bugfixes do not require patch notes.
  • If your product is distributed in South Korea, you must add patch notes to each update. To comply with Game Rating and Administration Committee (GRAC) requirements for release in South Korea, the Epic Games Store sends patch notes to GRAC.
    • For more information on GRAC requirements, see Distribution in Korea.

Patch Note Requirements

Patch notes must meet the following requirements:

  • Be in English.
  • Follow the Epic Games Store content requirements.

Documentation:

  • Content Requirements

Each set of patch notes must contain the following:

  • Version name or number: For example "v.1.1". You can enter up to 250 characters.
    • Note: Each name and number must be unique and cannot be reused from a previous patch on this product.
  • Description: Describe the changes in this patched version of your product. You can enter up to 2,000 characters, and you can apply rich text formatting, including bold, italic, and bulleted lists.
    • Note: Each description must be unique and cannot be reused from a previous patch on this product.

Technical Requirements

In order to distribute your product on the Epic Games Store, you must adhere to the following requirements and guidelines:

Cross-Platform Multiplayer for PC

Mobile Early Adopter: If you are in the Mobile Early Adopter program, go to the Mobile Early Adopter—Requirements and Guidelines—Cross-Platform Multiplayer documentation for guidance.

Products with online multiplayer functionality must support crossplay across all PC storefronts where the product is distributed. This ensures that players who purchase a multiplayer product on the Epic Games Store can easily connect with other players, regardless of where the product was purchased. You may use your preferred solution for crossplay, such as the free Epic One Services Crossplay functionality, your own method, or any third-party system that works across PC storefronts.

Crossplay is not mandatory for mobile (iOS, Android) products.

More information: Cross-Platform Multiplayer Guidelines

Early Access Requirements

Mobile Early Adopter: If you are in the Mobile Early Adopter program, go to the Mobile Early Adopter—Requirements and Guidelines—Early Access Requirements documentation for guidance.

The Epic Games Store welcomes developers to release their products for Early Access. This allows you to make your product available to users while it is still in development.

Some studios leverage Early Access to collect and incorporate user feedback, evolving their product by focusing on the features and functionality that most interest their users. While it is up to you to define what Early Access means for your product, releasing for Early Access can offer an iterative approach to building, testing, improving, and ultimately launching a complete, high-quality game to users.

More information: Early Access Requirements

Blockchain Technology

Mobile Early Adopter: If you are in the Mobile Early Adopter program, go to the Mobile Early Adopter—Requirements and Guidelines—Blockchain, NFT, or Cryptocurrency Technology for guidance.

Products that support blockchain, NFT, or cryptocurrency may be distributed on the Epic Games Store as long as these products adhere to specific policies regarding blockchain technology. If a product leverages blockchain technology in any capacity, these guidelines and requirements must be followed or the product cannot be published to the storefront.

More information: Blockchain Technology Guidelines

Promotional Content

You must disclose if your offer contains any paid advertisements that promote your own (or someone else's) brand, product, or business. Promotional content includes product placement, logos, slogans, and other content that promotes or advertises your own or someone else’s real-world brand. To disclose promotional content, follow these steps:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal at dev.epicgames.com/portal.
  2. On the home page, from the left-hand side of the page, select your game's product name under PRODUCTS.
  3. Select Epic Games Store > Offers > General Details > Promotional Content.
  4. Select Yes.

If you make this declaration, then the Epic Games Store labels the offer as promotional content.

More information: Manage Offers

Epic Games Store Achievements

Mobile Early Adopter: If you are in the Mobile Early Adopter program, go to the Mobile Early Adopter—Requirements and Guidelines—Epic Games Store Achievements documentation for guidance.

If a product supports achievements through other PC storefronts that sell third-party products, that product must also support Epic Games Store achievements. The achievements available to users on the Epic Games Store must be substantially similar to those offered through other storefronts.

  • Epic Games Store achievements are not mandatory for mobile (iOS, Android) products.

  • Web-based and HTML products are currently exempt from this requirement as they are not currently supported by the Epic Online Services (EOS) SDK.
  • If a product in Early Access does not yet have a finalized set of base game achievements defined, you only need to implement Epic Online Services achievements at the time of early release. Once ready for full release, Epic Games Store achievements must be enabled.

More information: Epic Games Store Achievements

This requirement does not retroactively apply to any existing products available on the Epic Games Store or any products that were onboarded in the Dev Portal prior to March 9, 2023. However, enabling achievements is recommended to promote an optimal user experience.

Additional Distribution Requirements

Pre-Purchase

Mobile Early Adopter: If you are in the Mobile Early Adopter program, go to the Mobile Early Adopter—Requirements and Guidelines—Pre-Purchase documentation for guidance.

The Epic Games Store supports running a pre-purchase for products. Users can pre-order a product by making a purchase even though it has not yet been released. Review the best practices and guidelines to help ensure a successful pre-purchase launch of your product on the Epic Games Store.

More information: Pre-Purchase Guidelines

In-App Purchases

Mobile Early Adopter: If you are in the Mobile Early Adopter program, go to the Mobile Early Adopter—Requirements and Guidelines—In-App Purchases documentation for guidance.

In-app purchases (IAPs) are purchase flows in your product. This includes storefronts, marketplaces, or any other for-sale content that prompts users to make a purchase. This also includes links to purchase your product on the Epic Games Store in the product or items for sale.

If your product contains any in-app purchase functionality:

  • If the product is on PC (Windows, macOS), you must clearly share the following questions (and answers) with users in your product’s FAQ page. This helps foster a transparent and trustworthy store experience for Epic Games Store users.
  • If the product does not support PC (Windows, macOS)—in other words, it is Mobile (iOS, Android) only— you don’t need to include a FAQ.

FAQ questions

  • Are there items (such as additional content, offers or currency) for sale that are used in the gameplay of your product?
  • Can your product be used without making any in-app purchases?
  • Are in-app purchases refundable?
    • Note: If refunds are available, you must include a link to your refund policy.
  • What payment platform is used to facilitate these purchases? Do you use Epic Games’ platform or a 3rd-party (including your own) payment system?
    • Notes:
      • Epic Games maintains no responsibility for providing payment details or facilitating refunds for any transactions made outside of our payment platform.
      • This question is not required for blockchain technology. Products leveraging blockchain technology cannot use the Epic Games Store payment mechanism for any offers, including base game offers and associated non-blockchain-related transactions.
        More information: Blockchain Technology Guidelines

Note: FAQ pages are not mandatory for mobile (iOS, Android) products on the Epic Games Store.

More information: Create an FAQ Page

Pre-Purchase Guidelines

Mobile Early Adopter: If you are in the Mobile Early Adopter program, go to the Mobile Early Adopter—Requirements and Guidelines—Pre-Purchase documentation for guidance.

You can request pre-purchase sales for your product. This means that users can pre-purchase your product even though you have not yet released it. To help ensure a successful pre-purchase release of your product on the Epic Games Store, we provide guidelines and best practices regarding how to set up pre-purchase, ratings for pre-purchase products, policies for pre-purchase delays, and more.

More information: Pre-Purchase Guidelines

Consent to Additional Terms

If you require users to consent to additional terms (for example, an End User License Agreement (EULA) or Terms of Service) before using your product, you must link to the additional terms from your product home page.

You cannot configure additional in-product terms in the Developer Portal. If your product has in-product terms, you must do the following:

  • Configure your product to manage the in-product terms.
  • Host the text of the terms in a publicly-accessible location.
  • Link to the text of the terms from your product home page.

More information: Configure Store Settings > Manage Product Configuration > Product Details

Epic Online Services Recommendations

Mobile Early Adopter: If you are in the Mobile Early Adopter program, go to the Mobile Early Adopter—Requirements and Guidelines—Epic Online Services (EOS) Recommendations documentation for guidance.

Testing Guide

For developers leveraging Epic Online Services in combination with the Epic Games Store, a testing guide is available to help you successfully implement these services. This guide provides test cases and recommendations for topics such as the multiplayer crossplay requirement, Epic Account Services, offline mode support, and player experience recommendations. We strongly encourage you to review this guide to help ensure a successful launch.

More information: Epic Games Store Testing Guide

Ecom Interface Recommendations

The Ecom Interface allows developers using Epic Online Services to support in-game purchases through the Epic Games Store. With this interface, you can manage products ranging from full games and downloadable content (DLC) to virtual goods and in-game currency. This includes making offers, completing purchase transactions, verifying ownership, and redeeming purchased items. If you choose to leverage the Ecom Interface, we recommend implementing ownership verification checks for your game and any DLC. Online ownership verification on a trusted server/API is strongly recommended as it is the most effective way to prevent piracy.

To learn more about the Ecom Interface and this recommendation, review the following resources:

  • Ecom Interface
  • Verifying Ownership
