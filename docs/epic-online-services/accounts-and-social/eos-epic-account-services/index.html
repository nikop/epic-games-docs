EOS Epic Account Services

EOS Epic Account Services provide player accounts that enable your game's players to tap into the Epic Games account ecosystem of nearly a billion players across multiple platforms and stores and gain access to the Accounts and Social EOS services and the Epic Games Store Ecommerce (Ecom) service.
