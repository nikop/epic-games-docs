Complete Trader Verification

Register your organization as a trader to sell your products on the Epic Games Store.

10 mins to read

Before you can sell products on the Epic Games Store, you must complete the trader verification process for your organization. The trader verification process involves the following steps:

  1. Set your trader status.
  2. Certify your account information.
  3. Provide your general information.
  4. Verify your phone number.
  5. Verify your identity.
  6. (Optional) Complete secondary address verification.

Prerequisites

To complete the trader verification process, you need the following materials:

  • An email address that is registered to an Epic Games Store account.
  • ID verification documents. See the Accepted Trader Verification Documents section of this page for a list of acceptable ID verification documents.
  • A smartphone that has access to the Internet and can take photos.

Start Trader Verification

To begin the trader verification process, navigate to the Trader Details section of the Developer Portal. To do this:

  1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (https://dev.epicgames.com/portal).
  2. Select the correct organization for your product.
  3. In the Organization window, click the Trader Details tab. The Trader verification pane displays.

Set Your Trader Status

You cannot change your trader status after you click Start trader verification. If you need to change your trader status after you begin verification, create a private discussion on this support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com. Under Topic, select Epic Games Store.

In the Trader verification pane, you can set your trader status to one of the following options:

  • Trader: A trader is a person or company that uses Epic Games Store publishing tools for their trade, business, craft, or profession.
  • Non-Trader: A non-trader uses Epic Games Store publishing tools for purposes outside their trade, business, craft, or profession.
  • Undeclared: You have not yet declared your trader status. Until you declare your trader status as either a Trader or Non-Trader, you can only distribute your products to players outside of the European Union (EU). This option is selected by default.

Note: Traders and non-traders must meet certain requirements to comply with EU law. If you don't know your trader status, see Trader Status: Understanding Marketplace Requirements.

To begin trader verification, you must set your trader status to Trader. To do this:

  1. Click Select in the Trader box near the bottom of the Trader verification pane.
  2. Click Start trader verification in the Trader box to begin trader verification.

Certify Your Account Information

First, certify that your public profile information is correct and provide an email address to use for trader verification. To do this:

  1. In the Trader verification window, review your public profile information and confirm it is correct.
  2. If all of your information is correct, select the certification checkbox at the bottom of the window.
  3. Click Continue.
  4. In Email address, enter your email address. The email address you provide must meet the following requirements:
    • The email address must be registered to an Epic Games Store account.
    • The email address must be unique to this trader account. You cannot use the same email address for multiple trader accounts. For example, you cannot use the same email address that you use for a FAB trader account.
  5. Click Continue.

Provide General Information

Next, fill out the form to provide general information for your trader profile and provide supporting documentation. To do this:

  1. Gather the documents you need to verify your information. See the Accepted Trader Verification Documents section of this page for a list of documents you can use to verify your information.
  2. In the General Information section, click + Add General Information. The General Information window displays.
  3. In the Account Type field, select an account type.
    • Individual: You are registering as an individual.
    • Registered Business: You are registering as a business entity.
  4. Fill out the fields for the account type you selected.
    • Individual:
      1. First Name
      2. Last Name
      3. (Optional) Date of Birth
    • Registered business:
      1. Registered Business Name
      2. Business Registration Number.
        • See the Registered Business Documents section of this page for more information on acceptable business registration number formats.
      3. (Optional) First Name
      4. (Optional) Last Name
  5. In the Email field, enter your email address.
    • The email address you enter in the Email field must match the email address you used at the start of the trader verification process. If you use a different email address in this field, you must verify the new email address later in the trader verification process. See the Certify Your Account Information section of this page for more information.
  6. In the Phone field, select your country from the dropdown menu, then enter your phone number.
    • Note: You must verify this phone number later in the trader verification process. Make sure you can receive either SMS messages or phone calls at this number.
  7. Enter your street address, city, state, and ZIP code (if applicable).
  8. Click Save.
    • If you entered a phone number, the Verify your phone number window displays. Proceed to the Verify Your Phone Number section on this page.
    • If you did not enter a phone number, proceed to the Verify Your Identity section on this page.

Verify Your Phone Number

If you entered a phone number in the Provide General Information section on this page, you must verify your phone number to continue. To do this:

  1. In the Verify your phone number window, click one of the following options:
    • Verify by SMS: You will receive an SMS text message containing the one-time verification code.
    • Verify by call: You will receive an automated phone call with a one-time verification code.
  2. Confirm the listed phone number is correct.
  3. Click Send code.
  4. After receiving the one-time verification code, enter the verification code in the Code field.
  5. Click Verify code. If your number is successfully verified, the Trader Verification window displays. The General Information section populates with the information you entered.

Verify Your Identity

Next, you must verify your identity by uploading photos of your verification documentation and your face using your smartphone.

A smartphone is required to verify your identity. For more information on the materials required to complete this process, see the Prerequisites section of this page.

  1. In the Trader Verification window, click Complete Verification. The Veriff window displays.
  2. Open the mobile verification link on your smartphone in one of two ways:
    • Scan the provided QR code with your smartphone.
    • Receive an SMS message with the link.
  3. When the link successfully connects your smartphone to the verification servers, the Veriff window displays a "Connected to your mobile" message to confirm the connection. Keep the Veriff window open on your computer during the verification process.
  4. Follow the instructions on your smartphone. The instructions are different depending on the type of verification documents you use, but in general, you must:
    • Take photos of your ID verification documents using your smartphone and submit them.
    • Take a photo of your face using your smartphone and submit it. The photo of your face must match the photo on your ID verification documents.
  5. After submitting all required photos, a confirmation message displays on your smartphone and computer. On your computer, click Continue. The Veriff window closes, and the Trader Information window displays.

Verification Statuses

The verification you submitted displays under the Verifications section with its current status. Your verification status can be one of the following options:

  • Pending: Your verification is currently under review. The review process typically takes less than 24 hours. You will receive verification status updates to the email address you provided during this period.
  • Retry: An issue occurred, and you must resubmit your ID verification. To do this:
    1. Sign in to the Developer Portal (dev.epicgames.com/portal).
    2. Select the correct organization for your product.
    3. In the Organization window, click the Trader Details tab. The Trader verification window displays.
    4. Click Continue Verification to resubmit your ID verification. See the Verify Your Identity section of this page for instructions.
  • Failed: Your ID verification failed. You will receive an email explaining the reasons your ID verification failed. If you receive this status, create a private discussion at this support website: eoshelp.epicgames.com. Under Topic, select Epic Games Store.
  • Verified: You have completed ID verification. You can proceed to the next step in the financial onboarding process: the tax interview. See the Next Steps section of this page for more information.

(Optional) Secondary Address Verification

If your address was not verified based on the documents you submitted in the ID verification process, you might receive an email asking you to complete secondary address verification. The email includes a link and instructions to upload a secondary document to verify your address.

Secondary address verification documents must be no more than 12 months old.

Use the link to upload at least one of the following documents to complete secondary address verification:

  • Utility bill (for example: a gas, electric, water, Internet, or phone bill).
  • Bank or credit card statement.
  • Official government tax document. See the Accepted Trader Verification Documents section for more information on accepted government tax documents.

Invalid Secondary Address Verification Documents

The following documents are not accepted as secondary address verification documents:

  • Invoices
  • Grocery, online shopping, or flower delivery orders
  • Leases
  • ID documents or passports
  • Self-published memos
  • Tax forms filled out by you or your business that have not received government certification (for example, a W2 or 1099 form in the United States).

Next Steps

After completing the trader verification process, you must complete a tax interview to set up your tax profile. See Complete the Tax Interview for detailed instructions on completing the tax interview.

Accepted Trader Verification Documents

To complete your trader verification, you must provide official documents. These documents must show specific information to qualify as acceptable documentation. The information required when registering as an individual is different from the information required when registering as a business.

Individual Documents

For individual trader verification, your documents must use the Arabic or Latin alphabet. We recommend you use a passport for individual trader verification because passports typically use Arabic or Latin characters, regardless of country of origin. If you cannot provide documents with Arabic or Latin characters, consider registering as a business or registering as a non-trader.

If you are attempting to complete trader verification as an individual, your documents must display the following information:

  • Name
  • Street address
  • City
  • Country

The following documents are the preferred trader verification documents for individuals:

  • (Recommended) Passport
  • ID card
  • Driver's license
  • Residence permit

Registered Business Documents

If you are attempting to complete trader verification as a registered business, your documents must display the following information:

  • Business name
  • City
  • Country
  • Business registration number
    • Note: Your business registration number may be in a different format or have a different name, depending on your businesss country of origin. Refer to the table below for accepted documents in your country.
